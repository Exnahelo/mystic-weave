# Mystic Weave — Next Steps

## Where the project is

- `10_world/` is locked setting input: do not redesign lore while building mechanics.
- The mechanical vision and design problem are locked in `20_mechanics/00_foundation/`.
- The cosmic frame is complete: Earth-like physical intuition, a 364-day calendar,
  twin co-orbital moons, and the Cataclysm as the origin of modern magic.
- No implementation work has begun. The current task is still foundational design.

## Next decision: minimal planet surface

Define only the wider-world information the mechanics need:

- where the Hollow Crown sits in relation to a few neighboring regions;
- broad access to trade, travel, migration, and external political pressure; and
- generic place types that let the system represent locations outside the Hollow Crown.

Do **not** fully build continents, cultures, or distant history. The goal is a
stable spatial contract, not exhaustive worldbuilding.

## Then, in order

- [ ] Define the spatial model: containment, location, routes, underground layers,
      and the Feywood's non-Euclidean pocket.
- [ ] Define time advancement and scheduled events.
- [ ] Define environmental state: weather, seasons, hazards, ambient magic, and
      resource availability.
- [ ] Define movement and travel.
- [ ] Revisit the development plan and decide the first entity-model question.

## Guardrails

- Treat later phases as provisional; do not design capability, resolution, magic,
  progression, or implementation early.
- Design from the vision and design problem, not from existing lore entities.
- When a decision is complete, capture it in its own `mw_mech_<topic>.md` note and
  link it from the mechanics map.
