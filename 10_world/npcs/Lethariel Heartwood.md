# Lethariel Heartwood

## Overview

Heartwarden Captain emeritus. Matriarch of House Heartwood. [[Discordant War]] commander. Lethariel is one of the few living witnesses to the [[Discordant War]]'s final days and one of the most weighted living presences in the [[Feywood]].

## Background

When word of the [[Mordraxian Rebels]]' move reached the [[Feywood]] and the Council of Elders deadlocked beneath the Heartwood on whether to invoke the [[Oath of the Fallen]], the Heartwood itself dropped a branch bearing a flower, addressed to her — then a young commander of House Heartwood. She performed the rare Pool ritual in the Council Chamber, placing the flower in the Pool's water and drinking, and the vision the Heartwood gave her overrode the Council's deadlock.

She led the elven contingent of roughly two hundred through the [[Hollow Crown]]'s western threshold and into the conflict. She fought alongside the [[Platinum Warden]] in the war's last battles. She was at the pit's edge of the [[Dark Hold]] when [[Vindrael]] gave his life and witnessed his sacrifice firsthand.

The bow she still carries was cut from the same branch the Heartwood had dropped to her.

## Role

Several centuries old, currently in residence at Heartwood Estate in the Outer Old-Growth Wild of the [[Feywood]], holding her Captaincy in emeritus standing while supporting the current House Heartwood Proving candidate through the preparation phase. She will resume the active Captaincy when the candidate departs for the Proving.

## Relationships

- [[Platinum Warden]] / [[Vindrael]] — fought alongside him in the war's final days; one of the few living witnesses to his sacrifice
- House Heartwood — matriarch
- [[Heartwarden]] — Captain emeritus
- [[Feywood]] — central figure in the realm's diplomatic and military memory of [[Drakenvale]] relations
- [[Draconic Council]] — through her position, holds the [[Feywood]]'s most weighted line of testimony into [[Drakenvale]]'s historical record

## Notable traits

Lethariel speaks rarely about the war. What she has said is canonical record. She is direct, weighted, and not given to ceremony — the kind of presence whose attention a visitor notices regardless of who else is in the room.

## Role in play

[[Discordant War]] legacy, [[Feywood]]-realm diplomatic continuity, House Heartwood matters, [[Heartwarden]] order business, the few living testimonies to [[Vindrael]]'s sacrifice.
