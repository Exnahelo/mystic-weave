# Trial of Wings

## Definition

The Trial of Wings is a ritualized duel overseen by the [[Draconic Council]], used to settle disputes of honor, grievance, or principle. An ancient draconic ritual once fought to the death, the Trial was preserved by the founders of [[Drakenvale]] after the [[War of the Fallen]] but reformed as non-lethal, and formalized into its current ceremonial structure during the [[Age of Harmony]].

It is rarely invoked but highly respected.

## Scope

Open to:
- [[Dragons]]
- [[Drakari]]

Mortals and kobolds may **request** the Trial, but most such matters are directed into mediation instead. The Trial is reserved for disputes whose resolution genuinely requires the structure of formal ritual combat — disputes of honor, grievance, or principle that mediation cannot adequately resolve.

## Mechanics in the world

The Trial is overseen by the [[Draconic Council]]. Its structure is ceremonial: the dispute is named, the parties accept the Trial, the form of combat is established within Council-approved bounds, and the outcome is binding under the [[Codex of Remembrance]].

Reformed as non-lethal during the [[Age of Harmony]], the Trial does not produce death by design. Severe injury can occur, and is part of the gravity that gives the Trial its weight, but the ritual structure is meant to resolve disputes without consuming the disputants.

The Trial occurs in the [[Draconic Hall]] under formal Council convening, or in designated ceremonial spaces appropriate to the parties' status.

## History

The pre-reform Trial of Wings was an ancient draconic ritual fought to the death — the form in which dragons resolved conflicts of honor in the ages before [[The Cataclysm]] and during the long draconic conflicts that followed. The founders of [[Drakenvale]] preserved the Trial deliberately, recognizing that some disputes among dragons could not be resolved through purely diplomatic means without the formal pressure of ritual combat.

Reform to non-lethal status during the [[Age of Harmony]] was not a softening of the ritual — it was a structural commitment. The founders had built a sanctuary that refused the kind of conflict the [[War of the Fallen]] had produced, and a death-Trial inside that sanctuary would have undermined the refusal. The non-lethal Trial preserves the gravity of formal combat while denying it the finality that earlier ages had accepted.

## Variants and disputes

There is no formal disagreement about the Trial's current structure, but quiet undercurrents persist. Some traditionalist dragons hold (privately) that the non-lethal reform was an error — that ritual combat without true stakes is insufficient pressure on the disputants. Most dragons in good standing reject this view, but the position is not entirely absent.
