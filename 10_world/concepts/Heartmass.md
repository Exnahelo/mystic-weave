# Heartmass

## Definition

The Heartmass is the **historical formation** at the center of the [[Hollow Crown]] from which the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] was carved. It is not a present-day location.

The Heartmass was a colossal pale formation of fused celestial stone and platinum-veined mountain heartrock — created when the meteor of [[The Cataclysm]] lodged in the broken core of the mountain and merged with the heartrock there. During [[The Founding]], the founders carved the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] from this mass. What remains today is the Stronghold; the Heartmass as a distinct formation no longer exists separately from it.

## Scope

The term is **historical/origin**. When the source materials describe "the Heartmass" or "carved from the Heartmass," they are referring to the original substrate — the formation as it existed before the carving. References in the present tense (e.g. "rises the Heartmass") in older source material are inherited descriptions of the underlying matter, not assertions that the Heartmass exists as a present-day landmark distinct from the Stronghold.

For all present-day purposes, what stands at the center of the [[Hollow Crown]] is the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]]. The [[Heartstone]] is the artifact discovered within the Heartmass during its earliest excavations.

## Origin

Formed during [[The Cataclysm]]. The meteor's largest fragment did not vanish on impact — it lodged within the shattered core of the mountain and, over time, fused with the platinum-veined heartrock there. The result was the Heartmass: a unified mass of celestial-and-mountain matter, pale white-and-silver, holding both the magical residue of the impact and the deep mineral character of the mountain it joined.

## Relationship to the Stronghold

During [[The Founding]], dragons, drakari followers, kobold laborers, and mortal artisans carved the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] from the Heartmass outward. The carving was not partial — the Heartmass became the Stronghold. There is no remaining Heartmass formation distinct from the Stronghold's living stone.

The Stronghold's distinctive luminous appearance — pale white-and-silver, fused celestial stone with platinum veins — is the Heartmass's character expressed in fortress form.

## Relationship to the Heartstone

The [[Heartstone]] was discovered within the Heartmass during the earliest excavations. The Heartstone is a discrete crystal artifact embedded within the celestial-platinum core; it is not the Heartmass itself, and it survives as a separable object within the [[Draconic Hall]] today. The Heartstone is the only "piece" of the Heartmass that exists today as a distinct entity outside the Stronghold's stone.
