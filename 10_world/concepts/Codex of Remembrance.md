# Codex of Remembrance

## Definition

The Codex of Remembrance is the governing legal and moral framework of [[Drakenvale]]. It emerged from the founding principles of the [[Oath of the Fallen]] and became the central legal-moral order of the realm during the [[Age of Harmony]].

## Core principles

- **Justice and Mercy** — fairness, proportionality, and opportunity for redemption
- **Balance and Unity** — harmony among alignments and peoples within the sanctuary
- **Responsibility and Restraint** — power wielded for the greater good
- **Sanctity of Sacred Sites** — the [[Platinum Heart]], [[Amethyst Vault]], and sealed [[Temple of Mordrax]] are inviolable
- **Secrecy of the Sanctuary** — disclosure of Drakenvale's location requires unanimous Council approval

## Mechanics in the world

The Codex is a living document, not a fixed canon. Amendments are proposed and debated at the [[Draconic Conclave]], a gathering of all resident dragons convened approximately every century or when major structural questions or external threats demand it. Its timing is not fixed — it is called by [[Draconic Council]] decree.

Legal violations are addressed through:

- Reparations
- Exile
- Community obligation
- Magical binding
- Atonement rituals
- Execution (rare; reserved for the gravest offenses, requires unanimous Council approval)

Capital cases are reserved for actions that place the sanctuary itself at existential risk — chiefly attempts to unseal the [[Temple of Mordrax]].

## Enforcement

The [[Draconic Council]] holds ultimate judicial authority. [[Drakari Stewards]] act as direct enforcers of Council rulings. The [[Wardens]] handle internal security and sacred-site protection. Conflict resolution occurs through mediation in the [[Amethyst Vault]], the [[Trial of Wings]], or structured community justice in the [[Draconic Hall]]. Severe cases may invoke divination, magical truth-binding, or direct Council judgment.

## History

The Codex emerged from the [[Oath of the Fallen]] sworn at the founding. After the [[Discordant War]], it was amended to more explicitly forbid actions that destabilized the realm's hard-won harmony, and redemption rituals were formalized.

## Connections

- [[Oath of the Fallen]] — its founding source
- [[Draconic Council]] — its supreme interpreter
- [[Draconic Conclave]] — the body that amends it
- [[Trial of Wings]] — one of its sanctioned ritual processes
- [[Discordant War]] — caused major amendments
