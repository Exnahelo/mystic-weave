# Oath of the Fallen

## Definition

The Oath of the Fallen is the founding compact of [[Drakenvale]] — the agreement entered into by dragons, drakari, and surviving mortal lineages at the close of the [[War of the Fallen]] to build a hidden sanctuary governed by justice, balance, restraint, and shared responsibility. Sworn at what would become the [[Platinum Heart]], it is the moral and political foundation from which the [[Codex of Remembrance]] later emerged.

## Scope

The Oath binds:

- The founding draconic coalition and their successors
- The [[Drakari]] who entered as kin to the founding dragons
- Surviving mortal lineages who joined the sanctuary
- The [[Elves]] of the [[Feywood]] as sovereign-equal signatories — not residents, but allies with their own polity
- The lasting custody of the [[Heartstone]], the wards of the [[Hollow Crown]], and the seal on the [[Temple of Mordrax]]

## Mechanics in the world

The Oath establishes the core compact: secrecy of the sanctuary, restraint of draconic power, mutual responsibility for the realm's survival, and the explicit refusal of the kind of conflict that ended the [[War of the Fallen]]. The sealing of the [[Temple of Mordrax]] under collective draconic warding was the founding compact's final act.

The Oath itself is not the [[Codex of Remembrance]]. The Codex is the legal-moral framework that emerged from the Oath's principles during the [[Age of Harmony]] and grew into a living document amendable by the [[Draconic Conclave]]. The Oath is the founding agreement; the Codex is its institutional expression.

### The mutual-aid clause

The most consequential single provision is the mutual-aid clause that binds [[Drakenvale]] and the [[Feywood]]. Each polity governs itself; each comes to the other's aid in true crisis. The clause has been honored exactly once in canonical history: the [[Feywood]]'s deployment to [[Drakenvale]] during the [[Discordant War]], led by [[Lethariel Heartwood]].

## Variants and disputes

The Oath is held with substantial reverence on both sides of the compact, but the two realms remember it differently. Drakenvale records the Oath as primarily its own founding agreement, with elven participation as a sovereign-equal signing. The Feywood holds the Oath as one of several ancient compacts the elves entered after [[The Cataclysm]], with [[Drakenvale]] as the most enduring partner.

The [[Vigil]] preserves a parallel inner oath, the **Oath of Scales**, sworn at the [[Platinum Heart]] at the same founding moment. The Oath of Scales is restricted knowledge — not public common knowledge in the [[Modern Era]] — and it binds the [[Vigil]] to defenses of the sanctuary in its gravest hours.
