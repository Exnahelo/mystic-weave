# Southern Dark Quadrant

The realm's wounded southeast. The southern dark quadrant holds the [[Rift of Discord]], the [[Shadowed Hollows]], the [[Platinum Oath Monastery]] on its defensible rampart shelf, the outlaw edge-settlement of [[Greymantle]], and the controlled corridors that connect them — the only sector of the [[Hollow Crown]] where the realm's ordinary civic life does not reach. **The corruption left by the [[Discordant War]] persists here**, attenuated across distance into ordinary terrain at the quadrant's outer edges and concentrated near the [[Rift of Discord|Rift]] and [[Shadowed Hollows|Hollows]] at its centers.

## What the quadrant is

The southern dark quadrant occupies the southeastern interior of the basin, bounded by the [[Inner Ramparts]] to the south and east, the broader grasslands and lake terrain to the north and west, and the rampart shelf where the [[Platinum Oath Monastery]] is built to the southeast. Within these bounds sit two principal sources of active corruption — **the [[Rift of Discord]]**, the wound where the [[Dark Hold]] once stood and where [[The Platinum Warden|Vindrael]] gave his life — and **the [[Shadowed Hollows]]**, the corrupted forest above and around the deep-buried [[Temple of Mordrax]]. Each source carries its own character of corruption: the Rift's is acute, physical-adjacent, marked by a low pulsing resonance; the Hollows' is diffuse, psychological, marked by a continuous sense of being watched.

The quadrant is **not uninhabited**. [[Greymantle]] sits on the western margin where the dark quadrant meets the broader basin's rougher terrain — an outlaw edge-settlement founded by Discordant War veterans and grown into a refuge for those the realm's standard institutions cannot accommodate. The [[Platinum Oath Monastery]] crowns a defensible rampart shelf to the southeast, where the surviving [[Platinum Accord]] (now operating as the **Vigil**) maintains continuous monitoring of the [[Rift of Discord|Rift]] and the [[Temple of Mordrax|Temple]] seal that lies far below. Together with the [[Vigil]]'s patrol corridors, these two communities are the quadrant's only sustained civic presences.

## Layered threshold geography

The quadrant's defining structural feature is **layered threshold geography**. The realm cannot simply seal off the dark quadrant — the corruption does not respect institutional declarations — but it can structure the space so that ordinary realm terrain remains reachable while the most dangerous interior zones are protected by perimeter corridors that absorb the corruption's outer effects.

The threshold corridors are:

- **[[Rift of Discord Edge]]** — the perimeter zone surrounding the [[Rift of Discord]]. Vigil-patrolled. The Rift's acute corruption attenuates across this zone before reaching ordinary terrain. Most travelers who reach the Edge feel the Rift before they see it; many turn back on their own, which is the Edge's intended effect.
- **[[Shadowed Hollows Approach]]** — the perimeter zone surrounding the [[Shadowed Hollows]]. Vigil-patrolled. The Hollows' diffuse psychological corruption attenuates across this zone. The Approach is quieter than the Rift Edge — less dramatic, less visibly institutional — but the corruption is present and the Vigil patrols it with the same professional seriousness.
- **[[Platinum Oath Approach]]** — the secured mountain corridor ascending from the dark quadrant's lower terrain to the [[Platinum Oath Monastery]]. Not a corruption-buffer but a fortified institutional access route. Walled stages, manned gatehouses, Vigil checkpoints. The journey itself is the filter; those who reach the summit have earned their arrival.

These corridors do not function as walls. They function as **graded transitions** — geography that makes the dangerous interior reachable to those with cause and reason while structurally discouraging casual approach.

## Civic presence

The quadrant has no civic government in the realm's ordinary sense. The Council does not maintain Administrative Quarter offices here. The Dragon Guard does not patrol. The realm's standard institutional reach effectively ends at the dark quadrant's outer boundary. Two institutions hold what civic presence exists:

- **The [[Vigil]] (the surviving [[Platinum Accord]])** — operating from the [[Platinum Oath Monastery]] as a Rift monitoring station, continuous patrol presence across the threshold corridors, working trade relationship with [[Greymantle]], formal arrangement with the Draconic Council that grants the order operational authority within its corridor while preserving the Council's plausible distance from the order's specific methods.
- **[[Greymantle]]** — self-governing under the Warden of Greymantle, a Discordant War veteran whose authority is reputational and historical rather than institutional. The Council tolerates Greymantle's outlaw character because the alternative is worse: the realm's misfits, war-broken, and quietly exiled need somewhere to go, and Greymantle absorbs them in ways the realm's civic settlements cannot.

The relationship between the two communities is professional and circumscribed. They trade. They maintain the road between them. They do not socialize. The Vigil has made clear, once, that problems with Greymantle's portion of the trade arrangement would not be tolerated; Greymantle has not required the point to be made twice.

## What lies beneath

The [[Temple of Mordrax]] lies far below the dark quadrant's surface, beneath the [[Shadowed Hollows]] and the [[Rift of Discord]]. The Temple is sealed — its founding wards reinforced by the Draconic Council in the immediate aftermath of the [[Discordant War]] after the [[Dark Hold]]'s collapse severed the Mordraxian Rebels' channeling shaft. The seal is the realm's deepest active institutional commitment. The Vigil maintains the Temple's outer monitoring; the Council maintains the warding work itself.

**The Temple is not approachable from the surface.** The [[Dark Hold]] excavation that stressed the seal was the closest the realm has ever come to a Temple breach, and that working reached only partway down before its collapse. What lies in the lower reaches of the bottomless pit at the [[Rift of Discord|Rift]]'s heart is not canonically documented. The Council maintains institutional silence on the matter. The Vigil maintains a different kind of silence, which is itself documentation of a sort.

## Travel through the quadrant

Travelers crossing the southern dark quadrant on legitimate business follow established routes. The **Dark Quadrant Corridor** is the realm's name for the Vigil-patrolled patrol-and-traffic system that connects [[Greymantle]], the [[Platinum Oath Monastery]] (via the [[Platinum Oath Approach]]), and the threshold corridors at the [[Rift of Discord Edge]] and [[Shadowed Hollows Approach]]. Access is controlled — the Vigil intercepts unauthorized travelers, redirects most casual approach, and engages with deliberate intruders according to their apparent purpose.

The corruption affects all travelers regardless of route. **Attenuation across distance is real;** travelers spending hours in [[Greymantle]] experience meaningfully less corruption than travelers spending the same hours at the [[Rift of Discord Edge|Rift Edge]], even though both are technically within the dark quadrant. Routes through the quadrant's outer reaches are correspondingly less hazardous than routes that approach the corruption sources directly.

## Character

The southern dark quadrant has a specific atmospheric character that distinguishes it from every other region in the realm. **It is not corrupted in the way the [[Mystic Wetlands]] are wet or the [[Volcanic Highlands]] are hot;** the corruption here is institutional, historical, and continuing — the lasting consequence of a war the realm fought a century ago and a deeper threat the realm has chosen to contain rather than eliminate.

Travelers crossing the quadrant carry the experience with them afterward. Even brief exposures produce the sensation of having been somewhere real — not quite remembered as pleasant, not quite remembered as frightening, but remembered as significant. **The quadrant does not advertise itself. It persists.**

The Vigil's continuous presence here is the realm's most concrete answer to the question of how a sovereign sanctuary lives next to its own deepest wound. The [[Platinum Accord|Accord]] watched the thing that killed their founder. They have not stopped watching. They will not stop watching for as long as the seal on the Temple holds and the [[Rift of Discord|Rift]]'s corruption persists.
