# Shadowed Hollows

The biome above and around the buried [[Temple of Mordrax]], where necrotic energy seeps upward through the rock. **The vegetation here is twisted and pale. Shadows move independently of light sources. Wraiths and shadow creatures are occasionally encountered.** The [[Sapphire Sentinels]] monitor this area covertly. The [[Wardens]] patrol the perimeter but do not enter without cause. **Cult activity has been detected here** — individuals drawn to [[Mordrax]]'s influence sometimes gather in the deeper hollows. The [[Rift of Discord]] is visible from the highest point.

## What the Hollows are

The Shadowed Hollows are the surface manifestation of the [[Temple of Mordrax]]'s buried presence. **Where the [[Rift of Discord]] is the wound left by the [[Discordant War]]'s final battle, the Hollows are the older wound** — the corrupted forest that has stood here since before the [[Dark Hold]] was built, since before the rebels began their excavation, since the Temple's seal was first laid in the founding age. The Hollows are not a war legacy. **They are the consequence of what the Temple holds, leaking slowly through what should have been ordinary rock and forest.**

The corruption here is **diffuse and psychological** rather than the [[Rift of Discord|Rift]]'s acute physical-adjacent effects. Visitors entering the Hollows feel watched, then attended to, then specifically known. The forest's stillness is the defining experiential feature — **a stillness that is not quiet, but listening**. Travelers who cross the Hollows interior carry the experience with them in ways that do not reliably resolve.

## The threshold

Access to the Hollows interior runs through the [[Shadowed Hollows Approach]] corridor — the perimeter zone that absorbs the corruption's outer effects before they reach ordinary realm terrain. **The [[Vigil]] patrols the Approach** and intercepts unauthorized travelers; entry to the interior proper requires Vigil sanction at a higher threshold than even the [[Rift of Discord Edge|Rift Edge]]'s sanction process. **The Hollows' psychological corruption means the Vigil evaluates not only legitimate purpose but also the traveler's psychological preparation for interior exposure.**

Travelers approved for Hollows interior access typically undergo preparatory briefing at a Monastery station before proceeding.

## Cult activity

**Cult activity has been detected here.** Individuals drawn to [[Mordrax]]'s influence sometimes gather in the deeper hollows — small groups, never large, drawn by what the Temple's presence radiates upward through the corrupted ground. Such gatherings are **the [[Sapphire Sentinels]]' specific monitoring target** and occasionally the [[Wardens]]' active-response target when activity escalates beyond observation thresholds.

The cult presence is **not continuous** — gatherings come and go, individuals appear and disappear, no organized faction has been established here in canonical record. **What persists is the attraction.** The Hollows pulls people drawn to that pull, and the realm's institutional response is calibrated to interrupt those gatherings before they consolidate into anything more than transient cells.

## What lies beneath

The [[Temple of Mordrax]] is buried far below the Hollows' surface. **The Hollows are the surface above; the Temple is the source.** The corruption rises through the rock, through the soil, through the canopy, manifesting as the Hollows' specific environmental signature. **The seal on the Temple is what makes the Hollows merely corrupted rather than catastrophic.** If the seal weakened — as it nearly did during the [[Discordant War]]'s rebel excavation — the Hollows would not stay merely Hollows.

The Temple is not approachable from the Hollows. **No surface path leads down to it.** The rebels who attempted to channel its power dug their shaft from the [[Dark Hold]] in what is now the [[Rift of Discord]], not from the Hollows. The Vigil's monitoring of the Hollows is **layered protection of the Temple-adjacent zone** rather than Temple access.

## Functions

- **Surface manifestation of the buried [[Temple of Mordrax]].** The Hollows exist because the Temple exists below; the corruption flows upward.
- **Cult attractor and monitoring zone.** The [[Sapphire Sentinels]] watch for gatherings; the [[Wardens]] respond when activity warrants.
- **Wraith and shadow-creature habitat.** Encounters are occasional but real. Most travelers who reach the Hollows interior without preparation do not engage with these phenomena directly; they leave before the threshold of contact is crossed.
- **Visual line of sight to the [[Rift of Discord]].** From the Hollows' highest point, the [[Rift of Discord|Rift]] is visible across the southern dark quadrant. **The two corruption sources are visually linked but operationally distinct.**

## Character

The Hollows' atmosphere is **brooding rather than acute**. The [[Rift of Discord|Rift]] declares itself; the Hollows watches. Travelers who have crossed both describe the [[Rift of Discord|Rift]] as a wound and the Hollows as a presence. **The Rift is what was done. The Hollows are what is.**

The forest's vegetation is twisted and pale — trees standing wrong, their bark darkened or silvered in irregular patches, their canopies blocking more light than their leaf density should account for, their branches not moving as ordinary branches move in wind. Shadows fall in patterns that almost match the sun's position but do not quite. The leaf litter on the forest floor accumulates without decaying. **Wildlife is nearly absent. Insects are reduced. The silence is dense.**

Travelers carry residual effects from Hollows exposure that can persist for weeks or longer. **A faint version of the zone's pervasive watchedness sometimes carries out into ordinary life.** This effect fades for most travelers. For some, it fades more slowly. **For a few, it does not fade entirely**, and those travelers develop a distinct relationship with solitude and with certain forest terrain that never quite returns to its pre-Hollows character.

## Access

Access to the Hollows interior requires:

- **[[Vigil]] sanction at a higher threshold than [[Rift of Discord Edge|Rift Edge]] access** — including evaluation of the traveler's psychological preparation
- **Council-authorized investigation** for explicit Council-level purpose
- **[[Wardens|Warden]] active-response authorization** when cult activity warrants intervention
- **[[Sapphire Sentinels]] covert operation** — the order's monitoring presence operates through channels distinct from the [[Vigil]]'s patrol structure

Casual visitors and devotional pilgrims are turned back at the [[Shadowed Hollows Approach]] threshold. **The Vigil does not negotiate Hollows entry the way it does for the [[Rift of Discord Edge|Rift Edge]];** the corruption's psychological character makes uncontrolled entry materially more dangerous than even the Rift's, and the order's discipline reflects this.
