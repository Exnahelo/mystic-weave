# The Trade Hall

Where the [[Feywood]] meets the realm in the commercial register. **Ancient, continuous, operationally precise.**

## Scene texture

The Trade Hall is the **largest single structure in [[Vaelmere]]**, built in the elven idiom to realm-utility scale — spanning multiple generations of growth, with working halls for inventory, storage chambers for goods in transit, receiving courts for exchange windows, and the **Trade Master's offices** set above the working floors. The structure integrates with several large old-growth trees; the floors are worked wood over packed earth; natural light floods the main floor through canopy-vents during the day, with bioluminescent Hearthlight supplementing in evenings.

The Hall's working register is **purposeful and professional without being rushed**. Trade goods move through in a steady rhythm — caravans arriving from [[Thornveil]]'s [[Outer Bounds]] in the outbound direction, [[Feywood Border]] exchanges returning in the inbound direction, internal [[Feywood]] goods flowing through at the scale appropriate to their production. Crates, sacks, barrels, woven baskets, bundled textiles, corked flasks of forest wine, sealed jars of preserved Heartmorel — the goods pass through, inventoried, checked, prepared for the next stage.

Sound in the Hall is the sound of competent commerce. Workers checking inventory against manifests, the controlled movement of goods, occasional conversation between elven staff and realm traders who are known regulars. **The register is notably more *working* than [[Thornveil]]'s ceremonial quiet; [[Vaelmere]]'s signature atmosphere is most pronounced here.**

## The Trade Master's office

The **Trade Master** — [[Vaelmere]]'s senior commercial figure — holds offices on the Hall's upper level. This is where the operational coordination happens: scheduling for upcoming [[Feywood Border]] exchange windows, quality-standard decisions, Trade Envoy corps management, coordination with [[Thornveil]]'s [[Outer Bounds]] Liaison and [[Crafthall]] Liaison, institutional relationships with realm-side [[Silver Scale Trading Company|SSTC]] counterparts and specific realm trade houses.

The office is not grand. **Elven civic architecture generally isn't**; the Trade Master works from a space sized for their work rather than their status. Records — ledgers, correspondence, trade-route documentation, and the written archive that the [[Feywood]]'s mercantile relationship generates despite its oral primacy elsewhere — occupy significant space.

Meetings with visiting realm trade partners happen here. **These are substantive working sessions; the relationships between the Trade Master and senior realm counterparts are often decades deep.**

## The Trade Envoy corps

[[Vaelmere]]-based Trade Envoys — the elves who present at [[Feywood Border]] exchange windows — are staffed and trained from the Trade Hall. **This is not a military organization; it is a specialist professional corps with distinct selection, training, and career path.** Envoys typically come up through [[Vaelmere]]'s trade community, with the sustained exposure to realm commercial practice that the work requires. Careers span centuries; senior Envoys often have realm-counterpart relationships that trace back through multiple generations of their realm partners.

Training includes:

- Realm-language fluency (Common across multiple registers, specific trade jargon, diplomatic vocabulary)
- Realm-side commercial practice (weights, measures, **Drake-currency**, negotiation conventions)
- [[Feywood]] production knowledge (the origin and character of every good the [[Feywood]] exports)
- The relational skill set the work requires — patience, discretion, professional warmth, and the specific elven humor that translates across the Border without giving offense

## Inbound flow

Realm goods arriving at [[Vaelmere]] from the [[Feywood Border]] are received, inspected, inventoried, and prepared for onward movement. The inbound flow handles:

- Metal tools and worked metal goods (the [[Feywood]]'s largest import category)
- Specialty craft materials the [[Feywood]] cannot produce internally
- Realm books and scrolls commissioned by specific [[Feywood]] institutions
- Foodstuffs and consumables that specific [[Feywood]] clients have ordered
- Standing-order goods for specific noble houses, [[Council of Elders|Council]] institutions, and the [[Silent Grove]]

Goods are tagged, documented, and forwarded — most to [[Thornveil]]'s [[Outer Bounds]], some to specific recipients in the [[Feywood]], a small amount retained for [[Vaelmere]]'s own use.

## Outbound flow

[[Feywood]] goods arriving at the Trade Hall from [[Thornveil]] (and from other [[Feywood]] producers directly) are aggregated, standardized for export, and prepared for [[Feywood Border]] exchange windows. The outbound flow handles the full [[Feywood]] export set:

- Refined herbal preparations (Silverleaf, Dawnmint, Redveil Shelf)
- Preserved culinary fungi (Heartmorel in particular)
- Resinheart Pine refined resins, sealants, incense
- Thornfiber basketry and fine weaving
- Forest wines, Goldcap cider, Goldcap vinegar, honey mead
- Select Honeybound honey and wax products
- Winterglass Lichen dyes and ritual-marking pigments
- Elven-worked goods from the [[Crafthall]]'s metal, wood, and leather lineages (**finished goods only; no raw timber ever**)
- Very rare Longbough artifacts when available

### Export exclusions (hard)

These are **never exported under any circumstances**:

- **Oathbloom-based preparations.** Ceremonial flora used for elven oath observances; never leaves the [[Feywood]].
- **[[Heartwood]]-derived material of any kind.** Branches, leaves, bark, or root material from the [[Heartwood]] never leave the [[Feywood]].
- **Living Witheroak wood.** Never exported in living form.

These exclusions are enforced rigorously at the Trade Hall.

## Visitors

Realm traders on sustained [[Feywood]] business spend much of their [[Vaelmere]] time in and around the Trade Hall. Longer-term relationships are supported by the Hall's working infrastructure — meeting rooms, documentation access, scheduling coordination, referral to specific [[Feywood]] producers when needed.

Realm scholars of elven commerce occasionally have access to the Hall's records with Trade Master approval. **These relationships span careers.**

Casual visitors do not enter the Hall. It is a working institution, not a public space.
