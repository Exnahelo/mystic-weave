# The Guest House

[[Vaelmere]]'s standing accommodation for sanctioned realm visitors. **Elven in design, realm-visitor-competent in service.**

## Scene texture

The Guest House is a distinct structure within [[Vaelmere]], sized for simultaneous occupancy by a handful to a dozen visitors. The structure follows the elven architectural idiom — grown-and-woven construction integrated with specific old-growth trees — but is laid out for the functional patterns realm visitors bring: individual private rooms, a shared dining space, common areas for reading or meeting, bathhouse facilities, laundry access, storage for travel gear. **Nothing about it would look out of place in an elven setting; nothing about it would fail the practical needs of a realm trader or scholar.**

Rooms are modest. Each has a bed, a washing station, a small workspace, storage for personal belongings, and a private window opening onto the surrounding canopy. Bioluminescent Hearthlight handles evening illumination, supplemented by beeswax candles. Linen and blankets are of good elven weave, warm and durable. The sound of the Guest House at rest is the sound of sanctioned occupancy — low voices, occasional movement, **the settling of a building that has hosted realm visitors for many generations**.

## Services

The Guest House provides:

- **Private rooms.** Individual occupancy by default; double-occupancy for guests traveling together, available on request.
- **Shared dining.** Three meals daily at [[Vaelmere]]'s rhythm (early, midday, evening). Food is [[Feywood]] plant-forward with modest protein accommodation; realm visitors with specific dietary needs can discuss options with the Keeper. The menu shifts by season and availability; bread, cheese, dried and fresh fruit, honey, preserved Heartmorel, vegetable preparations, Silverstream trout when available, occasional venison or boar.
- **Drink.** [[Feywood]] forest wines, Goldcap cider, honey mead at evening meals. Water refilled freely. **Realm ale and wine are not routinely available**; realm visitors accustomed to these adjust to elven equivalents during [[Vaelmere]] stays.
- **Bathhouse access.** Shared bathhouse facility with hot water warmed from the [[Commons Hearth]] infrastructure.
- **Laundry.** Simple-load laundry service by arrangement; most visitors handle their own over extended stays.
- **Storage.** Secure storage for travel gear, documents, and valuables. **Elven institutional integrity on this is excellent.**
- **Meeting space.** Common areas suitable for small meetings; larger gatherings use the [[Trade Hall]]'s meeting rooms by arrangement.

## The Keeper

The **Guest House Keeper** is [[Vaelmere]]'s senior hospitality figure — a long-tenured [[Vaelmere]] resident with extensive realm-visitor experience, usually in the role for decades, coordinating with [[Vaelmere]]'s broader civic and trade infrastructure on visitor care. **The Keeper is not a servant figure**; the role is substantive and the Keeper's judgment on visitor matters carries real weight with the Warden and [[Trade Hall|Trade Master]].

The Keeper handles:

- Visitor intake and room assignment
- Dietary and accessibility accommodations
- Coordination with visitors' [[Vaelmere]] business contacts
- Mediation of any visitor-related issues (rare but not unknown)
- The small library of realm books the Guest House maintains for extended-stay visitors

## Visitor patterns

Typical Guest House residents:

- **Realm traders on sustained business.** Most realm traders spending days to weeks at [[Vaelmere]] stay here. Long-term repeat visitors develop genuine relationships with the Keeper and with [[Vaelmere]] regulars.
- **Scholars.** Realm scholars with [[Vaelmere]]-level access for specific research — most commonly trade-relationship research, [[Vaelmere]] social studies, [[Feywood]] herbalism through the [[Feywood]] species accessible at [[Vaelmere]].
- **Diplomatic staff.** Non-ambassadorial diplomatic personnel on extended [[Vaelmere]]-level business stay here; full ambassadors receive [[Wardens Hall|Warden's-Hall]]-level accommodation for shorter visits.
- **Intake visitors.** Visitors arriving for [[Feywood]]-level business who are processed through [[Vaelmere]] on the way in or out sometimes spend brief nights here as part of their journey.

Length of stay varies. Short stays of a few days are common for routine trade visits. Medium stays of several weeks are common for scholarly work. Extended stays of several months happen but are unusual and typically involve specific approval from the Warden.

## Register and expectations

The Guest House is **warm but not casual**. Visitors are expected to operate within [[Vaelmere]]'s general cultural register — respect for the [[Feywood]]'s ethical framework, appropriate accommodation of the settlement's daily rhythm, discretion regarding [[Vaelmere]]'s inner civic life. **The Keeper quietly tracks visitor conduct**; sustained disrespect results in Warden-level involvement and, in rare cases, shortened stays.

Realm visitor experience at the Guest House is generally positive across the centuries. **Repeat visitors speak of the accommodations with genuine fondness**; the Keeper's reputation in the realm trade community is excellent.
