# Vaelmere

The [[Feywood]]'s outer settlement and trade waypoint, positioned in the Feywood's realm-facing interior where the sanctioned path from the [[Feywood Border]] leads inward before continuing onward to [[Thornveil]]. Vaelmere is **where external commerce becomes elven commerce** — realm traders bringing goods through the Border meet Vaelmere's trade houses; elven goods bound for the Border are staged here before their outward journey.

The settlement is also a real elven community in its own right, home to **roughly six hundred residents** who combine the trader, craftfolk, and civic roles a commercial waypoint requires with the continuous elven life that characterizes all [[Feywood]] settlements.

**Vaelmere exists to protect [[Thornveil]] from the friction of daily commercial contact with the realm** while honoring the mercantile alliance that has sustained the Feywood-realm relationship for a thousand years.

## Scene texture

A traveler approaching Vaelmere from the [[Feywood Border]] traverses several hours of [[Feywood]] interior before reaching the settlement. The path is guided for sanctioned visitors and known to returning elven traders; it passes through ordinary [[Feywood]] wilderness that gradually shows signs of regular use.

Vaelmere emerges from the forest **with more visible structure than [[Thornveil]] does**. The settlement's outermost edges are clearer-bounded, marked by a gentle transition from wild [[Feywood]] terrain to cultivated margins. Gardens appear, grown in loose organic patterns. Paths become clearer. Buildings become visible — first scattered at the settlement's outer reaches, then increasingly dense as the traveler approaches the central commons.

The architecture shares [[Thornveil]]'s organic-elven idiom but is **markedly more modest**. Vaelmere's buildings are grown-with and crafted-with the forest, incorporating living trees and using elven methods, but the buildings are smaller on average, less ambitious in their integration, and more practical in their function. Where [[Thornveil]]'s older structures represent centuries of accumulated craft in single dwellings, Vaelmere's buildings reflect a more working character — structures that serve their purpose well without demanding to be architectural statements.

The **Central Commons** of Vaelmere is a broad clearing roughly fifty paces across, with a grown wooden pavilion at its center that shelters the settlement's primary gathering space. Unlike [[Thornveil]]'s Council-linked central areas, Vaelmere's Commons is **explicitly a civic and commercial space** — trade announcements, community meetings, and seasonal observances all happen here.

The **light** matches the [[Feywood]]'s general character. Vaelmere's canopy is thinner than [[Thornveil]]'s — this is a newer settlement in elven terms, built in terrain where the canopy has not yet grown as dense — so more direct light reaches the ground areas. Many residents appreciate this; the settlement's character includes more sun exposure than the deeper [[Feywood]] interior, making it feel slightly more connected to the realm's ordinary world.

## Major buildings

Surrounding the Commons:

- **[[Trade Hall]]** — Vaelmere's primary mercantile institution. The Trade Master's offices, storage spaces for goods in transit. Realm visitors on trade business interact primarily with this facility.
- **[[Wardens Hall|Warden's Hall]]** — office and residence of the Warden of Vaelmere, the settlement's civic authority.
- **[[Guest House]]** — accommodations for realm visitors who are sanctioned for Vaelmere access but not proceeding onward to [[Thornveil]]. Simple but comfortable lodging; most visits last from several days to weeks.
- **[[Commons Hearth]]** — shared kitchen and dining space where the settlement's community takes many of its meals. Not mandatory, but a significant social anchor where daily life coalesces.

Beyond the Commons, **residential districts** spread outward through the surrounding forest. Clusters of dwellings in the elven pattern, with individual families and extended family groups occupying specific grown-spaces maintained across generations.

Workshops and craft sites are scattered through the residential areas rather than concentrated in a single crafthall. Vaelmere's craft economy is smaller and more diverse than [[Thornveil]]'s — individual craftfolk working from home-workshops integrated with their dwellings.

The **Greenshield guardhouse** sits at the settlement's outer edge, on the path that continues inward to [[Thornveil]]. Where [[Greenshield]] members holding outer-rotation duty operate from — not a fortress, but a practical working facility.

## Functions

### Mercantile waypoint

Vaelmere's primary institutional function is mercantile coordination. The realm-Feywood exchange windows conducted at the [[Feywood Border]] are operationally supported from Vaelmere:

- **Outbound side.** Elven goods bound for realm trade are aggregated at Vaelmere from across the [[Feywood]] — craftwork from [[Thornveil]]'s [[Crafthall]], herbal preparations and specimens from outer Feywood sites, specific magical materials. Vaelmere's [[Trade Hall]] inventories and prepares these goods for the journey to the Border.
- **Inbound side.** Realm goods arriving through exchange windows are received at Vaelmere and processed. From here, goods are distributed onward — to [[Thornveil]]'s markets, to specific noble families with standing orders, and to the [[Feywood]]'s broader elven community through Vaelmere's own markets.
- **Trade staffing.** The **Trade Master** and their staff coordinate the full operational flow. **Trade Envoys who present at Border exchanges are drawn from Vaelmere's experienced trade community** — the elves with the most sustained exposure to realm commercial practice, selected and trained for the work over careers that span centuries.

### Diplomatic buffer

Vaelmere serves as the primary site for realm-[[Feywood]] diplomatic contact that does not require [[Council of Elders|Council]]-level engagement. When realm officials visit the [[Feywood]] on matters below the [[Council of Elders|Council]] threshold, they typically:

- Cross the [[Feywood Border]] with sanctioned approval
- Travel to Vaelmere
- Conduct their business with the **Warden of Vaelmere**, the **Trade Master**, or specific other Vaelmere figures as appropriate
- Depart from Vaelmere back to the Border

**This structure protects [[Thornveil]] from the friction of routine diplomatic traffic while ensuring realm concerns receive appropriate engagement.** [[Council of Elders|Council]]-level matters — substantive diplomatic questions, major treaty revisions, formal ceremonial engagement — escalate to [[Thornveil]] with appropriate protocol. Ordinary matters resolve at Vaelmere.

### Elven community

Beyond institutional functions, Vaelmere is a real elven community where approximately 600 elves live permanent lives:

- **Established families** — multi-generational families whose deep local roots span the settlement's history
- **Civic and trade leadership** — the Warden's circle, the Trade Master and experienced traders
- **Craftfolk** — weavers, metalworkers, woodworkers, herbalists
- **[[Greenshield]] members** — rotating population holding outer-rotation duty; tours typically last years
- **Non-elf integrated residents** — perhaps **5-10% of population, notably higher than [[Thornveil]]'s rate**. Vaelmere's realm-facing function attracts more non-elf residents than the capital does

### Feywood edge awareness

Vaelmere's residents serve as the [[Feywood]]'s informal sensor for realm-side pressure. The **Greenshield Outer-Rotation Captain** coordinates monitoring of [[Feywood Border]] activity — not through formal patrol but through routine information gathering from traders returning from exchanges, visitors arriving with news, and the natural attentiveness of elves living near the [[Feywood]]'s edge.

Unusual realm-side activity flows from Vaelmere inward to [[Thornveil]] through established reporting channels. The [[Council of Elders|Council]] receives this information through Vaelmere's Warden as part of the settlement's standing coordination with the capital.

## Civic character

The **Warden of Vaelmere** (Tier-3 generative role) is the settlement's senior civic authority. Handles local dispute resolution, coordination with [[Thornveil]]'s [[Council of Elders|Council]], oversight of the [[Trade Hall]]'s operations, host-diplomat role for realm visitors, general civic administration. Wardens of Vaelmere typically serve for long tenures — decades to centuries.

The **Trade Master** (Tier-3 generative role) handles the settlement's commercial operations. Coordinates the operational flow of goods through the [[Trade Hall]], manages the Vaelmere-based Trade Envoy corps, maintains institutional relationships with realm-side [[Silver Scale Trading Company|SSTC]] counterparts.

The **Greenshield Outer-Rotation Captain** (Tier-3 generative role) commands the outer-rotation duty at Vaelmere. Rotating position — senior [[Greenshield]] officers take outer-rotation commands for fixed tours before returning to [[Thornveil]] headquarters.

## Character

Vaelmere is **working**, in ways that neither [[Thornveil]] (civic/ancient/sacred-core) nor the [[Silent Grove]] (focused/disciplined/ceremonial) quite are. Vaelmere's work is practical, commercial, outward-facing.

The Vaelmere community is **somewhat more accustomed to external presence** than [[Thornveil]] or any deeper [[Feywood]] location. Residents see realm faces regularly. This familiarity has produced a community that is comfortable with external interaction without losing its elven character.

**Humor in Vaelmere is somewhat more accessible than in [[Thornveil]].** The layered, slow-developing elven humor is still present, but Vaelmere residents have learned to include realm visitors in simpler observational humor. A realm trader who becomes a regular at Vaelmere's [[Commons Hearth]] develops, over years, genuine social relationships with Vaelmere residents — something that happens far more rarely in [[Thornveil]].

The respect principle still operates. **The settlement has learned patience for realm visitors who haven't yet fully learned the [[Feywood]]'s way** — but the same calibration applies as elsewhere in the [[Feywood]]: respect earns warmth, disrespect cools the welcome.

## Access

Accessible from the [[Feywood Border]] by the sanctioned path, several hours' travel. **Most realm visits to the [[Feywood]] end at Vaelmere; continuation to [[Thornveil]] requires additional approvals.**

Typical sanctioned visitors:
- Realm traders on mercantile business (the largest visitor category)
- Realm diplomats on matters below Council threshold
- Scholars with [[Council of Elders|Feywood Council]] approval for research at Vaelmere-level access
- Approved guests of specific Vaelmere residents or institutions

**Lodging:** at the [[Guest House]] for most visitors. Resident-hosted stays for guests of specific Vaelmere families or institutions.

**Continuation to [[Thornveil]]:** requires separate approval from the Thornveil [[Council of Elders|Council]]. Visitors approved for [[Thornveil]] access proceed with elven escort from Vaelmere; the journey takes additional hours.

**Dragon Guard presence: None.** Vaelmere is a sovereign elven settlement.
