# The Outer Bounds

Where [[Thornveil]] thins. Newer residents begin here. Trade leaves and returns here. **The boundary between the city and the [[Feywood]] around it is gradient, not line; the Outer Bounds is that gradient.**

## Scene texture

The district feels less like a city district than any of [[Thornveil]]'s inner zones. Canopy is managed but less dense; dwellings are set further apart; the ambient field is moderated by the mix of civic and wild. A walker moving out through the Outer Bounds experiences the city gradually releasing them into the [[Feywood]]'s larger forest — the change in light, the quieting of civic sound, the sense of space opening.

Paths here are better developed than in the Outer Wild but less continuous than in the inner districts. The trade-staging area has its own working infrastructure; the residential clusters have their own walks; the district's outer edge trails dissolve into [[Feywood]] wild paths that lead toward [[Vaelmere]] or outward.

The district receives more realm visitors than any other part of [[Thornveil]]. Not many — [[Thornveil]] is not an open city — but more than the inner districts. **This shapes the Outer Bounds' register: slightly more accommodating, slightly more ready for outsiders, while still firmly [[Thornveil]].**

## Trade staging

The Outer Bounds' trade-staging area is the city's interface with [[Vaelmere]]'s flow. Goods move through it in both directions:

**Outbound.** Craft goods from the [[Crafthall]], refined preparations from the Alchemy workshops, honey and wax from household keepers, forest wines and Goldcap cider from district producers — all aggregated at the staging area for transport to [[Vaelmere]]. The staging area includes covered storage, inventory-working spaces, and the working infrastructure for elven trader caravans.

**Inbound.** Realm goods arriving from [[Vaelmere]] are received here, inventoried, and distributed onward to [[Thornveil]]'s markets, the [[Crafthall]], specific noble family receiving halls, and the [[Council of Elders|Council]]'s administrative infrastructure. Metal tools, specialty craft materials, realm books and scrolls for those who commissioned them, occasional specific goods that particular [[Thornveil]] institutions have ordered.

The **Trade Envoy Outer Bounds Liaison** is a [[Council of Elders|Council]]-appointed senior role, coordinating directly with [[Vaelmere]]'s [[Trade Hall|Trade Master]]. The Liaison's offices are in the staging area.

## Integration zone

The Outer Bounds holds a disproportionate share of [[Thornveil]]'s newer residents:

**Integrated non-elves.** Humans, rare dwarves, occasional drakari, others of long-term residence in [[Thornveil]] most commonly begin and often remain in the Outer Bounds. Integration here is less socially observed than in the inner districts — the [[Common Hearth|Hearth]]'s tight cluster life or the [[Noble Groves|Groves]]' house-inflected register can be difficult to enter; **the Outer Bounds is more open**.

**Returning elves.** Elves returning from extended absences — realm-side work, outer-Feywood postings, long study elsewhere, diplomatic service — often settle in the Outer Bounds first as they re-establish [[Thornveil]] residency. Some stay; some move inward over years.

**Younger elves establishing lives.** Elves in the equivalent of young adulthood sometimes establish their first independent residences in the Outer Bounds, working in the [[Crafthall]] or [[Common Hearth]] and moving inward as their own work and relationships require.

The district's residential pattern reflects all this. **Clusters exist but are looser and less continuous than in the [[Common Hearth|Hearth]].** Single-household dwellings are more common. Specific households maintain their own character — some quite elven-traditional, some visibly shaped by non-elven residents' contributions to the shared life.

## Visitors

Realm visitors on sanctioned business who are not proceeding to deeper [[Thornveil]] access typically spend their visits here. Trade representatives conducting extended business with the Trade Envoy corps, scholars with specific Outer-Bounds-level access, diplomatic staff for matters that do not require [[Council of Elders|Council]]-level contact — all stay within the Outer Bounds during their [[Thornveil]] visits.

Visitors proceeding to deeper access pass through the Outer Bounds on their way and are collected by elven hosts here. **The Outer Bounds is often the visitor's first in-city [[Thornveil]] experience and shapes their sense of the rest of the city accordingly.**

## Connection to Vaelmere

The path between [[Thornveil]]'s Outer Bounds and [[Vaelmere]] is [[Thornveil]]'s most-traveled outbound route. The journey is done by elven trader caravans, by diplomatic envoys moving between the city and the trade waypoint, by elves with [[Vaelmere]] family or working relationships, and occasionally by realm visitors moving between the two under elven escort. The route is well-established and reasonably safe under Standing Green; under Turning or Stillfall the [[Feywood]]'s general responsiveness adjusts, and caravans move with more care.
