# Feywood Border

The realm-side threshold to the [[Feywood]] — the boundary zone surrounding the elven realm, located within the [[Western Temperate Forest]] outside the [[Feywood]] proper. From outside, the Border shows as an ordinary forest edge: a line of older, taller, stranger trees standing somewhat differently than their realm-side neighbors, the ground beyond them shifting in quality in ways that a traveler notices without being able to name. **There is no wall, no gate, no physical barrier.** The Border is marked by stone waymarkers set in place over generations and by the small [[Hall of Guides]] station that manages sanctioned expedition traffic.

The realm honors an ancient alliance with the [[Feywood]]: safe passage at the Border is reserved for those with guide sanction or elven welcome; unsanctioned entry is tolerated but genuinely consequential. Travelers who cross without preparation rarely return as they departed, and some do not return at all.

## A note on the Border's shape

The Border is a **perimeter zone**, not a single point. It surrounds the [[Feywood]] entirely. There is one sanctioned road in — the route through the [[Western Temperate Forest]] from [[Dracelune]], terminating at the marked crossing where the Hall of Guides station stands, with its waymarkers and registered traffic. That single road leads inward to [[Vaelmere]]. **But the perimeter itself is long.** Sanctioned exits return through the same marked crossing under guide supervision; unsanctioned exits via the [[Heartwood]]'s root network can deposit travelers anywhere in the realm (see [[Heartwood]] under The Root Network). And someone determined to enter the [[Feywood]] without sanction can do so anywhere along the perimeter — the Feywood is not walled, only thresholded. The consequences of unsanctioned entry are documented below.

## Scene texture

The realm-side approach is a forest road through the [[Western Temperate Forest]]. Well-maintained for the first several miles — a realm-civic route like any other — and then gradually, as it nears the Border, its character shifts. The road's edges are less sharply defined. The surrounding forest grows quieter in subtle ways. The sounds of realm wildlife diminish while the light under the canopy takes on a slightly different quality. A traveler can feel the Border approaching for perhaps a quarter mile before reaching it.

The Border itself is **a specific physical zone rather than a line**. The realm-side forest's familiar oaks, beeches, and pines give way to trees of similar species but subtly different bearing — older, taller, standing slightly farther apart, their canopies reaching higher. **Realm trees grow; Feywood trees watch.**

The ground at the Border changes character. Realm-side forest floor gives way to ground that is somewhat more densely carpeted with moss and leaf litter, with ferns appearing more thickly, and with occasional patches of bioluminescent fungi that glow faintly even in daylight. The transition is not sharp.

The **stone waymarkers** are the Border's most explicit marker. Upright stones, roughly twice the height of a person, placed at intervals along the known sanctioned crossing points. Ancient — some date to the sanctuary's founding period — carved with elven script that is recognizable to those familiar with Feywood work but not otherwise legible. **Crossing the Border at a waymarker is recognized by both realm and Feywood conventions as a proper crossing.**

The **Hall of Guides station** sits a short distance from the primary waymarker pair — a modest wooden-and-stone structure. Typically one or two guides are present, sometimes the Border Steward specifically. Provides expedition registration, guide assignment for sanctioned crossings, briefing materials about current Feywood conditions, emergency shelter, and the coordination point for returning expeditions re-entering the realm. **It is not an inn.**

The realm-side air at the Border is different from ordinary forest air. It carries a faint sweetness and a quality that travelers describe as **awaiting**.

Visitors standing at the Border can often see into the [[Feywood]]'s realm-facing edge. What they see looks like ordinary forest continuing beyond. The trick is that the ordinary forest they see does not extend as far as it appears; a few paces in, the geometry of the space begins to do whatever the [[Feywood]] has decided the geometry of the space will do that day.

## The crossing

Crossing the Border is the event that matters. **Sanctioned travelers** — accompanied by a [[Hall of Guides]] guide, or carrying an elven welcome-token, or under formal diplomatic protocol — cross safely. The crossing itself is experienced as a specific moment: a shift in the air, a change in the quality of sound, a brief sensation of passage through something that is more than ordinary air. Sanctioned travelers arrive at [[Vaelmere]], the inward elven settlement that handles external trade.

**Travelers crossing without sanction** have a different experience. The [[Feywood]]'s liminal geometry does not treat unsanctioned entrants kindly. They may:

- Find themselves in the [[Feywood]] but not where they expected, with no clear path back
- Walk for hours or days through terrain that does not resolve into recognizable features
- Exit the [[Feywood]] through an exit they did not choose, emerging into the realm at some location potentially far from where they entered
- Meet [[Feywood]] residents whose dispositions vary from curious to unfriendly
- Experience time passage that does not match the time that has passed in the realm
- Not return at all

This is not hostility from the [[Feywood]] in an ordinary sense. The elves do not seek out unsanctioned visitors to harm them. **Those who respect the wood, the wood respects.** Those who enter without respect, the wood does not protect.

The realm's [[Hall of Guides]] and the elven [[Council of Elders]] both take the same position: the Border is open, the crossing is available, the consequences of unsanctioned crossing are the traveler's own.

## The Hall of Guides presence

The **Hall of Guides Border Steward** is the senior Hall figure regularly stationed at the Border. Tier-3 generative role. Handles expedition registration, guide assignment, briefing travelers on current Feywood conditions, coordination with elven traders arriving at the Border, and the practical work of maintaining the Hall's Border operations. Stewards rotate in and out of Border duty on multi-week schedules.

Expedition traffic is modest but steady. Scholars with approved [[Feywood]] research commissions. Traders conducting the established mercantile exchanges. Diplomatic couriers on realm-Feywood coordination matters. Rarely, [[Council of Elders|Council]]-sanctioned representatives traveling to the elven Council on formal business. Each expedition is guided; **none cross without a [[Hall of Guides]] member accompanying**.

Guides who specialize in [[Feywood]] expedition work carry specific knowledge of the crossing, the current safe paths once inside, and the techniques for re-exiting at the intended Border location rather than somewhere else in the realm. **This knowledge is the Hall of Guides' most valuable canonical expertise.**

## Mercantile exchange

The realm-[[Feywood]] mercantile relationship is structured through periodic **exchange windows** conducted at or near the Border. During an exchange window — typically scheduled in advance, lasting several days — elven traders bring [[Feywood]] goods to the Border zone and realm traders bring the realm's contributions. The exchange happens on the realm side of the Border, in the cleared area near the [[Hall of Guides]] station.

**Feywood goods traded:** fey-crafted herbal preparations, certain enchanted woods and plant materials unavailable in the realm, specific artifacts of elven craftwork, occasionally magical items.

**Realm goods provided:** magical crystals, magical materials brought in through external trade, specialty metals, certain manufactured goods.

An **Elven Trade Envoy** is the senior [[Feywood]] figure who presents at exchange windows. Tier-3 generative role. Drawn from [[Vaelmere]]'s experienced trade community — long-tenured elven traders empowered to conduct the exchange on the [[Feywood]]'s behalf. The Envoy does not live at the Border; they cross from the [[Feywood]] for the exchange window and return afterward.

Between exchange windows, the Border is quieter. Casual traffic is minimal. The stone waymarkers stand where they have always stood. **The air continues to await.**

## Functions

- **Realm-side sanctioned threshold.** The Border is the realm's recognized entry point to the [[Feywood]].
- **Expedition infrastructure.** Hall of Guides station, stone waymarkers, adjacent cleared terrain.
- **Mercantile exchange site.** Periodic exchange windows.
- **Elven diplomatic contact point.** Formal diplomatic matters between the realm and the [[Feywood]] typically route through the Border initially. The elves do not typically travel into the realm's interior; the realm sends delegations to the Border.
- **Return point for returning expeditions.** Travelers returning from the [[Feywood]] aim to re-exit through the sanctioned Border, under guide supervision.

## Character

The Border has a specific atmospheric character. Not frightening like the [[Rift of Discord]] Edge. Not corrupted like the Hollows Approach. Not militarily serious like the [[Platinum Oath Monastery|Platinum Oath]] Approach.

**The Border is *awaiting*.** The space attends to what happens at it without malice and without particular benevolence. The stillness at the Border is different from the Hollows' listening quiet. The Hollows watches the traveler. The Border awaits the crossing.

Senior Border Stewards describe the Border as having moods — some days the crossing feels easier, some more charged, some when the [[Feywood]]'s interior seems closer to the threshold and some when it feels further away. These moods are not predictable from realm weather or calendar; they are [[Feywood]] phenomena experienced at the Border.

## Access

Reachable by realm-side road through the [[Western Temperate Forest]]. The [[Hall of Guides]] station does not gate the Border; it provides services for those who want them.

**Sanctioned crossing** requires:
- A [[Hall of Guides]] guide accompanying the traveler, OR
- An elven welcome-token (rare; certain realm figures carry tokens from specific elven contacts), OR
- Formal diplomatic protocol for [[Council of Elders|Council]]-level delegation

**Dragon Guard presence** is minimal — the realm-Feywood alliance is not a security matter requiring garrison.
