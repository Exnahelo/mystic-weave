# Heartwater Basin

The lake-and-river system at the [[Feywood]]'s hydrological heart — an asymmetric freshwater zone that shapes the [[Thornveil]] landmass and drains outward through the Outer Old-Growth Wild. The Basin is a full ecological system in its own right, supporting the Feywood's aquatic fauna, waterfowl, and riparian flora, and providing the water that sustains both [[Thornveil]]'s civic life and the deeper forest interior.

It is also the **second-fastest zone to register [[Heartwood]] stress**; druids monitor its surface behavior, amphibian choruses, and fish populations as early indicators of the broader land's health. The Basin is not a sacred site in the ritual sense but is continuously attended by the elves who live around it.

## Scene texture

The Basin is the [[Feywood]]'s water. A central lake, irregular in outline, sits across terrain that shapes [[Thornveil]]'s geography and the deeper interior beyond. Rivers feed in from higher ground in the Outer Old-Growth Wild and exit through channels that eventually find their way out to the Border's drainage. The water is consistently cool, clear enough to see a wading heron's legs to the knee, and carries the same faint ambient quality the rest of the Feywood does — a freshwater brightness that realm-side water does not quite replicate.

The lake's edges are reeded and irregular. Stands of tall reed alternate with patches of open shore. The shore itself varies — clean gravel that boats can put in against, soft mud under a carpet of moss, ragged transitions through willow-analog stands and riparian thicket. **There are no docks in the realm-built sense**; there are elven landing places, worked into the shore with the same organic restraint that characterizes [[Thornveil]]'s architecture.

Sound in the Basin is its own register:

- **Heartpond frog chorus at dusk** is one of the Feywood's canonical ambient sounds. On a Standing Green evening it carries for great distances.
- **Cranemoor heron calls** come across the water sporadically.
- **Silverstream trout** rise in the shallows when the light is right.
- Under canopy where the reeds thin, the quiet is deeper than anywhere else in the Feywood — water-silence, not forest-silence.

Boats are present but restrained. Elven craft tend toward narrow, shallow-draft designs worked from fallen wood; they move under paddle, not under sail. The Basin is not the sort of place where a trader's galley would make sense. **The water is a working ecology, not a transit lane.**

## Ecological role

The Basin is the **second zone in the Feywood's seven-stage decline order**. [[Thornveil]]'s cultivated plots register [[Heartwood]] stress first; the Basin's aquatic life follows.

Specific signals druids watch for:

- **Fish populations.** Silverstream trout thin in Turning. Absence of visible trout in the shallows when conditions should support them is an early warning.
- **Amphibian chorus.** Heartpond frogs reduce their evening chorus, then cease. Silence in warm-season evenings where chorus should be is a significant signal.
- **Waterfowl behavior.** Cranemoor herons depart early, moving to the outer drainages or beyond the Border entirely.
- **Surface behavior.** The water's surface holds a specific quality in Standing Green — a fineness of movement, a consistency of response to wind. In Turning it becomes flatter and more metallic. **Druids reading water can distinguish Standing Green from early Turning by surface alone.**

A rotation of druids from the [[Silent Grove]] attends the Basin on a schedule that varies by season and ambient-field conditions; between scheduled attendance, local elves living around the Basin's margins carry the observation load informally.

## Fauna

- **Silverstream trout** — primary protein source for the [[Feywood]]; steady population under Standing Green
- **Lakepike** — apex fish of the lake; large, ambush, rare but known. Older elven fishers speak of specific pike holding specific water for generations.
- **Feywood otter** — common along shallows and reed-margins; small family groups; handled gently by the elves who share their water
- **Cranemoor heron** — wading species; canonical sentinel of the waterline
- **Heartpond frog** — chorus species; health-indicator by sound as much as by sight
- **Silverback marten** — mustelid along river-banks and outer drainage
- **Briar-stinger** — biting insects in warm-season evenings; nuisance but localized

Occasional **oldroot bear** visits during seasonal fish runs. The bears work specific pools on a schedule elven fishers know and accommodate.

## Flora

Riparian species dominate the margins:

- **River Reed Onion** — culinary plant, tended in elven riparian plots
- **Reedbeds** — habitat structure, seasonal indicator, material for Thornfiber cane work
- **Watermeadow grasses** — margin species; tolerant of seasonal inundation
- **Dewberry bramble** at drier margins — berry shrub feeding birds, otters, bears, and elven gatherers alike

Shade margins carry the full canopy mix of the surrounding forest — Elderoak, Silverbeech, Grey Beech, stands of Resinheart Pine at higher banks.

## Human use

Elven riparian plots handle the Basin's cultivated work — reed onion tending, watermeadow management, aquaculture in the loose sense (no farmed ponds, but active encouragement of trout spawning habitat). Thornfiber cane is gathered seasonally for basketry, binding, and training apparatus.

**Fishing is a working practice, not a sport**; nets are used more than rods; the catch is small and consistent rather than large and irregular.

Realm visitors to [[Vaelmere]] occasionally request access to the Basin for scholarly purposes — realm freshwater ecology specialists, in particular, have sometimes been granted escorted access over decades. Access for trade purposes is rare; the Basin's produce does not move in volume.

## Travel notes

- From [[Thornveil]]: the Basin borders the city on several sides. Elves walk to it routinely.
- From [[Heartwood Estate]] (Outer Old-Growth Wild): several hours' walk to the nearest regular access point, or less depending on which drainage one follows.
- The Basin has no sanctioned crossing from the [[Feywood Border]]; realm traders do not pass through it.
