# Infernal Forge

The realm's sole production source for institutional-grade enchanted metalwork. The Infernal Forge is **a complex, not a single building** — primary smelting halls, casting floors, finishing workshops, ward-anchoring stations, and raw-material holding yards arranged around a central heat-pit where the volcanic system's fire is concentrated and channeled. **The Forge sits on a platform of reinforced volcanic stone in the active lava bed at [[Zarkharath]]'s center**, fueled by volcanic heat and the breath of [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] whose Lair rises across the causeway. The realm's [[Dragon Guard]] depends on the Forge. The [[Wardens]] depend on it. The [[Arcane Conservatory]] depends on it for the metals its enchantments require. **Without the Infernal Forge, the realm's institutional armament collapses.**

## What the Forge is

The Infernal Forge is the [[Infernal Forge Guild]]'s production complex. **It is enchanted infrastructure, not merely heavy industry.** Dragon-scale wards cover the entire platform; the wards funnel volcanic heat into productive use while protecting workers from temperatures that would otherwise be lethal.

The Forge produces:

- **Enchanted weapons, armor, and constructs** for the [[Dragon Guard]], the [[Wardens]], and the realm's other institutional buyers — the line for which the Forge is uniquely qualified
- **Ordinary-quality weapons, tools, hardware, and construction fittings** at industrial scale — the realm's general-economy metalwork
- **Specialty alloys** drawing on rare ores from [[Cinderpit]]'s deep-shaft and specialty-metal operations, including materials the realm would otherwise have to import

**Hundreds of pieces per year on the enchanted line, each tied to specific commissions and quality-graded by Guild standards.** The ordinary line moves substantially more volume than that.

## The complex

### Primary smelting halls

Where raw material from [[Cinderpit]]'s feedstock pipeline meets first heat. The smelting halls are the noisiest, dirtiest, hottest part of the complex — and the part of the cycle most directly tied to feedstock arrival rhythms. **A smelting hall in active casting season runs continuously**, its workers rotating in and out on Guild-mandated schedules.

### Casting floors

Where heated metal becomes shaped form. **The casting floors are the Forge's most controlled environment** — temperatures are precisely managed through ward-channeling, work pace is dictated by the cooling rhythms of specific alloy types, and the Guild's most experienced casting masters supervise directly. **A full day on the casting floor would kill an unprotected mortal;** the wards make survival possible, the Guild's discipline makes sustained work practical.

### Finishing workshops

Where cast pieces become finished goods — grinding, polishing, edge-work, fitting, the assembly of multi-component constructs. **Less hot than the casting floors but no less skilled.** The realm's most senior Master Smiths often spend their later careers here, where the work demands precision over endurance.

### Ward-anchoring stations

Where enchantment is committed. **The Forge's most specialized work happens here** — Ward-Wrights tying specific enchantments to specific pieces under conditions that combine the heat-channel infrastructure of the Forge platform with the specific magical procedures the enchantment requires. **Most ward-anchoring work is conducted in the presence of representatives from the commissioning institution** — a Dragon Guard wardwright observing armor commissions, a Conservatory enchanter observing rare-metal commissions — to verify the enchantment's commitment.

### Raw-material holding yards

Where the [[Cinderpit]] supply pipeline lands. Feedstock arriving from the [[Volcanic Highlands Trail]] enters the holding yards for sorting, weighing, and quality verification before being released to the smelting halls. **The yards are the Forge's working interface with the rest of the realm's economy** — and a fixture of the Master of the Causeway's daily coordination.

## The heat source

**The Forge's working temperature is fundamentally not under mortal control.** The volcanic system that runs beneath [[Zarkharath]] provides the base heat; the lava bed at the city's center is the surface manifestation of a much deeper thermal reality; the Forge's platform is built directly into that thermal field. **The wards channel and contain; they do not generate.**

[[Zarkeros the Inferno]]'s breath is the second heat source. **The dragon's contribution is direct.** The Lair across the causeway is positioned so that on the rare occasions Zarkeros breathes for the Forge — for specific high-importance commissions, for ceremonial moments, for the rare casting that requires fire above what the volcanic system alone provides — his fire reaches the Forge directly across the lava bed. **This is one of the city's most genuinely sacred phenomena.** The casting floor halts other work when it happens. The Guildmaster is present. The Master Smiths working the affected pieces are at full attention.

Such castings are not common. They are noted when they occur.

## Coordination with the Guild

**The [[Infernal Forge|Forge]] does not operate independently of the [[Infernal Forge Guild]];** the Guild *is* the Forge's operational structure. Guild membership defines who works here. Guild rotation schedules define when. Guild quality standards define what counts as a finished piece. The [[Infernal Forge Guildmaster|Guildmaster]] runs the Forge in [[Zarkeros the Inferno|Zarkeros]]'s name and serves as his operational deputy for the platform's daily life.

This means the Forge complex and the Guild's Administrative Complex (at [[Zarkharath]]'s outer edge) are **functionally one institution operating across two physical sites**. Commissioning agents arriving in [[Zarkharath]] meet first at the Administrative Complex; production work happens at the Forge platform; ward-anchoring brings commissioning representatives to the platform itself for specific pieces.

## Access

Forge platform access is **strictly controlled**. The platform's ward-protection makes work possible but does not make casual presence safe.

Access categories:

- **Worker access** — Guild members on rotation, working their shifts under Master Smith supervision. The Forge's primary population.
- **Guild-sanctioned tours** — formal walkthroughs of the casting floors and finishing workshops for visiting institutional representatives, distinguished apprentice candidates, or scholars with research clearance. **Brief, escorted, calibrated to keep visitors safe.**
- **Audience-related transits** — petitioners crossing the platform on their way to or from the causeway and [[Zarkeros the Inferno|Zarkeros]]'s Lair. Coordinated through the Master of the Causeway.
- **Commissioning observation** — representatives from buying institutions present for ward-anchoring or other commission-specific work. Standard for high-value pieces.

**Casual visitor access is not available.** The Forge is the realm's most economically important production site and one of its most genuinely hazardous environments. The Guild does not balance these two facts against curiosity-driven approach.

[[Dragon Guard]] presence is continuous on and around the platform — coordinated through the [[Zarkharath]] liaison station, calibrated to the Forge's strategic importance.

## Character

The Forge is **working space, not ceremonial space**. Despite the magnitude of what happens here — the realm's institutional armament, [[Zarkeros the Inferno|Zarkeros]]'s direct patronage, the volcanic system's continuous gift of heat — **the atmosphere is practical and disciplined**. Workers do their work. Master Smiths supervise theirs. The Guildmaster runs his Guild. The Forge produces what the realm needs because the people here do that, every day, in conditions that would kill an unprotected stranger.

**Pride is present but not displayed.** Forge workers know what they make and who depends on it. The pride lives in the work, not in announcements about it. A visiting commissioning agent who treats the Forge with respect is received well; a visitor who treats it as spectacle finds reception polite but truncated.

The Forge's continuity is its own statement. **The platform has run continuously through every season of [[Zarkharath]]'s history, since the city was founded around it.** The wards are maintained. The lava bed is contained. The work continues. **The realm's institutional armament rests on this continuity, and the Forge is unembarrassed about that fact.**
