# Volcanic Highlands Trail

The realm's primary industrial supply corridor, **running eastward from [[Zarkharath]]'s [[Infernal Forge]] causeway to [[Cinderpit]]'s mining operations through the open volcanic highlands**. The trail is wider and more heavily built than most of the realm's routes — a constant flow of loaded ore and coal wagons westbound, empty haulers and provisioning supplies eastbound, and the specialized thermal-gear caravans that service the deep-shaft operations all demand the infrastructure. The terrain is unforgiving: dark volcanic stone underfoot, sparse vegetation, ambient heat from active vents along the route, and the particular ashy horizon that marks all northeastern volcanic highlands travel. **Drakari-heavy hauler crews work the trail continuously.** A traveler unaccustomed to the thermal environment finds the journey exhausting; experienced travelers simply find it ordinary.

## Scene texture

The Volcanic Highlands Trail departs [[Zarkharath]] at the eastern end of the [[Infernal Forge]] causeway, where the city's stone infrastructure gives way to open highland terrain. **The trail surface is engineered** — the volcanic environment is too unforgiving for ordinary earth-road construction. The trail's working surface is built from quarried volcanic stone, laid in overlapping courses over a foundation of coarser fill, graded to drain the occasional storm runoff rather than hold it. **The result is a surface that wagons can work continuously without the muddy degradation that affects the realm's other roads during wet seasons.** This engineering is expensive; it is justified by the trail's economic importance.

### Terrain

Travelers leaving [[Zarkharath]] see the terrain change within the first mile. The city's mixed architecture — stone buildings with functional timber elements — gives way entirely to the highlands' spare geology. **Dark volcanic stone in every direction.** Sparse vegetation clinging to broken ground: low hardy shrubs, tough grasses adapted to thermal soil, the occasional stunted tree in sheltered pockets. **The horizon is open and wide**, giving the trail the distinctive visual character of highland travel where a traveler can see their destination for hours before reaching it.

The terrain rises gradually eastward. [[Zarkharath]] sits at the lower edge of the volcanic highlands where the causeway meets the basin's approach; [[Cinderpit]] is higher, situated on the broad mountain shoulder where its extraction operations can access both surface coal seams and deeper ore veins. **The trail's total ascent over its length is meaningful — roughly eight hundred feet of elevation gain — though the grade is distributed gradually enough that the climb is steady rather than sharp.**

### Thermal features

The trail's distinctive environmental signature.

- **Heat vents** emerge along the route at irregular intervals. A vent is a fissure or shallow pit where thermal activity from the volcanic system below reaches the surface — sometimes as a plume of visible steam, sometimes as mere heat radiating from bare stone. **Active vents are marked with iron warning posts**, and where necessary the trail routes around them rather than through them. **Most vents are stable features that have existed for generations;** occasionally a new vent opens, and the trail crews reroute around it when this happens.
- **Old lava channels** — dry stone channels where ancient flows once ran — cross the trail in multiple places. The trail crosses them on stone-bridge construction. **A traveler crossing one sees dark stone walls dropping several feet below the trail surface**, often with mineral deposits along the channel floors that the highlands crews occasionally work for specialty materials.
- **Atmospheric haze** is continuous along the trail. The volcanic system's constant low-level activity produces a slight haze that the prevailing winds carry across the highlands. The haze is not thick enough to obscure vision meaningfully but **gives the light its ashy quality throughout the day**. Midday sunlight is filtered; evening light turns rust-gold rather than ordinary gold; clear days still carry the particular sky-tint that distinguishes northeastern volcanic highlands travel from travel elsewhere in the realm.

### Sounds

The sounds of the trail are practical and continuous. Wagon wheels on stone surface — **a rhythmic rumble distinct from dirt-road travel**. Hauler teams calling to their animals. The occasional whistle-signal between wagon drivers. The low hiss of steam from active vents. The wind, which is nearly constant in the highlands and **carries its own character — drier, warmer, with the mineral smell that volcanic environments produce**.

## Travel conditions

The full trail length — [[Zarkharath]] to [[Cinderpit]] — is **approximately one day's travel with empty wagons, or a day and a half with loaded haulers**. The trail's grade and the thermal environment make for tiring work; hauler teams rotate shifts at the midpoint rather than push through. **Typical traveler footspeed is slower than on the [[Silverwood Trail]] despite the gentler grade** — the heat and the wind combine to reduce pace.

Travelers carry highlands-appropriate supplies: **extra water (critical in the dry thermal environment)**, thermal-shielding gear for midday travel during peak summer, cold-weather gear for winter crossings (the highlands cool sharply at night), and the standard road supplies for any multi-day journey.

### Midpoint waystation

The trail's midpoint hosts a built waystation — **more substantial than the [[Silverwood Trail]]'s waysides because the highland environment demands it**. The waystation provides:

- a covered shelter against thermal radiation and wind
- a water tank maintained from a nearby stable spring
- a cleared area for teams to rest
- fodder stores
- basic repair tools for wagon and equipment issues

**Maintained jointly by [[Zarkharath]]'s [[Infernal Forge Guild|Forge Guild]] and [[Cinderpit]]'s Pit Master's office.** Permanent staff rotate through in multi-week shifts. The waystation is not an inn but **travelers with genuine need can overnight there**; the rotating staff handles hospitality informally for those who require it.

### Thermal-shelter points

In addition to the midpoint waystation, several smaller thermal-shelter points exist along the trail. **These are simple covered structures — low stone shelters with thick walls and metal-shielded openings — positioned near active heat vents or in stretches where ambient thermal radiation is particularly intense.** Travelers use them during midday peak heat; hauler teams sometimes use them for brief cooling stops when working animals begin to show thermal stress.

### Water stops

The dry highland environment makes water critical. **Three natural springs along the route are developed as water stops** — simple stone basins fed continuously, with shaded rest areas adjacent. **Local tradition holds that travelers do not rush through water stops;** thermal stress builds quickly in the highlands, and skipping a water stop is how travelers get hurt.

### Seasons

- **Summer** — peak operational season despite the heat. Trail surface firm and dry, wagon teams move efficiently, daylight long.
- **Winter** — harder but still active; the highlands cool sharply overnight and can see brief snow at higher elevations, but the thermal activity keeps the trail itself largely clear.
- **Spring/autumn** — most comfortable for travelers but bring more variable conditions. Occasional storms moving off the mountains, temperature swings that require flexible gear.

## Functions

- **Primary feedstock route westbound.** The trail's largest-volume flow: **coal, ore, and specialty metals moving westbound from [[Cinderpit]] to [[Zarkharath]]'s [[Infernal Forge]]**. Continuous during operational seasons. Loaded wagons are the trail's most common sight.
- **Provisioning flow eastbound.** Food, manufactured goods, equipment, and supplies moving eastbound from [[Zarkharath]]'s infrastructure to [[Cinderpit]]'s village. Steady but lower volume than the westbound feedstock flow.
- **Specialized caravans.** Specialty metal shipments bound for the [[Arcane Conservatory]] or other institutional buyers travel the trail under specific escort arrangements. These caravans are small but high-value.
- **Crew rotation.** Hauler teams, mining crews, and Forge workers rotate between the two settlements regularly — **many families have members in both communities**, and the trail carries continuous personal travel alongside commercial traffic.
- **Emergency response route.** When incidents occur at [[Cinderpit]]'s deep shafts (cave-ins, thermal events, serious injuries) requiring [[Zarkharath]]'s more substantial medical or technical resources, response teams move along the trail rapidly.

## Character

The trail has a distinctive **working character** that distinguishes it from the realm's other routes. Regular travelers — hauler crews, rotation workers, commissioning customers — treat the trail as **infrastructure rather than as travel**. The journey is part of the workday for most who use it; it is not scenic tourism or recreational movement. **This gives the trail a businesslike, steady atmosphere that visitors notice.**

**Drakari are disproportionately represented on the trail.** Hauler crews are often drakari-majority because the thermal environment rewards heat tolerance; mining rotation workers are drakari-heavy because [[Cinderpit]]'s demographic skews that way; [[Infernal Forge|Forge]] workers crossing to [[Cinderpit]] for joint operations are drakari-heavy for similar reasons. **A traveler on the trail will meet more drakari here than on any realm route other than [[Zarkharath]]'s internal streets.**

Courtesy among trail travelers is **direct and practical**:

- **Loaded wagons have right of way over empty ones** (the reverse of the [[Silverwood Trail]]'s southbound-favoring convention, and for obvious reasons: loaded teams cannot easily stop or restart)
- Travelers pulling off for rest signal oncoming traffic with a double-whistle
- Strangers at water stops exchange brief greetings about road conditions and move on; **the trail's practical environment shapes a working courtesy rather than a social one**

The trail is not unfriendly. **It is simply focused.** Travelers looking for conversation find it at [[Zarkharath]]'s or [[Cinderpit]]'s taverns after arrival; travelers looking for quiet find the trail accommodates them.

## Access

The trail is **open to all travelers**. Trailheads at both ends are marked and unrestricted. Casual travelers are welcome but uncommon — the highlands environment is demanding enough that few visit without purpose.

[[Dragon Guard]] patrols pass along the trail on rotations shared with [[Zarkharath]]'s garrison. **A patrol presence is routine but not constant;** the trail's civic safety is high, and patrols exist for coordination and observation rather than for active security.

[[Wardens]] are not resident along the trail. **Rare anomaly-response operations involving unusual volcanic activity or deep-shaft phenomena** may move along the trail, but this is irregular.

**The highlands beyond the trail proper are genuinely hazardous.** Travelers wandering off the maintained route into the open highland wilderness face real risk — thermal hazards, unstable ground, the rare encounter with highland predators adapted to the thermal environment. **The trail's infrastructure is calibrated to ordinary travel; excursions beyond it require specialist knowledge and equipment.**
