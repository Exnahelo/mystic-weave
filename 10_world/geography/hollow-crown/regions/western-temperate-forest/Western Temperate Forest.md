# Western Temperate Forest

The largest western surface woodland of the [[Hollow Crown]], stretching from the basin's western edge outward toward the [[Feywood]] threshold. The region is old-growth temperate forest in its interior, deep silverwood stands in its central reaches, and progressively Fey-touched terrain along its western edge where the ordinary forest softens toward the [[Feywood]] proper. The [[Western Temperate Forest|forest]] holds the realm's western trade capital, [[Dracelune]], and supplies the [[Hollow Crown]] with timber, hunted goods, and the specialized Fey-adjacent flora that grows only in this liminal zone. It is the realm's primary trade-facing region and its principal staging ground for any movement into or out of the Feywood.

## Geographic Role

The Western Temperate Forest occupies the basin's western surface — bounded on its eastern side by the [[Central Draconic Grasslands]] and the agricultural belt around [[Ashfield Fields]], on its northern side by the rising approaches to the [[Inner Ramparts]] and beyond them the [[Alpine Peaks]], on its southern side by the wetland transition zone of the [[Southwestern Mystic Wetlands]], and on its western side by the [[Feywood]] threshold itself. Within these bounds, the forest's character varies considerably:

- **Old-growth temperate forest** dominates the central and eastern reaches — oak, beech, pine, the familiar species of the realm's surface woodland.
- **Silverwood stands** form the region's defining middle territory — dense groves of the silver-barked trees that give [[Silvercut]] its working economy and [[Silverwood Trail]] its name.
- **Fey-adjacent zones** along the western reaches, where the ordinary forest gradually softens toward the [[Feywood]] threshold and where the specialized flora of [[Dewhollow]] grows.

The terrain is rolling rather than mountainous. Streams and braided waterways cross the region; the [[Dracelune|Dracé]] river runs through the southwestern reaches and gives [[Dracelune]] its name and commercial position. The [[Silverwood Trail]] is the region's principal artery, running northward from [[Dracelune]] through the central silverwood stands to [[Silvercut]].
