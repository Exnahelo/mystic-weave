# The Lantern and Mule

A practical traveler's inn in [[Dracelune]] known for decent stew, fair prices, and tolerance for road animals so long as their handlers keep order. Teamsters, carriers, foresters, and messengers favor it over grander rooms with thinner patience.

A dependable inn for road traffic and first-night arrivals in [[Dracelune]]. [[Hob Denner]] is the primary anchor NPC and should remain so unless a coordinated continuity update expands the staff or adds a distinct stableyard node.
