# Southwestern Mystic Wetlands

The realm's primary drainage region beyond the [[Southern Lake]]. The southwestern mystic wetlands are the low basin where the [[Hollow Crown]]'s waters gather, slow, spread, and disappear into subterranean outflow channels. **Fog, marsh, floating vegetation, liminal pools, and disorienting magical phenomena define the region.** The Hollow Crown does not flood because the wetlands' deep outflow carries water through the mountain below the basin floor; this hidden hydrology is what makes the wetlands the realm's hydrological terminus rather than a perpetual inland sea.

## What the region is

The wetlands occupy the southwestern interior of the basin, bounded by the temperate forest's western edge and the [[Inner Ramparts]] to the south. **The region holds three authored entities:**

- **[[Mirefall]]** — the stilt-city built across raised platforms and high-ground islands in the wetland's eastern reaches. The realm's primary processing and export center for wetland-origin goods (rare herbs, restorative compounds, specialty specimens). Civic anchor for the broader region.
- **[[Brackenmoor]]** — the rare-specimen gathering village set at the transition zone where the western temperate forest gives way to the wetlands' upper reaches. Brackenmoor's residents wild-harvest the rare herbs, mosses, fungi, and small specimens that downstream Mirefall refines.
- **[[Valley Edge Overlook]]** — a high-vantage landmark at the wetland's upper transition, where the alpine descent meets the wetland basin proper. Connected to the [[Misty Descent]] above.

Beyond these named places: **wild wetland**. Bogs, fen edges, deeper marsh, the standing pools that form where drainage gathers without finding outflow.

## Hydrological function

The wetlands are the realm's drainage terminus. Sacred waters descending from the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]]'s southern face feed the [[Southern Lake]]; from there, streams, distributaries, and subsurface drainage move toward the wetlands. Within the wetlands, water spreads, slows, and is gradually carried into hidden subterranean channels that pass through the mountain below the basin floor before reaching whatever exits the realm has at lower elevations.

**No obvious surface river exits the Hollow Crown.** The wetlands are why this works. The basin does not flood because the deep outflow runs continuously, taking water faster than the wetlands accumulate it across most seasons. **This is hidden hydrology — the realm's water leaves through paths no surface traveler can follow.**

## The transition biome

[[Brackenmoor]]'s working terrain is the **transition biome** — the narrow ecological band where temperate forest hardwood ecology meets wetland water-saturated ecology. Certain species grow only here. Certain conditions occur only here. **The realm's rarest medicinal mosses, alchemical fungi, and specimen materials come from this band.** Brackenmoor's residents specialize across three functional zones: forest margin, fen, and deeper pools.

The transition biome's economic significance is disproportionate to its geographic size. The [[Arcane Conservatory]], the [[Hall of Scales]], and the realm's institutional alchemists all depend on it.

## Magical character

Beyond [[Mirefall]] and [[Brackenmoor]]'s working zones, the wetlands carry **disorienting magical phenomena** that the realm has not fully catalogued and does not particularly attempt to. Travelers reporting unusual experiences in the deeper marsh — directional confusion that compass and map cannot correct, channels that lead somewhere different on the return trip, shifts in the sounds of wildlife and water — are reliably believed by residents and reliably ignored by realm institutions until something specific demands attention.

The wetland's atmospheric influence reaches **upslope into the [[Alpine Peaks]]** at the [[Misty Descent]] — the warm, foggy, disorienting transition zone above [[Valley Edge Overlook]] is canonically the wetlands' character bleeding into the mountain's outer slopes.

**The wetlands are not corrupted in the [[Shadowed Hollows]]' or [[Rift of Discord|Rift]]'s sense.** The phenomena here are older, more diffuse, and arguably benign — the wetlands disorient travelers but rarely harm them, and the local communities have developed practical accommodations rather than institutional response. **The boatmen know which channels to take. The gatherers know which paths to follow. The deeper marsh is left to itself.**

## Civic presence

Civic authority in the wetlands is **informal and locally rooted**. Two offices anchor it:

- **The [[Hallkeeper of Mirefall]]** — Mirefall's senior civic authority
- **The [[Fenwarden of Brackenmoor]]** — Brackenmoor's senior civic authority

Both are **Tier-3 generative roles** — institutional positions with deep canonical character but specific incumbents that scenes can instantiate as needed.

[[Dragon Guard]] presence is minimal. A small liaison station at [[Mirefall]]'s threshold village handles regional coordination. [[Wardens]] are functionally absent except during rare anomaly-response operations involving unusual specimen behavior or the deeper marsh's occasional unsettled phenomena.

## Travel and access

The region is reached from the central realm via the **Wetlands Drainage Road**, descending southwestward from [[Drakenvale City]] through the grasslands, into the wetland's transition edge at [[Brackenmoor]], and continuing south to [[Mirefall]] proper. Beyond [[Mirefall]], travel is by flat-bottomed boat or guided poling-craft.

[[Valley Edge Overlook]] sits at the wetland's upper transition where the [[Misty Descent]] from [[Alpine Peaks]] meets the wetland basin proper. It is a vantage landmark rather than a settled place.

A connection runs east from [[Mirefall]] to [[Greymantle]] in the [[Southern Dark Quadrant]] — the only practical west-east transit at the basin's southern margin. A connection runs west from [[Mirefall]] toward [[Dracélune]] — the wetland's primary trade interface with the western temperate forest's commercial settlements.

## Character

The wetlands' atmosphere is **quiet, unhurried, and accommodating to slowness**. Both settlements run at a pace shaped by water rather than by clock. **Visitors accustomed to other regions' rhythms find the wetlands either welcoming or maddening, with little middle ground.**

The region attracts a small but persistent flow of people seeking quieter life — retired scholars, meditation-inclined practitioners, artists who want the wetland's light and solitude. **The wetlands absorb such residents without comment.**

The wetlands are **not isolated, but they are peripheral**. Realm institutions reach here when they need to and otherwise let the communities run themselves. **This works for everyone involved.**
