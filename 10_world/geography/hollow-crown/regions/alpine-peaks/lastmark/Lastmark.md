# Lastmark

**The realm's border fort and ward-controlled entry point**, carved directly into the mountain face at the [[Hollow Crown]]'s high approach within the [[Alpine Peaks]]. Walls cut from the living stone rise in tiered courses above the outer ramparts, **and the single sanctioned road into the kingdom passes beneath arched wardstones that no one crosses unsanctioned**. Lastmark is military first, civic second — the [[Dragon Guard]] garrisons heavily here, the [[Wardens]] coordinate anomaly response from here, and the [[Silver Scale Trading Company|SSTC]]'s inbound caravans are processed through the fort's customs halls before descending into the realm proper. **Its population is working and disciplined, its pace is steady, and its walls have never been taken.**

Lastmark sits at **the realm's only viable mountain pass** — the [[Alpine Pass]] crossing through the outer crater rim. **There is essentially one way into the [[Hollow Crown]] physically, and it passes through here.** This singular geography shapes everything about the fort's strategic position and explains [[Eryndor the Radiant|Eryndor]]'s nearby [[Eryndors Lair|Lair]] placement.

## Scene texture

Lastmark is built into the mountain itself. **The walls are not built walls — they are carved walls**, shaped from the living stone of the [[Alpine Peaks]] in tiered courses that rise from the outer approach road to the upper garrison levels. The effect, from outside, is **of the mountain itself having decided to present a fortress face to anyone approaching**. The structural joints are minimal. The architecture is austere, functional, and visibly unyielding.

### Approach

The approach from outside the realm is **a winding mountain road that climbs through broken alpine terrain for several days' travel from the nearest external waypoint**. The road is not hidden exactly — anyone with local guidance can follow it — but it is difficult. Switchbacks, unstable scree zones, weather that turns without warning, and a gradient that punishes wheeled transport. [[Silver Scale Trading Company|SSTC]] caravans time their crossings carefully. **A caravan arriving at the Lastmark outer gates has already earned the passage.**

### The outer gates

The outer gates are **the realm's actual threshold**. Two massive stone slabs carved in the same idiom as the walls, set into an arched opening **beneath a pair of standing wardstones**. The wardstones are the operational front of the realm's wider ward system — **the magical barrier that renders the [[Hollow Crown]] inaccessible to unsanctioned approach**.

A caravan, messenger, or sanctioned visitor passing under the wardstones is recognized by the ward structure and admitted. **An unsanctioned approach simply does not arrive;** the path loops, the traveler becomes lost, the mountain deflects them without their ever knowing they were turned away.

### Interior

Past the outer gates, the fort's interior **opens upward through the mountain**:

- **Customs halls come first** — wide stone chambers where arriving caravans are processed, goods inspected, credentials verified, and the formal acknowledgments of realm-entry are recorded
- **The commercial district** — merchants and provisioners supporting the caravan traffic and the garrison, inns for travelers staying a night before the further descent, a handful of better taverns that soldiers and Ward-Keepers share with caravan crews
- **The garrison proper** — higher in the mountain: barracks, drill yards carved into the mountain's interior, armories, the command structure, and the senior officer quarters

### Population

The population is working. **Soldiers form the largest group** — [[Dragon Guard]] regulars garrisoned for standard rotations, [[Wardens|Warden]] anomaly-response specialists, and the specialized **Ward-Keeper corps** that maintains the wardstone system. Civilian residents are fewer: the merchants and provisioners, the permanent staff of the customs halls, the families of the senior garrison who have made Lastmark home, and the small professional class (engineers, healers, stonemasons) the fort requires.

**Discipline is visible at every level.** Lastmark does not have loud public gatherings; the soldiers' rest days are spent at the taverns in moderate volume, the merchants open on a schedule, **and even the children are noticeably quieter than children elsewhere**.

### The view

The view from the upper levels is **spectacular**. On clear days, a visitor standing at the uppermost garrison observation platform can see outward across the mountain ranges beyond the realm — terrain whose external geography is beyond the realm's ordinary scope — **and inward, downward, across the [[Inner Ramparts]] descent toward the distant glimpse of the [[Hollow Crown]]'s central basin**. **Lastmark is the realm's high threshold in altitude as well as function.**

## The supply elevator

A distinctive piece of Lastmark's infrastructure is the **supply elevator** — a pulley-driven lifting platform running down the mountain's outer face from the fort's cargo level to a staging depot near the outer road. **The elevator is not a casual convenience.** It is a government-and-military asset, primarily used for:

- **Bulk supply movement** — [[Silver Scale Trading Company|SSTC]] inbound goods that do not need to go up through the inner customs halls on carts can be loaded at the outer staging depot and lifted directly to the fort
- **Military equipment** — weapon shipments, siege gear, the [[Infernal Forge]]'s heavier commissions for the garrison, armor resupply
- **Troop movement** — when rapid deployment is required, units can be lifted or lowered in platform groups rather than taking the long road
- **Occasional personal use** — rarely, a senior dignitary, an elderly diplomat, or a departing official uses the elevator for personal descent or ascent when the road is impractical. **This is exception, not routine.**

The elevator is engineered, ward-reinforced, and **staffed continuously**. It is a source of professional pride for Lastmark's engineering corps. **Travelers passing through Lastmark for the first time almost always ask about it.** Locals are happy to explain it. **Non-residents are not typically permitted to use it without specific authorization.**

## Functions

- **Ward-controlled entry.** Lastmark is **the single sanctioned entry point into the [[Hollow Crown]]**. The wardstones at the outer gates are the operational front of the realm's ward system; **all external contact that enters the realm physically does so here**. This is the realm's most significant security infrastructure.
- **[[Silver Scale Trading Company|SSTC]] customs and receiving.** SSTC inbound caravans are processed at Lastmark's customs halls. Goods are inventoried, credentials verified, and the official record of realm-entry is created here. **Outbound caravans receive their final inspection and departure clearance at the same halls.**
- **[[Dragon Guard]] garrison.** Lastmark hosts **the realm's largest standing [[Dragon Guard]] garrison outside the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale|Stronghold]]**. The fort is the realm's primary defensive anchor at the ward threshold and the staging point for any military response to external incursion.
- **[[Wardens|Warden]] coordination.** [[Wardens]] maintain a substantial presence focused on **external-origin anomaly response** — threats, incursions, or magical irregularities that originate outside the realm and reach the ward threshold. **This mission is distinct from the internal [[Wardens|Warden]] work conducted elsewhere.**
- **Diplomatic receiving.** Foreign diplomats, sanctioned messengers, and external officials arriving under diplomatic protocol are received at Lastmark and either escorted directly to [[Eryndors Lair|Eryndor's Lair]] (for [[Eryndor the Radiant|Eryndor]]-handled matters) or conducted under controlled convoy into the realm for further [[Draconic Council|Council]]-level reception.
- **Supply chain interface.** The military and civic supply chains connecting the external world to the realm run through Lastmark. The elevator, the cargo handling infrastructure, and the coordination with [[Stonemark]] and other upstream settlements all originate here.

## Senior leadership

### The Commandant

**The Commandant of Lastmark** is the fort's senior military authority — command of the [[Dragon Guard]] garrison, operational responsibility for the ward infrastructure, and the senior civic authority over Lastmark's population. **Typically a senior [[Dragon Guard]] officer with decades of service**, appointed by the [[Draconic Council|Council]] upon recommendation from the [[Dragon Guard]] leadership. Tenure is indefinite but reviewable.

The Commandant reports upward to the [[Draconic Council|Council]] for strategic matters and **coordinates laterally with [[Eryndor the Radiant|Eryndor]] directly on diplomatic affairs**, the [[Wardens|Warden]] leadership on anomaly response, and the [[Silver Scale Trading Company|SSTC]] senior authorities on trade logistics.

### The Chief Ward-Keeper

The **Chief Ward-Keeper** heads the specialized Ward-Keeper corps responsible for the wardstone system's maintenance and operational integrity. **This is the realm's most consequential security role at the infrastructure level** — the wardstones are what keeps the realm hidden and the external threat contained, **and their failure would be catastrophic**.

The Ward-Keepers are drawn from the [[Arcane Conservatory]]'s graduates and the [[Dragon Guard]]'s wardwork specialists. **Their training is multi-year, their standards are exacting, and their work is continuous.** A Ward-Keeper is never off-duty in any real sense; the wardstone system requires constant low-level attention and rapid specialist response if any irregularity is detected.

The Chief Ward-Keeper is typically a **senior specialist with both [[Arcane Conservatory|Conservatory]] credentials and [[Dragon Guard]] operational experience**.

## Lethira Vale's presence

**[[Lethira Vale]]** — the [[Silver Wing Envoys|Silver Wing]]–linked administrative and transport coordinator from [[Drakenvale City]] — maintains regular travel to Lastmark in her official capacity. **She coordinates with the Commandant on diplomatic matters**, handles the administrative interface for sanctioned external contact, and manages the practical coordination that connects [[Drakenvale City]]'s Administrative Quarter to Lastmark's customs operations. She is not resident here but is **a recurring canonical figure**; a character arriving at Lastmark on diplomatic or administrative business will frequently encounter her.

## Eryndor's presence

[[Eryndor the Radiant]] maintains his primary residence at **[[Eryndors Lair|Eryndor's Lair]]**, located near Lastmark. **His proximity to the fort is strategically deliberate.** For Lastmark's purposes, Eryndor's presence means that:

- The fort has a [[Draconic Council|Council]] dragon within rapid response range
- Diplomatic matters can be handled without delay
- The realm's moral and judicial authority is represented at the border even when the full [[Draconic Council|Council]] is convened elsewhere

## Access

Lastmark is accessible to **sanctioned travelers** through the outer gates. Realm residents with legitimate business ([[Silver Scale Trading Company|SSTC]] crews, military personnel on rotation, diplomatic staff, senior officials, and the occasional authorized visitor) pass in and out routinely. Foreign visitors arrive through the ward-sanctioned approach and are processed at the customs halls before any further access is granted.

**Unsanctioned approach does not reach Lastmark.** The [[Wardline Threshold]] outside the fort is the realm's first defense — its deterrence turns back unauthorized travelers before they reach the gates. **For sanctioned travelers who do reach the gates, the wardstones are the operational threshold, and they have never been breached.** Internal travel from Lastmark down into the realm is unrestricted for residents; inbound caravans follow the main road southeastward through the [[Inner Ramparts]] toward [[Stonemark]], and from there into the central basin.

The elevator is restricted. **Authorized personnel and freight movements only.** Travelers asking to use it are politely redirected to the road.
