# Alpine Pass

A high mountain pass cutting through the outer ring of peaks that encircle [[Drakenvale City|Drakenvale]]. **The air is thin and bitterly cold.** Glacial crystals embedded in the cliff faces glint with pale blue light — **ice magic infused into the rock itself, forming passive wards against fire-based intrusion**. The path is narrow and exposed, with sheer drops on both sides. **Ice drakes and frost wyverns patrol the skies overhead**, territorial creatures that tolerate no trespass lightly.

## Character

The Alpine Pass is **the realm's only viable mountain crossing**. It cuts through the [[Alpine Peaks]] outer ring at an elevation where weather is variable, atmosphere is thin, and the surrounding terrain is genuinely hostile. **Travelers who reach the Pass have already endured significant alpine approach;** the Pass itself is the final geographic barrier before descent into the realm's wardline-protected zone.

### Physical character

The Pass is **narrow and exposed**:

- **Sheer drops on both sides** — the path runs along a ridge cut between deeper alpine drops
- **No shelter from wind** — the prevailing wind funnels through the Pass at strength
- **Glacial crystal cliff faces** — pale blue formations embedded in the rock, **passive wards against fire-based intrusion** (a feature the realm's founders did not place but did not remove)
- **Bitter cold** — alpine cold at high elevation, with thin air making heat retention difficult

### Wildlife

- **Ice drakes** patrol the upper airspace — territorial, intolerant of trespass
- **Frost wyverns** share the airspace and similar disposition
- **Winter wolves** occasionally cross at the Pass's lower margins during seasonal movement
- **Smaller cold-adapted fauna** at the rocky margins

## Function

- **Mountain crossing.** The Pass is the realm's **only viable physical crossing** through the [[Alpine Peaks|outer ring]]. All approaches to [[Drakenvale City|Drakenvale]] from external regions cross here.
- **Passive ward integration.** The glacial crystals' fire-resistance combines with the realm's wardwork at the [[Wardline Threshold]] beyond to layer the realm's defensive geography.
- **Wildlife territory.** Ice drakes and frost wyverns are not part of the realm's institutional defense, but their territorial behavior contributes to the Pass's natural hostility to casual approach.

## Travel and access

Travelers approaching the realm reach the Pass after multi-day external alpine travel from the nearest outside waypoints. **Crossing the Pass itself takes most of a day in good conditions.** Bad weather can extend this significantly or make the Pass impassable until the weather clears.

After the Pass, travelers descend toward the [[Glacial Stream Crossing]] at the [[Alpine Peaks|alpine]]-[[Inner Ramparts|rampart]] transition, then through the rampart belt down toward [[Lastmark]] (institutional entry) or — theoretically — through the [[Wardline Threshold]] and [[Misty Descent]] toward the wetland-side descent (a route the wardline makes unusable for unsanctioned travelers).

**The Pass itself is not policed by the realm** — it sits outside the [[Wardline Threshold]], and the [[Dragon Guard]] does not patrol this far out. **The wildlife and the weather provide natural deterrence.**
