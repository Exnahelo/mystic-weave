# Grasslands Edge Post

A frontier trade-and-watch post sitting on the [[Draconic Grasslands Edge]] corridor where the basin's eastward routes funnel toward the [[Northeastern Volcanic Highlands]]. The Post is a small fixed installation — a watch station, a modest trade depot, and the lodging and stable infrastructure that supports caravans moving between the central realm and the eastern volcanic territory. It is not a town. Its population is the rotating staff who keep it running: a small [[Dragon Guard]] detachment, a handful of [[Silver Scale Trading Company]] factors and stewards, and the trade and stable workers who handle through-traffic.
