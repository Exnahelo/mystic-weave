# Draconic Grasslands Edge

The eastern outflow of the [[Central Draconic Grasslands]] basin — the broad corridor where the grasslands' open terrain meets the rising approaches to the [[Northeastern Volcanic Highlands]] and the routes leading outward toward [[Zarkharath]]. The Edge is not a destination in itself but a transit corridor: roads cross it, caravans move through it, and the realm's eastward movement is funneled through its width. Specific scenes set on the Edge typically involve travel, encounter, or the [[Grasslands Edge Post]] watch station that anchors the corridor's principal junction.
