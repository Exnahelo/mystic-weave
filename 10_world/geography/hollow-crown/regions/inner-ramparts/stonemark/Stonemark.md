# Stonemark

The realm's principal quarry village, set in the [[Inner Ramparts]]' broken foothill terrain below [[Lastmark]] **where the escarpments descend toward the central basin**. Stonemark's residents work the quarries that have supplied the realm's construction economy since the sanctuary's founding — pale [[Heartmass]]-related stone from the high cuts, working-grade granite from the mid-slopes, and the specialized carving stone the finer masons favor. **The village is built around the quarries rather than beside them**, its streets following the contours the extraction left, its buildings constructed largely from the waste stone the commercial cuts did not want. Residents are practical, weathered, **and proud of the stone that has raised half the realm's civic architecture**.

## Scene texture

Stonemark occupies the transition zone where the [[Inner Ramparts]]' steep escarpments soften into the basin's approach terrain. The village sits in a broad bowl carved by centuries of quarrying — **the quarries came first, and the village grew up around them**, taking shape in the spaces the extraction left. This origin is visible everywhere. **Streets curve along the lips of worked pits rather than following straight lines.** Buildings are set at varying elevations, connected by stone stairs and ramps. The central square is **a leveled floor of pale stone that was once a working face** and is now the village's civic anchor.

### Approaches

The approach from [[Lastmark]] follows the main road southward down the [[Inner Ramparts]] escarpments. **The grade is steep in places** — wagons move slowly, and loaded stone-hauling rigs require additional teams to manage the descent safely. Travelers arriving from Lastmark pass through several smaller quarry operations on the upper slopes before reaching Stonemark proper. **These smaller cuts are mostly worked out now, their faces weathered and their access roads disused;** they are monuments to the village's long working history.

The approach from the central basin climbs upward through gentler terrain. **The road from [[Drakenvale City]] connects to the lowland approach road**, which then rises to Stonemark through a series of broad switchbacks. **This is the route the stone travels** — cut blocks loaded at Stonemark's yards, hauled down to the basin for delivery to civic construction projects, guild workshops, and the realm's steady demand for building material.

### Three districts

The village proper spreads across the bowl in three functional districts:

- **The Working District** — closest to the active quarries. Stone yards, cutting sheds, the primary masons' workshops, the hauler teams' stables, the smithies that shape and maintain quarrying equipment. **Loud during working hours.** Dusted white with stone particulate that the rain washes off daily and the cutting work replaces.
- **The Central Square and Commercial District** — the village's civic and commercial core. The common hall (stone-built, naturally, **with pale [[Heartmass]]-related blocks forming the facade**), the **Chisel and Wedge** (the inn), three taverns, the general merchants, the healer's practice, and the administrative offices of the Quarrymaster and Cutting Warden.
- **The Residential Slopes** — housing spread up the gentler eastern slope where the ground is stable enough for permanent construction. **Senior masons and quarrymaster families in larger stone homes closer to the center**, working families in more modest houses further up the slope, newer residents in the outer ring that is still slowly growing.

### The village's sound and sight

The village's distinctive sound is **the rhythmic strike of hammer on chisel** — sustained, coordinated, present during all working hours from the active quarry faces. **The sound is the village's heartbeat.** Residents sleep through it. **Newcomers find it distracting for about a week, then find they cannot sleep without it.**

The village's distinctive sight is **the stone everywhere**. Buildings of stone. Walls of stone. Retaining structures of stone holding slopes stable. Stone tools, stone hearths, stone drinking fountains in the square fed by spring water that tastes faintly of minerals from the rock it passes through. **Residents consider wood decoration in a home vaguely suspect**, as though the owner is trying to pretend they live somewhere else.

## Functions

- **Primary quarrying operations.** Stonemark works **three main stone types**:
  - **Pale [[Heartmass]]-related stone** from the high cuts — the same stone the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale|Stronghold]] is built from, **though of lesser grade than the Stronghold's original carving**
  - **Working-grade granite** from the mid-slopes, used in most civic and domestic construction across the realm
  - **Specialized carving stone** (marble, softer limestone, specific veined varieties) from particular deposits the village's geologists have mapped and track carefully
- **Finished masonry.** Beyond raw stone, Stonemark produces finished masonry work — dressed blocks, architectural elements (columns, cornices, lintels), carved decorative work for civic commissions. **The village's senior masons handle commissions that would otherwise require traveling masters.**
- **Masonry apprenticeships.** The village hosts **the realm's primary masonry training program**. Apprentices come from across the realm to learn quarrying and stoneworking under Stonemark's senior masters. Training runs multiple years. Graduates carry Stonemark credentials and work throughout the realm as recognized masons.
- **Specialty cuts for institutional buyers.** The [[Arcane Conservatory]]'s ward-anchor stones, the [[Infernal Forge]]'s reinforcement blocks, the Administrative Quarter's archival-storage slabs, and similar institutional commissions require specific stone grades cut to exact specifications. **Stonemark handles these commissions through dedicated specialty teams.**
- **[[Crystal Caverns]]-adjacent geology.** The village's deeper quarry shafts have historically reached the upper levels of the [[Crystal Caverns]] network. **This adjacency produces two distinct economic effects:** occasional unexpected specimens when deep cuts expose crystal-bearing formations, and the ongoing surveying relationship with [[Deephollow]]'s Guild of Underdelvers who coordinate with Stonemark's deep-cut operations on geological boundary issues.

## The deep cuts

Stonemark's most distinctive feature is the **deep cuts** — a network of shafts and worked passages descending from the village's lower quarry faces into progressively deeper stone. **These shafts reach depths that connect canonically with the upper [[Crystal Caverns]] network.**

The deep cuts have their own operating protocols:

- Worked by **specialized crews trained for deep-quarry conditions**
- The air below certain depths requires ventilation work
- Light requirements grow substantial
- **Water seepage from the aquifers above the [[Crystal Caverns|Caverns]] creates ongoing drainage challenges**
- The crews that work the deep cuts are among Stonemark's most respected and most hazard-exposed workers

The deep cuts' connection to the [[Crystal Caverns]] is **not an open access route**. The quarrying work stops well before reaching navigable cavern terrain, and the boundaries are monitored by both Stonemark's crews and [[Deephollow]]'s Guild of Underdelvers. **But the adjacency is real.** Occasional deep-cut expansions expose crystal-bearing formations, and when this happens, **a joint protocol between Stonemark and Deephollow determines whether the formation is worked by Stonemark (for the stone) or handed to Deephollow (for the crystals)**. The coordination is professional and mutually respectful.

In rare cases, the deep cuts have encountered phenomena the regular quarrying does not expect — **unusual pressure pockets, subtle resonances from the crystal-bearing formations deeper in the system**, and once, during the Age of Harmony, a minor underground stream that carried trace sacred resonance from sources the village never fully traced. These occurrences are handled carefully, reported upward through the appropriate institutional channels, **and treated as work that requires respect**.

## Civic character

The village is run through the **Quarrymaster's** office. The Quarrymaster is the village's senior civic and operational authority — **overseeing active quarry operations, coordinating the commissioning pipeline, handling the interface with the Administrative Quarter in the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale|Stronghold]] and with institutional buyers**, and serving as the village's representative in realm-wide matters affecting stone supply. **Typically a senior mason with decades of experience**, selected from among the village's master-tier practitioners by a consensus process that takes account of both technical mastery and civic judgment.

The **Cutting Warden** is the village's specialist authority on the carving and finishing work that distinguishes finished masonry from raw stone. **Oversees the masters' workshops, trains apprentices in advanced technique, and handles the interface with customers commissioning specialty work.**

The village's social fabric is built around stone-working families. **Multi-generational lineages are the norm**, though the apprenticeship program means a steady flow of trained practitioners comes through. Apprentices who stay after training marry into established families or establish new working lines within the village's existing professional structure. **Mobility between Stonemark and the realm's other settlements happens regularly** — senior masons occasionally take positions in [[Drakenvale City]], [[Dracélune]], or elsewhere — but most trained practitioners return home to Stonemark eventually.

The village has a specific relationship with [[Lastmark]]. **Many Stonemark families have relations posted at [[Lastmark]]'s garrison.** The [[Dragon Guard]] presence at Lastmark draws steadily from the region's working population, and Stonemark contributes its share of sons and daughters to military service. **When [[Dragon Guard]] personnel have leave, many come home to Stonemark;** the village's taverns on such evenings have a particular character — working masons drinking with off-duty soldiers, stories flowing both ways, **the steady cross-cultural exchange of two working communities that know each other well**.

## Character

Stonemark is **direct, practical, and weathered**. Residents are accustomed to hard physical work, **visible calluses on every adult hand**, and the routine presence of dust that does not fully wash out. Humor runs dry. Conversation tends toward the specific — a particular cut, a particular client, a particular problem and how it was solved. **Abstract or philosophical discussion rarely starts at Stonemark but will sustain once started if the topic is grounded in something concrete.**

Hospitality is substantial. The inn is clean, the meals are hearty, and the taverns keep reasonable hours. **Travelers passing through are welcomed if they show basic respect for the work;** visitors who treat quarrying as a quaint backdrop find themselves receiving less than full welcome. **The village is proud of what it does, and that pride shapes how visitors are received.**

The village's relationship to the realm's civic architecture is **personal**. When a Stonemark mason walks through [[Drakenvale City]], they recognize individual blocks they or their ancestors cut. **The [[Stronghold of Drakenvale|Stronghold]]'s lower walls include stone that came from Stonemark's cuts.** The Hall of Scales was built partly from Stonemark's finest carving grade. The [[Arcane Conservatory]]'s ward-anchor elements were quarried here. **This recognition is not boastful** — residents rarely mention it unprompted — **but it is always present, a continuous sense of participation in the realm that other villages do not share in quite the same way**.

## Access

The village is open to travelers. The road from [[Lastmark]] brings traffic moving between the border fort and the basin. The road from [[Drakenvale City]] brings stone-hauling rigs, commissioning customers, and occasional institutional representatives on specialty-cut business. **The informal routes to [[Deephollow]]'s upper network pass near Stonemark's deep cuts but are not generally used by surface travelers.**

Accommodation is at the **Chisel and Wedge** or in the two small guesthouses operated by senior mason families. Rooms are plain, well-built (the village has capable carpenters and excellent masons), and inexpensive. Meals are generous and reliably good.

[[Dragon Guard]] presence is light — a small patrol station supports [[Lastmark]]'s operations and handles the road traffic between the two settlements. [[Wardens]] are effectively absent except during rare anomaly-response incidents in the deep cuts. Local order is handled by the Quarrymaster's office and the village's own social structure.

The active quarries and working faces are accessible to visitors with legitimate interest. **Casual tourism is politely discouraged** — the work is hazardous, and uninstructed visitors pose risks to themselves and to working crews. The deep cuts are **not accessible to casual visitors at all**; access requires specific business, crew escort, and appropriate safety preparation.
