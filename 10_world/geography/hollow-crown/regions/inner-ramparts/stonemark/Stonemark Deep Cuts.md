# Stonemark Deep Cuts

The deeper quarry excavations below [[Stonemark]]'s active operations — **where the stone cuts become tunnels and the tunnels occasionally break into crystal vein territory**. The Deep Cuts are not officially mapped beyond the last marked extraction point. **[[Deephollow]] claims jurisdiction over anything crystal-bearing; [[Stonemark]]'s Mining Authority claims everything within the [[Alpine Peaks]] foothills. The [[Draconic Council|Council]] has ruled twice and satisfied nobody.** Workers who push too deep sometimes do not come back, **which is attributed to unstable rock rather than examined carefully**.

## What the Deep Cuts are

The Deep Cuts begin where [[Stonemark]]'s **lowest active extraction faces** transition from open quarry working into worked-tunnel descent. The boundary is informal — there is no signed threshold, no Quarrymaster's gate marking "below this point." Crews working the lowest faces simply keep cutting, and what they cut becomes the Deep Cuts.

The tunnels descend irregularly. **Some shafts go straight down. Others slope; others zigzag through the stone following the working veins.** The further down, the less mapped the territory. **Beyond the last marked extraction point, official records do not exist.** What lies down there is known only to the crews who have worked it and the few who have ventured deeper without returning.

## The disputed jurisdiction

[[Stonemark]]'s **Mining Authority** asserts jurisdiction over all extraction within the [[Alpine Peaks]] foothills' geological zone — meaning, everything the Stonemark crews can reach by the methods of [[Stonemark]] quarrying. **By this claim, the Deep Cuts belong to [[Stonemark]] regardless of what they yield.**

[[Deephollow]]'s **Guild of Underdelvers** asserts jurisdiction over all crystal-bearing formations within the [[Crystal Caverns]] system — meaning, anything that yields crystals belongs to [[Deephollow]] regardless of who reaches it first. **By this claim, the Deep Cuts' deeper formations belong to [[Deephollow]] when they prove crystal-bearing.**

**Both claims are sincere. Both have institutional weight. The two jurisdictions overlap geographically in the Deep Cuts.**

The [[Draconic Council|Council]] has ruled on this matter twice in living memory. Both rulings attempted compromise: one designating specific zones to each party, another establishing joint extraction protocols. **Both rulings have been honored in form and contested in practice.** [[Stonemark]] crews continue to cut where the work takes them; [[Deephollow]] surveyors continue to assert claims when crystals appear; the joint protocol applies in theory and produces dispute in execution.

The Council does not seem inclined to rule a third time. **Both communities have learned to live with the ambiguity**, with the working result that the Deep Cuts operate under genuine uncertainty about which institution authorizes any given expansion.

## Hazards

The Deep Cuts are **dangerous in ways the upper quarries are not**:

- **Unstable rock.** The geology shifts with depth in unpredictable ways. Pressure pockets, sudden voids, fracture lines that the surface working methods cannot anticipate.
- **Unmapped territory.** Beyond the last extraction marker, crews work by line-of-sight and tactile knowledge. **Getting lost is possible.** Falling is possible. Encountering features no one has documented is routine.
- **Unaccounted disappearances.** Workers who push too deep sometimes do not come back. **The official explanation is unstable rock.** Whether that is the full explanation is not a question the surface community asks aloud.
- **Crystal-formation phenomena.** Where the Cuts break into crystal-bearing territory, **resonances and pressure variations occur** that the surface miners do not have full vocabulary for. [[Deephollow]] crews have more experience with these phenomena and routinely advise caution; [[Stonemark]] crews routinely proceed anyway.
- **Boundary disputes occasionally physical.** When the two communities' crews encounter each other deep in the Cuts, friction is possible. **Genuine violence has not occurred in living memory**, but tense exchanges and equipment standoffs have.

## Access

Deep Cuts access requires:

- **[[Stonemark]] crew membership** with deep-shaft training, OR
- **[[Deephollow]] Underdelvers Guild authorization** for cross-jurisdictional work, OR
- **[[Draconic Council|Council]]-sanctioned investigation** for formal inquiries (extremely rare)

**Casual visitors do not enter the Deep Cuts.** [[Stonemark]]'s upper tour-accessible quarries are well-marked and routinely visited; the Deep Cuts are not. **The transition from tour-accessible to working-only is enforced quietly but effectively** by the Quarrymaster's office.
