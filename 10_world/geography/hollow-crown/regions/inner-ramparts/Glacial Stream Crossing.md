# Glacial Stream Crossing

A stream fed by snowmelt from the peaks above, **spanned by a weathered stone bridge of ancient construction**. The water runs clear and painfully cold, carrying faint traces of latent magic downstream toward the valley. **Everfrost Moss carpets the rocks along the banks.** The bridge offers a natural rest point — sheltered enough to catch breath, exposed enough that nothing can approach unseen. Giant eagles circle high above, and winter wolf tracks mark the muddy banks.

## Character

The Crossing sits at the **alpine-rampart transit boundary**, where the [[Alpine Pass]] descent gives way to the [[Inner Ramparts]]' upper belt. The stone bridge predates the realm's recorded construction history; **the founders did not build it, and they did not record who did**. The bridge has held continuously since.

Travelers passing through experience the Crossing as a **threshold rest** — the cold air of the alpine descent thins here, the wind drops in the bridge's lee, and the stream's faint magic carries enough latent warmth that travelers can briefly thaw cold-stiffened hands without unhealthy temperature shock. **The water is drinkable, though painfully cold.** Locals advise warming it slightly before drinking when possible.

**Everfrost Moss** grows along the wet stone banks — a magical lichen that maintains its crystalline ice-form even in summer thaw. The moss is harvested rarely; the realm's herbalists use small quantities in cold-stable preparations, and the [[Arcane Conservatory]] occasionally commissions specimens for study. Reckless harvesting damages the bank ecology and is discouraged by [[Dragon Guard]] patrols when observed.

## Wildlife

- **Giant eagles** circle high overhead. The eagles are not generally hostile to travelers crossing the bridge — the Crossing is a known transit point and the eagles' territorial response is calibrated to crossings rather than incursions. **Travelers who linger or stray off the bridge into the eagles' immediate hunting zones may receive different attention.**
- **Winter wolves** leave tracks along the muddy banks. The wolves are seasonal — winter and early spring presence is common, summer absence is typical. Travelers crossing during winter conditions should expect to see tracks and may occasionally see wolves at distance.
- **Other alpine wildlife** moves through the area on its own rhythms. The Crossing is a chokepoint for animals as well as for travelers.

## Function

- **Rest point along the [[Alpine Pass]] descent.** Travelers coming from the alpine pause here before continuing into the rampart belt. **The bridge is the realm's most sheltered alpine-transit waypoint.**
- **Water source** for travelers carrying limited supplies through the alpine wilderness.
- **Specimen harvest site** for Everfrost Moss when commissioned.
