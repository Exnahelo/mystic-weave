# Inner Ramparts

A broken escarpment belt running between the [[Alpine Peaks]] and the lower basin regions: **cliff roads, ledges, old impact fractures, hanging shelves, falls, and narrow stable corridors**. The Inner Ramparts are not a single continuous wall — they are a transitional highland zone running around much of the basin interior, **belt-like but irregular**, containing some of the realm's most rugged terrain and the few stable settlements that the rampart geography permits.

## What the region is

The Inner Ramparts form **the basin's middle ring** — the broken zone between the [[Alpine Peaks]] crater rim (which holds the realm's only sanctioned entry at [[Lastmark]]) and the central basin's lower-elevation regions ([[Draconic Grasslands]], temperate forest, [[Southwestern Mystic Wetlands|wetlands]]). The rampart terrain is the **legacy of the meteor impact** that hollowed the mountain; the impact's stone-fracturing went deep, and the ramparts are where the basin's interior surface still shows the impact's age in cliffs, hanging valleys, old fracture lines, and the unstable shelves that geology has not yet smoothed into ordinary slope.

The region holds:

- **[[Stonemark]]** — the realm's principal quarry village, set in the rampart foothill terrain where the escarpments descend toward the central basin. Supplies the realm's construction economy with stone since the sanctuary's founding.
- **[[Stonemark Deep Cuts]]** — the deeper subterranean quarry network below Stonemark's surface operations, where cut tunnels reach toward [[Crystal Caverns]] territory. **Disputed jurisdiction; canonically dangerous.**
- **[[Glacial Stream Crossing]]** — alpine snowmelt stream spanned by an ancient stone bridge. Rest point along the rampart-to-alpine transit.
- **The rampart wilderness** — broken-shelf terrain between and beyond the named features. Not formally civic; not generally traversed.

## Rampart features

The Inner Ramparts contain a variety of geographic features beyond settled places:

- **Hidden watchpoints** — observation posts maintained by the [[Dragon Guard]] at key vantages along the rampart belt
- **Cliff roads** — narrow routes cut along sheer faces; usable but unforgiving
- **Old stone cuts** — abandoned quarrying sites and ancient excavations
- **Aeries and dragon ledges** — open-air landing and resting places that the realm's draconic population uses
- **Cave mouths** — access points into deeper systems; some lead to the [[Crystal Caverns]] network
- **Waterfalls and basin-feeding streams** — the rampart's contribution to the basin's hydrological system; melt and rainfall from the [[Alpine Peaks]] descends through the ramparts before reaching the basin floor
- **Scattered minor settlements** — beyond [[Stonemark]], small unauthored holdings exist in the more stable rampart sections; these are not formally civic and are typically too small to anchor independent narrative

## Civic presence

Civic presence in the Inner Ramparts is **concentrated at [[Stonemark]]** and effectively absent elsewhere. [[Stonemark]]'s Quarrymaster's office handles civic and operational authority for the quarry village. Beyond [[Stonemark]], **the rampart belt is patrolled lightly** — [[Dragon Guard]] rotations cover the connecting roads and the more accessible terrain, with deeper rampart wilderness left to itself.

[[Wardens]] are not resident in the region. Anomaly response in the rampart wilderness — rare events involving unstable geology, the [[Stonemark Deep Cuts|Deep Cuts]]' deeper irregularities, or unusual phenomena — is coordinated through [[Stonemark]] when it occurs.

## Travel and access

The **Alpine Approach** descends from [[Lastmark]] (in the [[Alpine Peaks]] above) through the Inner Ramparts to [[Stonemark]] and continues down to [[Drakenvale City]]. **The grade is steep at the upper descent**, manageable through [[Stonemark]], and ordinary from [[Stonemark]] to the basin floor.

[[Glacial Stream Crossing]] sits along the rampart-alpine transit, providing a marked rest point for travelers moving between the alpine wilderness and the rampart belt. The bridge there is ancient stone construction.

[[Stonemark Deep Cuts]]' deeper shafts reach the upper [[Crystal Caverns]] network; **this is not an open access route** but creates ongoing coordination between [[Stonemark]]'s crews and [[Deephollow]]'s Guild of Underdelvers on geological boundary matters. The Deep Cuts are formally disputed jurisdiction; the [[Draconic Council|Council]] has ruled twice without resolving the matter to anyone's satisfaction.

## Character

The Inner Ramparts' atmosphere is **serious, weathered, and shaped by elevation**. [[Stonemark]] runs on disciplined craft. The rampart wilderness between and beyond is not unfriendly but **not casual**; the terrain demands competence, the weather changes without warning, and the realm's ordinary civic comforts do not extend here.

Travelers crossing the Alpine Approach experience the rampart character cumulatively. **The descent from [[Lastmark]] takes most of a day** before reaching [[Stonemark]], passing through old quarry sites, switchbacks across hanging ledges, the steady visual reminder that the basin is interior to a broken mountain. **Travelers reaching [[Stonemark]] and continuing onward toward the basin floor often comment on the relief of leaving the rampart geography behind**, even though the rampart routes are well-maintained and not particularly dangerous in ordinary conditions.

The rampart region carries the realm's **architectural memory** with particular concentration. [[Stonemark]]'s quarries have supplied half the realm's civic buildings; the older stone cuts and abandoned workings throughout the belt represent generations of effort that no longer have continuing function. **Walking the ramparts is, in a sense, walking through what the realm has been using to build itself for centuries.**
