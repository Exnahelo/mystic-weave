# Drakenvale

> A hidden sanctuary within the [[Hollow Crown]], governed by the [[Codex of Remembrance]] — justice, balance, and wisdom — where dragons of many callings coexist alongside drakari, kobolds, and mortals.

## What this note is

Drakenvale is the **name of the realm/sanctuary as a whole** — its political identity, its cultural identity, its civic order. It is not the same thing as the metropolitan settlement (see [[Drakenvale City]]) or the central fortress (see [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]]).

In casual speech and most source documents, "Drakenvale" can mean the realm, the city, or the fortress depending on context. This note holds the realm-level meaning. When wikilinks or prose mean the city specifically, they should point to [[Drakenvale City]]; when they mean the fortress, [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]].

## The realm

Drakenvale is the political-civic-spiritual entity that occupies the [[Hollow Crown]]. It exists because dragons who once destroyed civilizations in the [[War of the Fallen]] chose to build something else instead — a hidden order where coexistence is maintained not by trust alone, but by law, ritual, memory, and constant vigilance.

The sanctuary is governed by the [[Draconic Council]] — three ancient dragons ruling by consensus. The legal-moral framework is the [[Codex of Remembrance]]. The [[Draconic Conclave]] gathers all resident dragons every century or so to debate amendments to the Codex.

[[Drakari Stewards]] serve as civic officers, mediators, military leaders, and direct enforcers of Council rulings. Mortals may advise the Council and hold skilled roles but do not possess a Council vote. Kobolds are protected under the Codex and integrated into education, labor, and sanctuary life — they are essential to maintenance, logistics, wardcraft, mining, and engineering.

## Sovereign Allies

The [[Feywood]] is the elven realm bordering the [[Hollow Crown]]'s western temperate forest, governed by its own Council of Elders. The Feywood is not part of Drakenvale; it is a sovereign-equal ally bound to the sanctuary by the [[Oath of the Fallen]] since the founding. Day-to-day relations are conducted at the Feywood Border and at the elven outer settlement of [[Vaelmere]], which handles routine trade and diplomatic contact. Council-level matters route to [[Thornveil]] through formal channels.

## Citizenship and belonging

Drakenvale places heavy moral weight on belonging and obligation. Residents are expected to make the realm better through service, labor, craft, scholarship, vigilance, cultivation, protection, or governance.

Citizenship exists in two layers:

- **Basic citizenship** — acquired by birth within the realm or through formally granted acceptance. Carries the protections and expectations of ordinary belonging.
- **Standing citizenship** — earned through service, discipline, office, or meaningful contribution. Required for the highest levels of trust, institutional authority, and the halls of power.

Leaving the [[Hollow Crown]] without approval is treated as betrayal because the sanctuary's secrecy is foundational.

## Social order

Drakenvale is hierarchical but not caste-segregated:

- **Dragons** — elder-protector nobility; founders, guardians, exemplars
- **[[Drakari]]** — naturally high standing as civic inheritors of draconic culture
- **Mortals** — integrated into civic life, may rise to positions of serious responsibility
- **Kobolds** — protected, respected, indispensable in engineering, maintenance, logistics, mining, wardcraft

All dragons stand equal under the [[Codex of Remembrance]]. The legacy of the [[Discordant War]] leaves an unspoken layer of caution and distrust around questions of loyalty, especially in matters touching the [[Temple of Mordrax]], secrecy, or old fault lines within the realm. This distrust is cultural rather than legal.

Kinship in Drakenvale is shaped as much by mentorship, service, oath, and shared work as by bloodline. Social identity is deeply tied to what one contributes and how one carries oneself.

## Religion

[[Solveris]] is the primary sacred anchor — justice, mercy, law, protection. The [[Platinum Heart]] is his sanctum, watched over by the Platinum Flame. [[Mordrax]] is forbidden but theologically unavoidable; her sealed Temple stands as a warning against unchecked ambition and corruption.

Spiritual life is maintained primarily by the [[Platinum Acolytes]]. The [[Vigil]] preserves the deeper Solveris tradition under the public face of Platinum Oath Monastery.

## Conflict resolution

Through mediation in the [[Amethyst Vault]], the [[Trial of Wings]] (a ritualized non-lethal duel reformed during the [[Age of Harmony]]), or structured community justice in the [[Draconic Hall]]. Severe cases may invoke divination, magical truth-binding, or direct Council judgment.
