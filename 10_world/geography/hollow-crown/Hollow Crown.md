# Hollow Crown

The canonical realm root for the Drakenvale interior basin.

## Overview

The Hollow Crown is the hidden interior basin that contains the sanctuary of Drakenvale. It was created by the impact of [[The Cataclysm]] — the meteor that broke against the world, struck a mountain of unimaginable scale, and shattered its summit, hollowing the interior and casting ruin across the upper heights. The outer mountain endured as a ring of jagged alpine heights, broken ridges, glacial walls, and unstable escarpments that conceal the basin from the world beyond. From outside, the Hollow Crown appears to be only an impossible mountain. Inside, a living realm.

## Geography

At the basin's center stands the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]], carved from the [[Heartmass]] — the colossal formation of fused celestial stone and platinum-veined mountain heartrock that lodged in the broken core when the meteor struck. The Stronghold is what the Heartmass became; no separate Heartmass formation exists today.

[[Drakenvale City]] occupies the southern face of the Stronghold and curves along the north shore of the [[Southern Lake]] in tiered stone districts. Beyond the city, the basin spreads outward in regions of distinct character.

## Magical / sacred character

The basin is saturated by draconic presence and old magic. The land is rich in platinum, gemstones, magical flora, and deep mineral resonance. The weather of the basin is said to mirror the emotional state of its dragons — calm mists in times of peace, powerful storms in times of unrest.

In one dark quadrant the land is wounded. The [[Shadowed Hollows]] spread above the buried [[Temple of Mordrax]], where necrotic seepage twists the forest and darkens the living earth. Adjacent to it lies the [[Rift of Discord]], a separate scar left not by the meteor but by the [[Discordant War]] that nearly shattered the sanctuary from within.
