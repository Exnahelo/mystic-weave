# Drakenvale City

The realm's principal metropolitan center, built onto the southern face of the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] and curving along the north shore of the [[Southern Lake]]. The city rises from the lakefront in tiered stone districts, its upper terraces blending into the Stronghold's lower approaches and its lower districts spreading along the water in market streets, plazas, workshops, and residential quarters. Species-mixed by default, civically ordered under the [[Codex of Remembrance]], and carrying the daily texture of an active high-functioning capital — guilds working, markets trading, [[Wardens]] patrolling, [[Dragon Guard]] overhead, and the [[Sacred Pools]] cascading down the Stronghold's face as the city's defining visual landmark.

## Note

In casual speech "Drakenvale" can refer to the realm, the city, or the Stronghold depending on context. This note is the metropolitan settlement specifically. See [[Drakenvale]] for the realm/sanctuary as a whole, and [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] for the central fortress.

## Scene texture

Drakenvale City occupies the shoreline crescent where the Stronghold meets the [[Southern Lake]]. The city is built *into* the landscape rather than on top of it — streets follow the natural contours of the basin's southern slope, buildings rise from the same pale [[Heartmass]] stone as the fortress above, and the lakefront curves in a long gentle arc punctuated by piers, promenades, and the broad stone plaza where the ceremonial path to the [[Sacred Pools]] begins its ascent.

A visitor arriving for the first time takes in the scale before the detail. The Stronghold rises behind and above the city in a single overwhelming presence, its carved towers and ramparts dominating the sky. The [[Sacred Pools]] cascade down its southern face in a continuous shimmer of water catching light on platinum-veined stone. The city spreads along the lake in concentric tiers, each tier set slightly higher than the one seaward of it, stone roads and worked staircases connecting them. Drakenvale does not sprawl. It rises.

Streets are busy but not chaotic. The [[Dragon Guard]] maintains patrols on the major thoroughfares and the lakefront; [[Wardens]] handle internal security and anomaly response in the less-visible parts of civic life. Crime exists, but open lawlessness is unusual — the city is too well-monitored and too civically invested for it to flourish publicly. What persists does so quietly, behind trade fronts and in the lower residential warrens near the lake's western edge.

The population is mixed by default. [[Drakari]], [[Humans]], [[Elves]] (rare), kobolds, [[Vulkar]], [[Dwarves]], [[Halflings]], and halfbreeds work alongside each other without enclave patterns. Species-specific districts do not exist in formal terms, though certain trades cluster along ethnic and cultural lines. Kobolds tend to work the [[Infernal Forge]] supply chains and underground logistics. Vulkars disproportionately fill certain scholarly and hospitality roles. Elves are rare but present, typically long-residing.

The city carries music almost continuously. The [[Sapphire Choir]] maintains performance spaces throughout — formal halls, open-air stages in the plazas, small intimate venues in residential districts.

## Districts

- **The Stronghold Terraces** — uppermost districts, where the city meets the fortress. Administrative and civic-adjacent residences, upscale guildhalls, and the formal ascending path to the Administrative Quarter. Higher rent, higher status, direct access to the Stronghold's services.
- **The Middle Tiers** — primary market and workshop districts. Most residents live and work here. The three great market plazas sit here. The [[Circle of Artisans]]' main civic hall is here. Guild activity is densest.
- **The Lakefront** — shoreline districts from the ceremonial plaza at the base of the [[Sacred Pools]] around to the western curve. Piers, fish markets, boat workshops, dockside inns, and the [[Silver Scale Trading Company]]'s main warehousing. The liveliest and most trade-focused part of the city.
- **The Lower Warrens** — older, denser residential quarter along the western lakefront. Working residents, long-established families, and the city's more marginal populations. Not a slum — the Codex's civic discipline prevents that — but the part of the city where underworld activity persists most visibly. [[Wardens]] patrol here with patience rather than aggression.
- **The Promenade** — ceremonial lakefront walk extending from the base of the [[Sacred Pools]] around the city's full arc. Public gardens, formal monuments, scheduled [[Sapphire Choir]] performance spaces. Residents walk here on festival evenings and rest days.

These are described as scene texture rather than navigable sub-locations. They are not separate location nodes.

## Markets

Three great plazas:

- **Lakefront Market** — fish, grain, imported goods. Loud, crowded, fast.
- **Guild Market** — craft and service. Rhythmic and professional, with journeymen moving between workshop and stall.
- **Upper Market** — finer goods, arcane supplies, items commissioned through the [[Circle of Artisans]]. Quieter, more deliberate.

## Institutional presence

The city hosts the physical presence of multiple realm-wide institutions:

- [[Silver Scale Trading Company]] — principal external trade body. Lakefront warehousing and administrative offices. [[Kaerys Emberclaw]] is the senior authority; [[Tazrik Flameweaver]] manages operational logistics.
- [[Circle of Artisans]] — civic-cultural craft body. Main hall in the middle tiers.
- [[Sapphire Choir]] — cultural institution maintaining oral tradition, music, ceremonial performance. Multiple performance spaces; administrative HQ in the upper tiers.
- [[Dragon Guard]] — visible patrol presence throughout. Local barracks in the Middle Tiers and Lakefront.
- [[Wardens]] — internal security presence, less visible but continuous. Warden stations in each major district.
- [[Platinum Acolytes]] — civic presence operates from a small temple in the upper tiers, coordinating with senior Acolytes at the [[Sacred Pools]] and [[Platinum Heart]].
- [[Infernal Forge]] supply chains — Zarkharath's metalwork output moves through city warehouses before distribution to guilds and the Dragon Guard.

Two institutions are *not* in Drakenvale City: the [[Hall of Scales]] (its own settlement, overseen by [[Ardrynn the Measured]]) and the [[Arcane Conservatory]] (inside the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] proper).

## Anchor NPCs

- [[Kaerys Emberclaw]] — senior [[Silver Scale Trading Company]] leader. Operates from the Lakefront. Principal interface for trade, commerce, external economic matters.
- [[Lethira Vale]] — [[Silver Wing Envoys]]–linked administrative and transport coordinator. Quieter operator, effective at routes, permissions, threshold politics.
- **Lead Artisan of the Circle** — senior civic craft authority, Tier-3 generative role.
- **Director of the Sapphire Choir** — senior cultural authority, Tier-3 generative role.

[[Tazrik Flameweaver]], Kaerys's operational deputy, is present in the city but is more often on the road with caravans.

## Access

The city is fully open. Residents and registered visitors move freely. External visitors arriving via trade caravan or escorted travel are processed at the Lakefront or the Administrative Quarter depending on their business. Unauthorized entry to the realm is theoretically impossible due to ward-controlled access at [[Lastmark]]; once a visitor is in the realm, Drakenvale City is where they most likely arrive first.
