# Ardrynn the Measured

## Overview

Ancient dragon scholar and overseer of the [[Hall of Scales]]. Ardrynn is an ancient dragon of Lawful Neutral bearing, respected across the sanctuary for her even-handedness and her refusal to favor any philosophical faction within the academic body. She has overseen the [[Hall of Scales]] for centuries and is considered one of the realm's quieter powers — not a [[Draconic Council]] dragon, but a pillar of the civic-intellectual order.

## Fields embodied

As an ancient dragon, Ardrynn naturally commands three magical fields:

- **Sacred**
- **Alchemy**
- **Druidry**

Her field combination overlaps with three different sitting Council members — Sacred with [[Eryndor the Radiant]], Alchemy with [[Zarkeros the Inferno]], Druidry with [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze]]. This positions her as a peer to each of them in one specific field, but allied to none of them in totality. The combination is institutionally significant: she can speak with authority across the philosophical divisions the Council represents.

## Nature and temperament

Her scales carry the faint green-bronze patina of long indoor life and unhurried decisions. She is said to take her meals in the [[Hall of Scales]]'s open atrium, where students may approach her with questions if they are brave enough. Most are not.

She is approachable but not easily moved. Her measured nature is a genuine trait, not a diplomatic affectation.

## Position

- Overseer of the [[Hall of Scales]] — broad academy and university-level scholarship
- Holds quiet but substantial influence across academic, civic, and intellectual matters
- Not a [[Draconic Council]] member, but recognized as a pillar of the civic-intellectual order

The [[Arcane Conservatory]] operates one tier above her institutional reach. The Conservatory is overseen by [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze]] and handles elite advanced magical study; the [[Hall of Scales]] handles broad higher learning and foundational academic work.

## Role in play

Academic politics, institutional arbitration, scholarly investigation, research gatekeeping at the foundational tier.

## Relationships

- [[Hall of Scales]] — her institution, overseen for centuries
- [[Arcane Conservatory]] — the institution one tier above her reach; coordinated relationship with [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze]]
- [[Eryndor the Radiant]] — peer in Sacred field; mutual respect
- [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] — peer in Alchemy field; cooler relationship
- [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze]] — peer in Druidry field; institutional adjacency
