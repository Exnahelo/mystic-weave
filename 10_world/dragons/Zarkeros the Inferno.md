# Zarkeros the Inferno

## Overview

Ancient dragon. Council seat of strength, defense, and resource power. Zarkeros governs from a position of force, clarity, and practical consequence. He respects [[Drakenvale]], but not sentimentally. To him, the sanctuary survives because it is defended, provisioned, disciplined, and feared when necessary. He places little value in weakness disguised as compassion.

## Fields embodied

As an ancient dragon, Zarkeros naturally commands three magical fields:

- **Elemental**
- **Alchemy**
- **Runecraft**

His manifestations align with his domain: elemental power as raw force and breath, alchemy as transformation in service of strategic production, runecraft as the binding logic of fortification and ward-anchored defense. The [[Volcanic Highlands]] and forge-linked infrastructure carry his magical signature.

## Nature and temperament

He is blunt, dangerous, and often easier to understand than those who attempt to sound kinder than they are. His pragmatism is not cruelty — it is the discipline of someone who has watched sanctuaries fail and decided his own would not. He is not without honor; he is without softness.

## Position

- Council seat of strength, defense, and resource power
- Commands the [[Dragon Guard]]
- Commands the [[Wardens]] in crisis (peacetime command is [[Eryndor the Radiant]]'s)
- Heavy influence over strategic production through the [[Volcanic Highlands]] and the [[Infernal Forge]]
- Aligned with the [[Infernal Forge Guild]] in matters of metallurgy and weapon-craft

## Role in play

Military action, deterrence, logistics, defense, strength politics, strategic resource questions, crisis command.

## Relationships

- [[Eryndor the Radiant]] — Council peer; the natural counterweight. Where Eryndor wants power justified by service, Zarkeros wants service justified by survival.
- [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze]] — Council peer; respects Varethyn's strategic foresight, dismisses what he sees as excessive subtlety.

## History

Founding member of the current [[Draconic Council]]. His position consolidated after the [[Discordant War]], when the Council's military posture was reorganized to prevent another internal rupture. The lessons of that war shape his governance — he has seen what happens when the sanctuary is unprepared from within.

## Open questions

- Whether his dismissal of "soft" approaches has political consequences he doesn't see
