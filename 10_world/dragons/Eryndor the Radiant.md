# Eryndor the Radiant

## Overview

Ancient dragon. Council seat of justice, diplomacy, and moral authority. Eryndor is the most approachable of the three [[Draconic Council]] dragons and the one most likely to engage seriously with questions of law, mercy, public duty, redemption, and moral consequence. He believes [[Drakenvale]]'s ideals are worth the cost of maintaining them, and that power must justify itself through protection, restraint, and service.

## Fields embodied

As an ancient dragon, Eryndor naturally commands three magical fields:

- **Sacred**
- **Warding**
- **Binding**

Each dragon's manifestation of an embodied field is unique. Eryndor's specific manifestations align with his role as moral authority — sacred power expressed as judgment and protection, warding expressed as the maintenance of the [[Hollow Crown]]'s ancient defenses, binding expressed as oath-keeping and the enforcement of the [[Codex of Remembrance]].

## Nature and temperament

He commands deep respect among [[Wardens]], [[Platinum Acolytes]], and those who see the sanctuary primarily as a moral project rather than merely a strategic one. He is patient, historically minded, and capable of real warmth, but disappointment from him carries unusual force. When angered, he becomes colder rather than louder.

## Position

- Council seat of justice, diplomacy, and moral guidance
- Commands the [[Wardens]] in peacetime
- Closely associated with the [[Platinum Acolytes]]
- Holds influence over matters of redemption, formal judgment, and high diplomacy

## Role in play

Justice, petitions, sacred authority, moral disputes, matters of redemption, high diplomacy, formal judgment.

## Relationships

- [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] — Council peer; tension between moral idealism and pragmatic force
- [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze]] — Council peer; respects Varethyn's perspective but prefers more direct engagement

## History

Founding member of the current [[Draconic Council]]. His association with [[Solveris]] and the [[Platinum Heart]] traces back to the post-[[Discordant War]] reconstruction.
