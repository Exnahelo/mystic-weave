# Solveris

## Overview

Solveris is among the oldest known deities of the planet — the cosmic principle of preservation, continuity, and form. He is one half of a primal polarity whose other half is [[Mordrax]], and the conflict between them is not a struggle of good against evil but the fundamental dynamic by which the world holds and changes. Mortals across [[Drakenvale]] interpret Solveris as the deity of justice, mercy, law, and protection — and that interpretation is real and binding for civic life — but it is interpretation. What Solveris is, beneath the mortal frame, is the pole that holds.

The dragons worshipped Solveris before the [[Cataclysm]]. Mortal faith on the planet, where it developed at all, descended from this older draconic understanding. Drakenvale's pantheon is the oldest known religion of the planet.

## Domains

The mortal-civic domains commonly attached to Solveris in [[Drakenvale]]:

- Justice
- Mercy
- Law
- Protection

These are mortal-civic interpretations of the deeper principle. At the cosmic level, Solveris is the pole of preservation, continuity, and form — the holding-together against dissolution. Mortal religion translates that into the moral terms civic order requires.

## Origin and scope

The cult of Solveris is not a religion of the sanctuary's founding generation. It is older than the realm by an unknown depth of time. The dragons of the world recognized Solveris and [[Mordrax]] as the cosmic poles long before the [[Cataclysm]] reshaped the planet, and the polarity is held to be the dragons' first religious understanding. Mortal civilizations that grew alongside dragons absorbed this understanding naturally — dragons themselves were deity-tier figures to mortals, and offered no other faith.

Solveris-worship is therefore not specific to Drakenvale. Drakenvale is the most concentrated and theologically articulated expression of the older draconic-derived faith currently known. Where this religion persists outside the [[Hollow Crown]], specific forms, names, and emphases vary by culture. Other faiths also exist on the planet, distinct from this lineage. The [[Elves]] are the principal example currently established in canon, but the absence of established others should not be read as their non-existence — Drakenvale knows what Drakenvale knows, and the wider world is largely undocumented.

The names "Solveris" and "Mordrax" themselves are of unknown origin. No one knows where they came from. The dragons have always called them by these names, and so have the mortals who learned the religion from dragons, and so does every record old enough to mention the deities at all. A religion whose own naming-history is lost is older than any preserved record.

## Worship

Within [[Drakenvale]], spiritual life is maintained by the [[Platinum Acolytes]], a religious order aligned with Solveris's principle as the realm receives it and closely associated with [[Eryndor the Radiant]]'s moral authority. The [[Platinum Heart]], watched over by the Platinum Flame, is the sanctuary's primary site of Solveris devotion.

Seasonal ceremonies, atonement rites, sacred observances at the [[Sacred Pools]] and Platinum Heart, and the [[Trial of Wings]] reinforce Solveris's place in the realm's spiritual identity. Other peaceful faiths consistent with the [[Codex of Remembrance]] may be tolerated, but none rival the symbolic centrality of Solveris in Drakenvale's public religious life.

## Mythology

In the modern era, the [[Platinum Warden]] is the figure most associated with Solveris's defining acts: founding the sanctuary, drafting the [[Oath of the Fallen]], and giving his life to seal the [[Temple of Mordrax]] during the [[Discordant War]]. The Warden was devoted to Solveris in life. After his death the sanctuary's Solveris-aligned institutions — the [[Platinum Acolytes]], the Platinum Flame, [[The Vigil]] — formed around his memory and the moral architecture he gave the realm.

The Warden's elevation must be read correctly. He was not a manifestation of Solveris; he was a devotee whose life-and-death act became foundational for the sanctuary's understanding of what Solveris's principle requires of mortal political life. The deity is older than the Warden by an unknown depth of time; the Warden's significance is in what he made of the deity for [[Drakenvale]].

## Clergy and orders

- [[Platinum Acolytes]] — the primary religious order
- The Platinum Flame — the sacred fire watched at the [[Platinum Heart]]
- [[The Vigil]] — a closely-related order maintaining inner liturgy and memorial inscriptions

## Iconography

Associated with platinum, white-and-silver light, the Platinum Flame, and the dragon-form of the [[Platinum Warden]]. Solveris devotion is common across the sanctuary and does not map cleanly to any single draconic lineage or embodiment.

Outside the [[Hollow Crown]], where draconic-derived religion persists, iconography varies by culture. No single representation of Solveris is canonical at planetary scale.

## Connections

- [[Mordrax]] — cosmic counterpart; the pole of dissolution opposite Solveris's pole of preservation
- [[Platinum Heart]] — primary site of Solveris devotion in [[Drakenvale]]

- [[Codex of Remembrance]] — the legal-moral architecture of the sanctuary, oriented around Solveris's principle as the realm receives it

## Open questions

Most cosmological-status questions move into different shape under the framing above: Solveris is among the oldest known deities of the planet, recognized by dragons before the [[Cataclysm]], articulated in mortal civic life through Drakenvale's pantheon. What remains:

- Whether Solveris is "a being with intent" or "a structural principle" or something stranger — a question theologians ask and the [[Codex of Remembrance]] does not require an answer to
- Whether Solveris and [[Mordrax]] are genuinely two distinct deities or two faces of a single underlying polarity. Liturgical convention treats them as two; the cosmological frame as a single dynamic expressed bilaterally. The Codex does not require an answer; some theologians treat the question itself as the religion's deepest mystery
- The exact relationship between Solveris-worship in [[Drakenvale]] and the older worldwide draconic-derived faiths — whether Solveris is named the same elsewhere, whether the domains are interpreted similarly, whether the polarity is articulated in the same way
- Whether the [[Elves]] who have come to know Solveris recognize anything of their own celestial framework in the principle, or whether the religions are genuinely distinct in object as well as in form
