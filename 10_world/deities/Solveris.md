# Solveris

## Overview

Solveris is the primary sacred anchor of [[Drakenvale]] — the deity of justice, mercy, law, and protection. The [[Platinum Heart]], the highest and holiest sanctum of the sanctuary, is his. He is the moral foundation around which the [[Codex of Remembrance]] orients, and the figure most associated with the sanctuary's founding ideals.

## Domains

- Justice
- Mercy
- Law
- Protection

## Worship

Spiritual life in Drakenvale is maintained by the [[Platinum Acolytes]], a religious order aligned with Solveris's ideals and closely associated with [[Eryndor the Radiant]]'s moral authority. The Platinum Heart, watched over by the Platinum Flame, is the sanctuary's primary site of Solveris devotion.

Seasonal ceremonies, atonement rites, sacred observances at the [[Sacred Pools]] and Platinum Heart, and the [[Trial of Wings]] reinforce Solveris's place in the realm's spiritual identity. Other peaceful faiths consistent with the Codex may be tolerated, but none rival the symbolic centrality of Solveris in Drakenvale's public religious life.

## Mythology

Solveris was the deity to whom the [[Platinum Warden]] was devoted. The Warden's leadership in founding the sanctuary, his sacrifice in closing the shaft of the [[Dark Hold]] during the [[Discordant War]], and his role as principal architect of the Oath all stand as Solveris's defining acts in the modern era.

## Clergy and orders

- [[Platinum Acolytes]] — the primary religious order
- The Platinum Flame — the sacred fire watched at the [[Platinum Heart]]
- [[The Vigil]] — a closely-related order maintaining inner liturgy and memorial inscriptions

## Iconography

Associated with platinum, white-and-silver light, the Platinum Flame, and the dragon-form of the [[Platinum Warden]]. Solveris devotion is common across the sanctuary and does not map cleanly to any single draconic lineage or embodiment.
