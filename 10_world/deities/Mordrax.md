# Mordrax

## Overview

Mordrax is the forbidden but theologically unavoidable figure in [[Drakenvale]]'s spiritual order. Her sealed [[Temple of Mordrax]], buried beneath the [[Shadowed Hollows]], stands as a permanent warning against unchecked ambition and corruption. She is not worshipped within the sanctuary — invocation, devotion, or organized pursuit of her power is forbidden under the [[Codex of Remembrance]] — but she cannot be erased from the realm's theology because her temple predates the [[Oath of the Fallen]] itself.

## Domains

- Corruption
- Ambition unbound
- Power pursued without restraint
- That which the [[Codex of Remembrance]] exists to refuse

## Worship

Forbidden. Mordrax has no sanctioned clergy, no sanctioned rites, no sanctioned shrines. Active devotion is treated as a threat to the sanctuary and is among the gravest offenses under the Codex — the only category for which the Codex permits execution by unanimous Council approval.

What persists is illicit and underground:

- [[Mordrax Cults]] — small, recurring, hard to extinguish. They surface in different forms across centuries.
- The [[Mordraxian Rebels]] — the historical faction that pursued her power openly during the [[Discordant War]]. Their attempt to unseal her temple via the [[Dark Hold]] cost the [[Platinum Warden]] his life.

## Mythology

Mordrax is theologically positioned as the dark counterweight to [[Solveris]]. Where Solveris embodies justice tempered by mercy, Mordrax embodies ambition unconstrained by either. The two are not formally framed as siblings, lovers, or rivals in canonical liturgy — the relationship is left philosophical rather than mythological — but functionally, the moral architecture of Drakenvale is built around the gap between them.

The temple itself is older than the sanctuary. The founders did not build it; they inherited it. The early Council debated destroying it; in the end, the [[Oath of the Fallen]]'s final founding act was to seal it under collective draconic warding, treating it as a wound to be contained rather than a problem to be solved.

## Iconography

Suppressed. Public depiction of Mordrax is itself suspect. What survives in restricted scholarly archives describes her as faceless, voracious, and bound — though whether the binding is physical, ritual, or metaphoric is itself contested.

## Connections

- [[Temple of Mordrax]] — her sealed sanctum, the deepest forbidden site in the [[Hollow Crown]]
- [[Shadowed Hollows]] — surface region above her temple, slowly twisted by necrotic seepage
- [[Rift of Discord]] — separate scar, but in the same wounded quadrant
- [[Solveris]] — moral counterweight; the [[Platinum Heart]] is positioned as the deliberate spiritual opposite of her sealed sanctum

## Open questions

- What is Mordrax — a captured being? An emanation? A primordial principle? Different traditions answer differently and the [[Codex of Remembrance]] does not clarify.
- Why does the temple predate the [[Oath of the Fallen]] and the sanctuary itself? Who built it? What did they intend?
- How active is her cult network in the present age?
