# The Unchanged

## Overview

The Unchanged is the trickster of the planet's religious imagination — the figure of variability, threshold, and the unknown variable. Where [[Solveris]] and [[Mordrax]] form a primal cosmic polarity, the Unchanged is the third position that refuses to settle. It is not part of the draconic-derived religion that gave the planet its central pantheon; it appears alongside that religion in folk practice across cultures.

Whether the Unchanged is a genuine deity, a celestial of some other kind that survived the [[Cataclysm]] without giving its power away as the [[Elves]] did, the planet's first contact with the trickster archetype that recurs in nearly every mythological structure, or simply a name mortals attach to the strangeness of changing things — no one knows. Different cultures attach different names: the Far-Come, the Visitor, the Nameless, the One-Who-Is-Not. These names contradict one another by design. **Unknowability is the point.**

No telling about the Unchanged lasts long without changing in the telling.

## Role

The Unchanged occupies the position the trickster archetype occupies in nearly every mythological tradition — the figure of disruption, transformation, and the unknown variable. In other planetary mythologies (where this archetype recurs under different names) the trickster is the figure invoked when change cannot be held off, when an outcome is genuinely uncertain, or when something needs to be addressed at the threshold between states.

The Unchanged plays this role in the planet's folk theology. It serves as the unfixed religious variable — the figure whose ambiguity creates room for stories, decisions, and outcomes that the cosmic poles of [[Solveris]] and [[Mordrax]] would otherwise have already determined. The figure is canonical *as ambiguity*, not despite it.

## Domain

Change itself. Thresholds. Doorways. The cusp between old and new. Decisions that cannot be unmade. Bargains struck at borders. Names whispered at births and at funerals. Coins turned in the hand before a journey. Mirrors not quite trusted. Strangers met at crossroads. Dawn and dusk, but not noon or midnight. The places and moments where what something was is becoming what something else will be.

The Unchanged is not invoked for protection or for justice. It is invoked, if at all, when something is going to change and the asker wants to be on the right side of the changing.

## Nature

What is consistent across the variants is the shape of the thing's nature. The Unchanged does not appear the same way twice. It is a shapeshifter, but not in the manner of dragons or fey or skinwalking magic — those are creatures wearing forms. The Unchanged does not seem to wear forms. The form is what the Unchanged is, in any given moment, and the moment is what is changing.

Hence the name: a god whose only constancy is its inconstancy, and who keeps no shape long enough to be known by it.

This shapeshifting quality is consistent with the trickster role: a figure that cannot be pinned to a form is also a figure that cannot be pinned to an outcome.

## Worship

Not worshipped in temples. There are no Unchanged clergy, no Unchanged liturgies, no formal rites. What exists instead is folk practice — a coin left at a threshold, a small offering at a gate, a phrase spoken before crossing a border, a token tied at a crossroads tree.

Different cultures attach different names: the Far-Come, the Visitor, the Nameless, the One-Who-Is-Not. These names contradict one another and are not reconciled, because reconciliation would defeat the practice. The Unchanged is the god you greet without claiming to know.

## Doctrinal silence

The [[Draconic Council]] does not speak of the Unchanged. The [[Codex of Remembrance]] does not name it. Whether this silence is doctrinal disapproval, deliberate ignorance, or something the Codex itself was instructed to omit, no one currently in a position to ask is willing to ask.

## Open questions

All of them — origin, nature, intent, scope of power, whether it can be communicated with at all, whether the folk practice has any actual effect, whether the Council's silence is meaningful. Resolution of these questions would defeat the figure.

Specific surfaceable questions:

- Whether the Unchanged has any relationship to [[The Two]] or only happens to co-occur in cosmological speculation
- Whether any individual has ever knowingly encountered the Unchanged and recorded the encounter accurately
- Whether the trickster role this figure plays is *the* trickster of the planet, or one of several — a question made harder by the fact that any trickster figure resists being counted
