# War of the Fallen

## Overview

The War of the Fallen is the conflict whose end gave rise to [[Drakenvale]]. Dragons of many embodiments waged conflict severe enough to annihilate the lesser civilizations caught between them. In the aftermath of one such civilizational devastation, a coalition of dragons, drakari, and surviving mortals came to believe that continued endless war would destroy not only the mortal world, but draconic legacy itself. From that conviction came the [[Oath of the Fallen]] and the founding of the sanctuary.

## Causes

Long-running draconic conflict in the age following [[The Cataclysm]]. Dragons newly empowered by the [[Nine Magical Fields]] waged war on one another with weapons the world had never seen. Mortal civilizations existed primarily as terrain — sometimes as participants, more often as casualties.

## Course

The war's specific battles, factions, and dragons-in-conflict are not preserved in canonical detail in the surviving material. What is preserved is the war's final character: a conflict severe enough to consume not only the warring dragons but the mortal peoples around them, culminating in the annihilation of a specific mortal civilization caught in the crossfire.

That annihilation became the catalyst for change. The leading figure in the response was the [[Platinum Warden]] — Vindrael in his personal name — a devotee of [[Solveris]] and the principal architect of the sanctuary ideal. Under his leadership, dragons, drakari, and surviving mortal lineages chose to build something other than continued war.

## Consequences

- The [[Oath of the Fallen]] — a founding agreement to build a hidden sanctuary governed by justice, balance, restraint, and shared responsibility
- The founding of [[Drakenvale]] within the [[Hollow Crown]]
- The carving of the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] from the [[Heartmass]]
- The [[Trial of Wings]] — preserved by the founders as a non-lethal resolution mechanism for the kind of disputes that had consumed the war
- The [[Codex of Remembrance]] — emergent from the Oath's founding principles
- A draconic culture defined by what it refuses, not only what it embraces

## Memory

Drakenvale's founding identity is built on the war's lessons. Every institution traces back to a refusal of what the War of the Fallen made possible. The [[Codex of Remembrance]]'s "power tempered by restraint" is the governing translation of what the war meant.

The [[Feywood]] elves watched the war from outside it and chose not to fight. When the survivors raised the Oath, the elves emerged in their own time and entered as sovereign-equal signatories.
