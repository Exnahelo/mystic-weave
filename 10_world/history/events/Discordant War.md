# Discordant War

## Overview

The Discordant War is the civil rupture that nearly broke [[Drakenvale]] from within. A faction known as the [[Mordraxian Rebels]] sought to unseal the [[Temple of Mordrax]] by excavating a ceremonial shaft from their fortified keep, the [[Dark Hold]]. The [[Platinum Warden]] fought to the heart of the Hold and gave his life at the pit's edge, collapsing the shaft and severing the rebels' connection to [[Mordrax]]'s power. The [[Draconic Council]] then restored the wards on the Temple. [[Lethariel Heartwood]] of the [[Feywood]] led an elven contingent alongside the Warden in the war's final days, fulfilling the [[Oath of the Fallen]]'s mutual-aid clause.

The wound where the Dark Hold once stood is what the realm now calls the [[Rift of Discord]].

## Causes

### Breakdown of harmony

The equilibrium of Drakenvale did not hold forever. Deep philosophical tensions, distrust, ambition, and unresolved spiritual fracture resurfaced over time. Although dragons remained formally equal under the [[Codex of Remembrance]], resentment grew in some quarters over the cultural and moral centrality of [[Solveris]] within public sanctuary life. What had once been managed through restraint, mediation, and ritual hardened into factional grievance.

The [[Mordraxian Rebels]] eventually moved to unseal the [[Temple of Mordrax]]. Their effort was not merely theological rebellion but a direct challenge to the sanctuary's moral and political order.

## Course

### The Dark Hold

The Mordraxian Rebels held a fortified keep in the southern reaches of the [[Hollow Crown]] — the [[Dark Hold]] — built on the site as a focus for the tapping of deep corruption. At the keep's center the rebels excavated a ceremonial shaft, descending into the earth in pursuit of the dark energy they sought to channel and weaponize. The Hold was the operational center of the rebellion: from it, the faction coordinated its forces, developed its workings, and pressed toward the [[Temple of Mordrax]] buried far below. The shaft was the means; the Temple was the goal.

### War within the sanctuary

The conflict was not an external invasion, but a rupture within the sanctuary itself. Alliances fractured. Trust collapsed. Sacred and political authority were tested simultaneously. Fighting scarred the southern reaches of the Hollow Crown and accelerated as the Dark Hold's ceremonies grew bolder and the shaft descended deeper.

### The Feywood's aid

When word of the Mordraxian Rebels' move reached the [[Feywood]], the Council of Elders convened in the Council Chamber beneath the Heartwood to consider the mutual-aid clause of the [[Oath of the Fallen]]. The Council was deadlocked.

In that deadlock the Heartwood itself acted: a branch fell bearing a single flower, addressed to [[Lethariel Heartwood]] — then a young commander of House Heartwood. She performed the rare Pool ritual in the Chamber, placing the flower in the Pool's water and drinking, and the vision the Heartwood gave her overrode the Council's deadlock.

The Feywood deployed. Lethariel led a contingent of roughly two hundred elves through the Hollow Crown's western threshold and into the conflict. The elven force was not large in numerical terms but was decisive in quality, arriving at a moment when the sanctuary's defenders were stretched thin across the war's spreading violence. Lethariel and her contingent fought alongside the [[Platinum Warden]] through the war's final days. She was at the pit's edge when the Warden gave his life and witnessed his sacrifice firsthand.

The Feywood's deployment in the Discordant War is the only canonical fulfillment of the Oath's mutual-aid clause. It is honored in both realms — written into Drakenvale's record and held in elven oral tradition.

### Sacrifice of the Platinum Warden

At the war's final confrontation, the [[Platinum Warden]] fought his way to the heart of the [[Dark Hold]] and gave his life at the pit's edge. The exact means by which he did so are not fully preserved in canon, but the result is established: his sacrifice collapsed the shaft, severed the [[Mordraxian Rebels]]' channel to [[Mordrax]]'s power, and broke the foundation of the rebellion. With the corruption channel closed, the [[Draconic Council]] was able to restore and reinforce the wards on the [[Temple of Mordrax]] below. The Warden's act made the sealing possible; the Council's working completed it.

## Consequences

The Warden's sacrifice became the defining moral event in Drakenvale's history. It transformed the [[Temple of Mordrax]] from a dangerous site into a lasting theological wound. The Dark Hold itself was left where it had broken — the shaft collapsed inward, the keep ruined and never cleaned up, the unstable magic the ceremonies had loosed still bleeding into the surrounding land. That wound became the [[Rift of Discord]].

In the aftermath:

- the [[Shadowed Hollows]] emerged as the corrupted surface region above the buried [[Temple of Mordrax]]
- the [[Rift of Discord]] remained where the [[Dark Hold]] had stood, unstable and slow to settle
- the [[Codex of Remembrance]] was amended to more explicitly prohibit actions that destabilized alignment harmony
- redemption rites and stronger sacred vigilance became permanent features of the realm's moral structure
- old trust between dragon lineages was never fully restored to its pre-war state
- the [[Vigil]] (also remembered as the Platinum Accord) emerged as the order tasked with [[Rift of Discord]] vigilance and preservation of the Warden's legacy

## Memory

The war did not destroy Drakenvale, but it permanently changed what the sanctuary believed about itself. It is remembered as the founding generation's failure — the proof that the [[Oath of the Fallen]]'s vision could be threatened from within as well as from without. It is also remembered as the moment the [[Feywood]] honored its compact and the moment the [[Platinum Warden]] became something more than a leader.
