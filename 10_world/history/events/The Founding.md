# The Founding

## Overview

The Founding is the era in which the survivors of the [[War of the Fallen]] gathered in the [[Hollow Crown]] and built the sanctuary that became [[Drakenvale]]. Led by the [[Platinum Warden]], the founders entered the [[Oath of the Fallen]], carved the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] from the [[Heartmass]], discovered the [[Heartstone]], welcomed the [[Feywood]] elves as sovereign-equal allies, and established the first sacred and civic institutions.

## Causes

The [[War of the Fallen]] had ended a civilization. The coalition of dragons, drakari, and surviving mortals who had stopped the war did not disperse — they chose to build something that would refuse what they had just survived.

## Course

### Settlement of the Hollow Crown

The Hollow Crown was not so much chosen as inherited: it was where the war had ended, where the wounded coalition had withdrawn, and where the founders could begin again. Dragons, drakari followers, kobold laborers, and mortal artisans began the work of carving the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] from the [[Heartmass]]. The first wards were laid into the stone and basin. Safe routes through the surrounding mountain were established in secrecy.

### Discovery of the Heartstone

During the earliest excavations within the [[Heartmass]], the founders uncovered the [[Heartstone]] — a radiant crystal embedded within the fused celestial-platinum core. In the sanctuary's early formation, the three founding ancient dragons each imbued the Heartstone with their own three magical fields, gathering the full [[Nine Magical Fields]] into it and binding memory, intention, and collective purpose into a single living artifact.

### Joining of the Feywood

The [[Elves]] predate Drakenvale by ages. The [[Feywood]] is one of the cradle-lands where their power pooled most deeply during the long age of healing after [[The Cataclysm]]. By the time the survivors of the War of the Fallen began building the sanctuary, the elves had lived in the Feywood for ages — they watched the war from outside it and chose not to fight.

When the founders raised their compact, the elves emerged in their own time and entered the [[Oath of the Fallen]] as sovereign-equal signatories. Their compact was mutual: each polity would leave the other to govern itself, and each would come to the other's aid in times of true crisis. The Feywood remained its own realm, not a province of Drakenvale.

### First Sacred and Civic Foundations

As the Stronghold rose, the earliest institutions took shape:

- the [[Draconic Hall]] as the seat of governance and chamber of the [[Heartstone]]
- the [[Platinum Heart]] as the sanctuary's highest [[Solveris]]-aligned sanctum, where the [[Oath of the Fallen]] was first sworn
- the earliest forms of the [[Amethyst Vault]] as a place of mediation, reflection, and arcane wisdom
- the beginnings of the sacred waterworks that would later become the [[Sacred Pools]]
- the first ward-networks throughout the [[Hollow Crown]]

### The Sealing of the Temple

The [[Temple of Mordrax]], beneath what would later become the [[Shadowed Hollows]], predates the Oath by ages. It was already ancient when the survivors gathered in the basin; its presence was a fact the founders inherited, not a structure they raised. The early Council debated what to do with it. Some held that the Temple should be destroyed if destruction were possible. Others argued that the sanctuary could not be founded on the pretense that [[Mordrax]] did not exist.

In the end, the Temple was sealed under the wards of the Oath as the founding compact's final act — a sealed and heavily restricted site of remembrance, warning, and containment rather than public devotion. The seal has held since, with one catastrophic interruption: the [[Discordant War]].

## Consequences

- A functioning sanctuary inside the [[Hollow Crown]]
- The [[Codex of Remembrance]] emergent from Oath principles
- A sovereign-equal alliance with the [[Feywood]]
- The [[Temple of Mordrax]] sealed but not destroyed — a wound contained, not closed
- The [[Heartstone]] established as the political and spiritual focus of draconic governance
- The founding cohort of dragons, including the [[Platinum Warden]], became the realm's defining figures

## Memory

The Founding is remembered as the realm's deliberate beginning — not an inheritance, but a choice. The [[Oath of the Fallen]] is held as the moral founding moment; the carving of the Stronghold is held as the physical founding moment; the elven joining is held as the diplomatic founding moment. All three are commemorated in different forms of liturgy and inscription.
