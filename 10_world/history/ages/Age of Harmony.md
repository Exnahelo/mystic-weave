# Age of Harmony

## Overview

The Age of Harmony is the long era following [[The Founding]] in which [[Drakenvale]] flourished as a center of magical learning, sacred law, draconic diplomacy, craft, training, and guarded prosperity. The [[Codex of Remembrance]] emerged from the founding principles of the [[Oath of the Fallen]] and became the central legal-moral order. The sanctuary's ideal of coexistence was at its strongest in this age.

## Course

### Growth of the Sanctuary

The city expanded around the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]]. Agricultural settlements, quarry and mining communities, forest villages, wetland towns, and deeper cavern settlements grew into a functioning network. Drakenvale ceased to be merely a hidden fortress and became a complete interior world.

### Institutions and Rituals

This era saw the establishment or formalization of many enduring institutions:

- the [[Draconic Council]] as the central governing authority
- the [[Draconic Conclave]] as the rare gathering of dragons for major structural questions and legal revision
- the [[Trial of Wings]] — preserved by the founders after the [[War of the Fallen]] but reformed into its current ceremonial structure during this age, formalized as non-lethal
- the [[Infernal Forge]] as the realm's center of enchanted craft and strategic metallurgy, governed by the [[Infernal Forge Guild]]
- [[Zarkharath]] as the city that grew around the Infernal Forge over time
- growing authority of temple, guild, educational, and military institutions
- the formal use of the [[Heartstone]] in moments of true deadlock or exceptional guidance

## Character of the era

Dragons of many dispositions and magical embodiments contributed to the realm's flourishing. It was not a perfect peace — tension persisted — but it stood as proof that a peace worth building was possible.

## Consequences

The Age of Harmony established the institutional and ritual scaffolding that the realm still uses. When the [[Discordant War]] later fractured the sanctuary, what was preserved through it was largely what this era had built. The institutions endured even when the moral consensus that supported them did not.

## Memory

Remembered as the realm's strongest period of coexistence and as the proof-of-concept that the [[Oath of the Fallen]]'s vision could function in practice. Also remembered, retrospectively, as the period during which the seeds of the [[Discordant War]] were already being sown — the philosophical tensions that would later rupture were present even at the height of harmony, simply managed rather than resolved.
