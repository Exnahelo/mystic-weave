# The Two

## Definition

"The Two" is the cosmological register for the primal polarity worshipped
as [[Solveris]] and [[Mordrax]]. They are not a separate, deeper pair
standing behind the deities — they *are* the deities, pointed at in the
register of creation and cosmic substrate rather than the register of
worship. Where liturgy speaks of Solveris and Mordrax, the oldest
cosmology speaks of the Two: the opposition of preservation and
dissolution whose collision is the world's origin.

This is why references overlap. The deities and the Two are one polarity
under two names for two purposes — Solveris and Mordrax where the polarity
is invoked, propitiated, and built into civic order; the Two where it is
treated as the cosmic precondition everything else rests on.

The names "Solveris" and "Mordrax" are themselves of unknown origin (see
those notes). "The Two" is older still as a way of pointing at the
polarity — a description rather than a name, used where the polarity is
spoken of as cosmic principle rather than as object of worship.

## The collision and the world

The draconic creation myth holds that the world was brought into existence
by the collision of the Two: two opposing forces meeting, and from their
meeting the world arising. This is the same opposition that civic religion
receives as Solveris (the pole that holds) against Mordrax (the pole that
unmakes). The creation myth is the cosmological face of the polarity; the
deities are its worshipped face.

In draconic cosmology, the collision is the act of creation, not an event that merely preceded a separately-existing world. The world is the collision's outcome — the standing tension between the two poles, held rather than resolved. The collision did not end; it became the condition of existence. Creation is not the victory of one pole over the other, but the failure of either pole to become absolute. The duality visible throughout draconic religion (opposing forces in balance) traces directly to this: it is the polarity that made the world, received into worship.

## The Cataclysm is later, and separate

The collision of the Two is the world's origin. [[The Cataclysm]] is a far
later event — the extra-solar impact that brought, or woke, magic in the
world the Two had already made. The two must not be conflated: the
collision *made* the world; the Cataclysm *wounded and transformed* it,
immeasurably later. Whether the Two were present at, caused, witnessed, or
merely echoed in the Cataclysm is unsettled and unsettlable.

## Magic

That magic in its present form arrived with [[The Cataclysm]] is settled —
before it, dragons held only minor ability. *Where* magic came from is not
settled. It is a live dispute across traditions, including one strand
within draconic religion that holds the deities themselves sent it. The
full dispute and its competing accounts live in [[Origin of Magic]]; the
Two appear there only as the deities credited by the theistic strand, not
as a cosmological source standing apart from worship.

## Variants and disputes

Draconic cosmology preserves the polarity of the Two as the world's deepest structure: preservation and dissolution held in unresolved tension. Other traditions do not necessarily share this framing. Some interpret the same forces through different symbols; some subordinate them beneath older gods, ancestral powers, elemental orders, or impersonal laws; others reject the polarity entirely as a draconic theological projection.

Within [[Drakenvale]], the polarity is received as the deities [[Solveris]] and [[Mordrax]]. In the oldest draconic cosmology, they are spoken of as the Two whose collision made the world. Whether that account describes reality itself, a culturally specific revelation, or a mythic structure imposed on older events remains disputed outside draconic tradition.
At the cosmological level, draconic doctrine treats the Two as a single opposition by definition. The question of "two faces of one reality, or two distinct beings?" belongs primarily to worship and theology — to how Solveris and Mordrax are invoked, personified, and related to civic order — rather than to whether draconic cosmology requires an underlying polarity. It remains a canonical open dispute; see [[Solveris]] and [[Mordrax]].

[[The Unchanged]] is sometimes claimed to have witnessed the collision.
Whether this is true, partly true, or a story the Unchanged tells about
itself is undeterminable.

## Open questions

- Whether the worshipped deities are two faces of one polarity or two
  distinct beings — a dispute at the level of worship; see [[Solveris]],
  [[Mordrax]].
- Whether the collision could recur, and whether the world's continued
  existence depends on the poles remaining in held tension rather than one
  consuming the other.
- Whether any being besides possibly [[The Unchanged]] has any direct
  connection to the collision.
