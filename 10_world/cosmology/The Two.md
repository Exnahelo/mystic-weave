# The Two

## Definition

The Two are the primordial opposition whose collision predates the world itself. The first opposition — the collision of the Two — was already complete before the world existed. [[The Cataclysm]] came later, immeasurably later, in a world the Two had already shaped.

What the Two were, and what their collision produced, is unrecorded in canonical detail. Their existence is foundational cosmology rather than narrative — referenced, acknowledged, but not described.

## Scope

The Two operate at the level of cosmic substrate. They are not deities in the sense that [[Solveris]] and [[Mordrax]] are deities. They are not figures who can be invoked, propitiated, or served. The Two precede the categories within which deity-figures function.

## Mechanics in the world

Their relationship to the world is causal but inaccessible. The collision shaped the world's underlying conditions. Whether [[The Cataclysm]] was caused by them, witnessed by them, or merely echoed in them is unsettled. The wound the Cataclysm left is the world we have. Magic as it is now arrived with that wound. Whether magic comes from the Two, came independently, or was already implicit in the reality the Two shaped is unanswered.

## Variants and disputes

Most variants treat the Two as a near-axiom of cosmology — present in any account of the world's deepest origins, but described in different terms across different traditions. Elven cosmology, dwarven deep-stone tradition, draconic ancient memory, gnomish residue-pattern theory — each frames the Two slightly differently, but each preserves the essential structure: an opposition that predated everything else.

The [[Unchanged]] is sometimes claimed to have witnessed the collision. Whether this is true, partly true, or a story told by the Unchanged about itself is undeterminable.

## Open questions

- Essentially everything. What the Two were, what they wanted, why they collided, whether they survived in any form, whether they could collide again, whether the world's continued existence depends on their continued absence.
- Whether any current being besides possibly the [[Unchanged]] has any connection to them.
- Whether the Codex of Remembrance, the Oath of the Fallen, or any other founding instrument addresses the Two implicitly.
