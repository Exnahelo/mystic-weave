# Heartstone

A quasi-sentient crystal artifact at the heart of the [[Draconic Council]], housed in the [[Draconic Hall]] of the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]]. The Heartstone serves as a silent fourth voice on the Council — not a vote, not a speaker, but a presence that weighs in on every deliberation through the emotional and magical texture it produces in the chamber. It is the realm's central artifact of draconic unity.

## Origin

The Heartstone was discovered during the carving of the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] from the [[Heartmass]] — the colossal formation of fused celestial stone and platinum-veined mountain heartrock that lodged in the basin's broken core when [[The Cataclysm]] struck. As the founders cut the fortress out of the formation, they uncovered a single luminous crystal at its core, alive in a way ordinary mineral was not.

The three founding [[Draconic Council]] dragons — between them embodying all [[Nine Magical Fields|nine magical fields]] — imbued the crystal with their magic. What had been a luminous formation became something more: a focal artifact carrying the integrated draconic magical signature of the realm's founding. It has stood at the center of the [[Draconic Hall]] ever since.

## Form

The Heartstone is **no taller than a grown drakari**, set on a low pedestal at the exact center of the mosaic floor of the [[Draconic Hall]]. It is platinum-veined crystal — the same metal-shot luminescence found in the [[Heartmass]] generally, concentrated and given form. It glows softly at all hours.

Its **veins pulse in slow, irregular rhythms** that track the emotional weight of whatever is happening in the chamber. When the Council is in agreement, the rhythm steadies. When the Council is in conflict, the light thickens and slows. Those attuned to it can sometimes hear, at the edge of perception, **a sound that is not quite language and not quite music**.

## Sentience

The Heartstone is not sentient in the way the [[Heartwood]] is. It does not appear to think in person-shaped ways. It has no language. It does not communicate through symbols or signs in the manner of a speaking entity.

What it does is **weigh in**. Its presence alters the chamber. When deliberation in the [[Draconic Hall]] is going wrong — when a Council member has arrived in bad faith, when an argument has lost its grounding, when a decision is forming for the wrong reasons — the Heartstone's rhythm changes, its light shifts, and the dragons in the room feel the difference. The old Council records describe the Heartstone occasionally producing **a clarity no one in the room brought with them**.

This is the sense in which the Heartstone serves as a fourth Council member. It does not speak. It does not vote. But the three deliberating dragons take its presence seriously, and the chamber's decisions are shaped by what the Heartstone's response has been to the deliberation.

In moments of true Council deadlock — rare, perhaps a handful of times in a century — the Council consults the Heartstone directly. What this consultation entails is not publicly known. The result is respected without question.

## Function

- **Witness to deliberation.** Present at all formal Council convenings in the [[Draconic Hall]].
- **Witness to oaths and treaties.** External agreements ratified in the Hall, the most binding oaths of the realm, accession to the [[Codex of Remembrance]], succession of Council authority — all are sworn in the Heartstone's presence. Oaths sworn before it carry a different weight than oaths sworn elsewhere.
- **Council deadlock consultation.** Direct exchange with the deliberating dragons in cases of genuine impasse.
- **Symbol of draconic unity.** The Heartstone embodies the integration of the founding dragons' magical fields — the proof that the realm's three founding wills can be held in a single coherent form.

## Removal

The Heartstone is not removable. **Attempts to move it, even ceremonially, have never been made.** The Council has never seen reason to. Whether the Heartstone *could* be moved if the attempt were made is unanswered; the question has not been put to the test.

## Relationship to other artifacts

- **The [[Heartmass]].** The Heartstone is an artifact uncovered from the Heartmass formation and infused by the Council. The [[Heartmass]] itself was the larger formation; the Stronghold was carved from it; the Heartstone is its luminous crystalline core, drawn out and consecrated.
- **The [[Platinum Heart|Platinum Flame]].** Distinct artifact, distinct character. The Flame is [[Solveris]]'s regard held in the realm; the Heartstone is the Council's integrated draconic magic given form. The two are not coordinated — they exist in parallel as the realm's two foundational sacred objects.
