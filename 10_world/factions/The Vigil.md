# The Vigil

## Overview

The Vigil is the concealed surviving remnant of the **Platinum Accord**, publicly known only as the monastic order of Platinum Oath Monastery. Publicly, the Vigil appears as the disciplined monastic order of Platinum Oath, associated with [[Rift of Discord]] vigilance, hard devotional practice, and dangerous perimeter work. Internally, it preserves the living remnant of the Platinum Accord — a sacred order founded with its own oath, the **Oath of Scales**, sworn at the [[Platinum Heart]] at the same founding moment as the [[Oath of the Fallen]].

Its true identity is not common public knowledge.

## Purpose

The Vigil's duties include monitoring [[Rift of Discord]] instability, maintaining continuity with the [[Platinum Warden]]'s legacy, safeguarding restricted knowledge, and preparing selected candidates for extreme-edge operations. It is the order most closely bound to the Warden's defense of the sanctuary in its gravest hours.

## Structure

**Type:** Restricted monastic watch order / Accord remnant.

**Leadership:** [[Serevane]].

**Status:** Canonical, active, partially concealed.

The order maintains two layers of identity:
- **Public** — the monks of Platinum Oath Monastery, recognized as a guarded order associated with [[Rift of Discord]] vigilance and harsh devotional discipline
- **Restricted** — the inner Vigil proper, which preserves the Accord, the [[Platinum Warden]]'s tomb, and the deeper Solveris-aligned tradition the Accord embodies

## Holdings

- **Platinum Oath Monastery** — the public face of the order; located at the [[Rift of Discord]]'s edge
- The [[Platinum Warden]]'s tomb — maintained by the Vigil; the resonance of his death is used for [[Rift of Discord]] monitoring
- Restricted archives of the Accord
- The Oath of Scales — a parallel inner oath sworn alongside the [[Oath of the Fallen]]

## Relationships

- [[Solveris]] — doctrinal anchor, shared with the [[Platinum Acolytes]] but with deeper inner tradition
- [[Platinum Acolytes]] — the visible Solveris-aligned order; the relationship between the two is structural and partly preserved as restricted knowledge. Many Acolytes know little of the Vigil's inner identity.
- [[Platinum Warden]] / [[Vindrael]] — the founder figure whose legacy they preserve
- [[Draconic Council]] — operates with Council awareness but maintains its own internal authority
- [[Wardens]] — quiet operational coordination at the [[Rift of Discord]] perimeter

## Doctrine

The sanctuary's defenses are maintained not only by visible institutions but by sworn vigilance against the slowest threats — the threats that don't announce themselves. The [[Rift of Discord]] is the prime example: an unstable wound that requires not response but ongoing attention. The Vigil's discipline is the discipline of long watch.

The Oath of Scales is the inner counterpart to the [[Oath of the Fallen]] — a binding to the sanctuary's defense at the level of last resort.

## Internal tensions

The double-identity structure is functional but produces friction. Junior monks know the public order; senior figures know more; only the innermost circle knows the full Accord. This means the Vigil contains ranks of partial knowledge — a form of compartmentalization that protects restricted material but creates internal asymmetry.

The relationship with the [[Platinum Acolytes]] is the most delicate institutional question. The Acolytes are visibly Solveris-aligned; the Vigil is restricted-Solveris-aligned. Public coordination would compromise the concealment; complete separation would deny shared doctrine. The current equilibrium — quiet awareness at senior levels, parallel public operation, no formal joint structure — is sustained by deliberate practice.
