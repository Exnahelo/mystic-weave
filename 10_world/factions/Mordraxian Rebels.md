# Mordraxian Rebels

## Overview

The Mordraxian Rebels are the historical faction that rose against [[Drakenvale]] during the [[Discordant War]]. They sought to unseal the [[Temple of Mordrax]] by excavating a ceremonial shaft from their fortified keep, the [[Dark Hold]], pursuing the dark energy they sought to channel and weaponize. Their effort was not merely theological rebellion but a direct challenge to the sanctuary's moral and political order.

The faction was effectively destroyed when the [[Platinum Warden]]'s sacrifice collapsed the shaft and the [[Draconic Council]] resealed the [[Temple of Mordrax]]. They are not active in the [[Modern Era]] — but [[Mordrax Cults]] persist as fragmented underground successors.

## Purpose

To unseal the [[Temple of Mordrax]], channel its power, and reshape (or destroy) the sanctuary's moral and political order. The Rebels positioned themselves as the rejection of [[Solveris]]-aligned centrality and of the [[Codex of Remembrance]]'s restraint-doctrine.

## Structure

**Type:** Rebel faction (defunct).

**Leadership:** Specific named leadership not preserved in surviving canonical record. The Rebels were composed of dragons, drakari, and aligned mortals who had concluded that the sanctuary's [[Solveris]]-aligned order was unjustifiable.

**Status:** Defeated; not active in the Modern Era. Successor cult activity persists as [[Mordrax Cults]].

## Holdings (former)

- The [[Dark Hold]] — fortified keep in the southern reaches of the [[Hollow Crown]]; built as the focus for tapping deep corruption. Now ruined; the site is the [[Rift of Discord]].
- The ceremonial shaft — excavated from the Dark Hold downward toward the [[Temple of Mordrax]]. Collapsed by the [[Platinum Warden]]'s sacrifice.

## Relationships

- [[Mordrax]] — the deity they pursued; their power-channel relied on access to her sealed presence below
- [[Temple of Mordrax]] — their goal; never reached
- [[Platinum Warden]] — the figure who broke them
- [[Draconic Council]] — the authority that resealed the Temple after the war
- [[Mordrax Cults]] — fragmented modern successors

## Doctrine

The Rebels held that the sanctuary's order was a contingent imposition by a [[Solveris]]-aligned faction, not a necessary moral truth. Their pursuit of [[Mordrax]] was not nihilism — it was a counter-doctrine claiming that ambition unconstrained by [[Codex of Remembrance]]-style restraint was a legitimate path. They rejected the framing of [[Mordrax]] as corruption per se, holding instead that the sealing of the Temple was itself the corruption — a refusal to acknowledge what was theologically real.

This is, by [[Codex of Remembrance]] standards, profound heresy. By the Rebels' standards, it was the only honest theology.

## Defeat

The [[Discordant War]]'s final confrontation occurred at the [[Dark Hold]]. The [[Platinum Warden]] fought to the heart of the keep and gave his life at the pit's edge. His sacrifice collapsed the shaft, severed the channel, and broke the foundation of the rebellion. The Council then restored the wards on the [[Temple of Mordrax]] below. Surviving Rebels were either killed in the war's aftermath, exiled, or driven underground.
