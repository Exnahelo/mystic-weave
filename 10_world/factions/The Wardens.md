# The Wardens

## Overview

[[Drakenvale]]'s internal security, sacred-site, and anomaly-response force. The Wardens protect the [[Platinum Heart]], the [[Amethyst Vault]], the [[Heartstone]] chamber, and the sealed [[Temple of Mordrax]]. They investigate magical anomalies, internal disturbances, corruption threats, cult activity, and breaches involving sacred or strategically sensitive sites.

## Purpose

To preserve the sanctuary from internal threat — corruption, cultic activity, unauthorized intrusion at sacred sites, and the slow magical breaches that the realm's deeper history makes possible.

## Structure

**Type:** Internal security and sacred protection force.

**Leadership:** Commanded by [[Eryndor the Radiant]] in peacetime. Temporarily militarized under [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] in crisis. This dual command structure is one of the sanctuary's persistent institutional tensions — the Wardens belong to no single Council member by default, and crises shift their command in ways that have political weight.

## Holdings

- The [[Platinum Heart]] — primary sacred site under their watch
- The [[Amethyst Vault]] — protected and administered alongside scholarly oversight
- The [[Heartstone]] chamber — within the [[Draconic Hall]]
- The sealed [[Temple of Mordrax]] — outer perimeter; the Temple itself is sealed under draconic warding, but the Wardens guard the approach

## Relationships

- [[Draconic Council]] — operate under Council authority; the dual command between [[Eryndor the Radiant]] and [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] is the political reality
- [[Dragon Guard]] — distinct force; jurisdictions overlap during emergencies
- [[Platinum Acolytes]] — sacred coordination on Platinum Heart matters
- [[Vigil]] — quiet coordination at the [[Rift of Discord]] perimeter

## Doctrine

Internal vigilance is the Codex's quietest demand. The Wardens act on the principle that the gravest threats to [[Drakenvale]] historically come from within — the [[Discordant War]] is the institutional memory that shaped their modern role.

They are not an investigative service in the modern criminal sense; they are sacred-site guardians with investigative function. Their work on cults, anomalies, and corruption threats falls under that broader sacred mandate.

## Internal tensions

The split command between [[Eryndor the Radiant]] (peacetime) and [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] (crisis) means individual Wardens know which voice they will be answering to depends on the realm's current state. This produces a force whose institutional culture is shaped by both philosophies — moral-justice in calm times, hard-edged force when pressure rises. Critics within the Wardens themselves describe this as institutional schizophrenia; defenders describe it as a feature of dual oversight.

The Wardens' jurisdiction overlap with the [[Dragon Guard]] during emergencies has produced friction historically.
