# The Rangers

The [[Greenshields]]' specialist arm for the work that doesn't fit settled patrol or fixed-post defense. Rangers are sworn Greenshields who have taken additional specialist training and serve in the order's most distributed posts — continuous threshold-watch at the [[Feywood Border|Border]], outer-range reconnaissance through the [[Feywood]]'s edges, military-coded escort when ordinary civilian escort isn't sufficient, and the rare operational coordination with realm-side forces under Council authorization.

**The branch is not a separate order.** Rangers wear Greenshield identity, take the Greenshield oath, answer to the Greenshield Commander through the Captain of the Ranger Branch, and rotate back into general Greenshield service if their specialist career ends.

## Doctrine

The Ranger branch exists because the [[Greenshields]]' general posture — settlement defense, fixed-post border garrison, scheduled interior patrol — does not cover all of the order's defensive work. Several specific kinds of work require something more distributed, more autonomous, and more attentive to the [[Feywood]]'s particular geometry:

### Threshold-watch

The realm-to-realm [[Feywood Border|Border]] is not a wall the [[Greenshields]] man; it is a liminal threshold the [[Heartwood]] maintains and the order attends from the elven side. **Reading the threshold** — what crosses, who crosses, what does not cross that should have, what crosses that should not have, what attempts the threshold and is rejected by the [[Heartwood]]'s working — is specialist work. The general Border garrison does the bulk of this; **Rangers extend the work into the threshold's active edges, where the geometry is most responsive and signals are most subtle**.

### Outer-range reconnaissance

Patterns matter at the [[Feywood]]'s edges. Wildlife behavior, vegetation health, the specific quality of the canopy, the frequency and character of unsanctioned approach attempts — these signals accumulate at the [[Feywood]]'s outer extents and are read most carefully by elves whose work is to be there continuously. **Rangers conduct extended reconnaissance through the Outer Old-Growth Wild, the western and southern [[Feywood]] reaches, and the specific zones where realm-side pressure registers first.**

### Military-coded escort

Most sanctioned visitors are escorted by the [[Hall of Guides]] — civilian-coded, no martial register. Some escorts require something more: visitors whose business may attract hostile attention, ceremonial movements that warrant martial accompaniment, scenarios where the [[Hall of Guides]]' civilian register would be inappropriate. **Rangers handle these cases with arms ready.**

### Realm-side operational contact

When the [[Council of Elders|Council]] authorizes operational coordination with realm-side forces — typically [[Dragon Guard]] or [[Wardens]], in scenarios approaching Oath-invoking crisis — Rangers serve as the order's interface. **Such authorizations are extremely rare;** the [[Discordant War]] is the only canonical instance of significant realm-side operational coordination. Rangers maintain readiness for it as part of the branch's structural commitment.

The doctrinal stance across all four pillars is the same: **defensive posture, attentive presence, action calibrated to context rather than to standing orders.** Rangers do not pursue, expand, or campaign beyond their authorized range.

## Relationship to the Greenshields

Rangers are part of the [[Greenshields]], not separate from them. **The distinction matters and is preserved in the order's culture, its structure, and its self-conception.**

Rangers take the Greenshield oath at [[Thornveil]] headquarters, on the same terms (fifty-year initial commitment, at-pleasure continuation), and serve under the same overall chain. The branch is a specialist track within Greenshield service, not a separate institution. **A Ranger who steps out of specialist work returns to general Greenshield service; a Greenshield who develops Ranger aptitude can transfer in.**

Operationally, Rangers operate with significant autonomy within their posts. The work demands it — threshold reading and outer-range reconnaissance cannot be conducted on rigid schedules. The Captain of the Ranger Branch oversees this autonomy: setting doctrine, maintaining standards, intervening when individual Rangers' judgment requires correction, and coordinating with the Commander on matters that affect the branch's overall posture.

The branch's relationship with the rest of the [[Greenshields]] is **friendly but culturally distinct**. Rangers tend toward independence, observational habit, comfort with extended time alone or in small units, and the specific quietness that the [[Feywood]]'s edges produce in elves who attend to them. **Greenshields who try Ranger work and find it does not fit return to general service without stigma.**

## Structure

### The Captain of the Ranger Branch

A single Captain leads the branch, selected by the Commander from among the most senior Rangers, with input from the Council on appointments of significant duration. Based at [[Thornveil]] headquarters for command-coordination work but spends substantial time in the field. Tenures of half a century to a century are typical.

### Senior Rangers

Veterans of significant Ranger experience — **typically a hundred years or more in branch service**. Senior Rangers lead specific posts, serve as primary instructors during specialist training, and carry the practical authority that Captain-level direction relies on for execution. **~20–30 Senior Rangers active at any given time.**

### Rangers

The body of the branch. **~115–120 sworn Rangers** hold active branch service at any given time, distributed across the posts described below.

### Ranger Trainees

**~5–10 trainees** in the two-year specialist program at any given time. The number is small because the branch's selectivity is high and throughput is correspondingly slow.

## Selection and training

### Selection

Rangers are selected from two sources, **roughly half from each**:

- **Year-four Greenshield trainees** showing aptitude for outer-range work. Selected trainees complete their fifth Greenshield training year with Ranger-track adjustments and, after taking the Greenshield oath, transition directly into the two-year Ranger specialist program.
- **Sworn Greenshields** who develop the aptitude during general service and apply for transfer. Often after a decade or more of general service in patrol or border-garrison postings where their fit became apparent.

The branch looks for: comfort with sustained independent presence; capacity for sustained attention under solitude; demonstrated ability to read the [[Feywood]]'s signals at depth; sound judgment under conditions where standing orders do not apply; willingness to operate at the edges of the order's settled work; and ***clear regard*** — the capacity to perceive accurately under conditions designed to confuse, by intent or by environment.

### Training

**Two years** of specialist training, conducted partly at [[Thornveil]] headquarters but mostly through extended field work under Senior Ranger supervision at the [[Feywood Border|Border]], the outer-range posts, and the [[Feywood]]'s edge zones.

Year one is foundations: deepened reading of the [[Feywood]]'s geometry, the [[Heartwood]]'s specific signals, the threshold's behavior at the [[Feywood Border|Border]], the patterns of unsanctioned approach, the [[Hall of Guides]]' work and the protocols for joint or sequenced escort.

Year two is application: trainees take operational responsibility under Senior Ranger oversight, conducting threshold reading, reconnaissance, and escort work at functional capacity. **By year-end, the trainee is operating substantially as a Ranger;** the formal completion of training is the recognition of what they have already become.

Trainees who do not complete the program return to general Greenshield service without stigma.

## Service

### Threshold-watch (~50 Rangers)

Continuous Ranger presence at the realm-to-realm [[Feywood Border|Border]], integrated with the general Greenshield Border garrison but functioning as a specialist sub-unit. Organized into watch teams that rotate shifts on the Border's various sections.

The work is **sustained attentive presence at the [[Feywood]]'s outer edge**, reading what crosses and what does not. Most of the work is uneventful — sanctioned visitor passage, routine trade movement, the ordinary background of Border activity. The discipline is in distinguishing routine from anomaly across long stretches of routine. Rangers on threshold-watch develop specific reading skills the general Border garrison does not — subtle signals of the [[Heartwood]]'s working at the threshold, early indications of unsanctioned approach, the quality of the geometry's response to specific kinds of pressure.

### Outer-range reconnaissance (~70 Rangers)

Distributed Ranger presence through the [[Feywood]]'s outer extents — the Outer Old-Growth Wild, the western and southern [[Feywood]] reaches, the specific zones where realm-side pressure registers first. Organized into small units of three to six conducting circuits ranging from days to several weeks.

**The reconnaissance work is the branch's most distinctively elven posting.** Rangers in the field develop deep familiarity with the specific zones they cover, become known to the wildlife and the broader ecology, and accumulate the kind of informal authority that long presence in a place produces. **The work is the order's primary informational sensor for what is happening at the [[Feywood]]'s edges;** reports flow continuously back to [[Thornveil]] through established channels.

Reconnaissance postings are typically multi-year — five to fifteen years in a specific zone is common.

### Sanctioned escort (~20 Rangers)

Military-coded escort work for sanctioned visitors whose business warrants martial accompaniment. **Escort needs are episodic rather than continuous.**

The work overlaps with [[Hall of Guides]] routes but **differs in stance**. Hall of Guides escort is civilian-coded; Ranger escort is military-coded: arms ready, awareness elevated, escort posture appropriate to identified or anticipated threat. The two institutions coordinate routes and protocols when both are involved (occasional combined escort for high-significance visitors).

### Realm-side operational contact (~10 in HQ liaison/specialist roles, readiness-based)

The branch's most rarely-exercised work. When the [[Council of Elders|Council]] authorizes operational coordination with realm-side forces, Rangers serve as the order's interface. **The [[Discordant War]] is the only canonical instance**, in which approximately thirty Rangers were among the force deployed alongside the larger Greenshield contingent.

In ordinary times, this pillar is maintained through readiness rather than active work: training in mixed-sovereign protocols, familiarity with realm-side military structure and culture, and the small number of standing relationships that the Captain of the Ranger Branch maintains with realm-side counterparts at the Council-authorized level.

## Equipment

Kit suited to extended outer-range work and threshold-watch.

The primary weapon is the bow, as in the broader [[Greenshields]]. **Rangers tend to favor specific bow types appropriate to forest conditions** — shorter, more compact bows for dense cover; longer-range bows for the open patches at the [[Feywood]]'s edges. The branch does not standardize beyond competence; weapon choice is individual, and Rangers' bows are often deeply personalized over decades of service.

Close-combat secondary weapons trend lighter than general Greenshield kit — short swords, paired blades, or specialized forest-fighting weapons. **The work rewards mobility over weight.**

Armor is the lightest in the order. Rangers wear minimal hard pieces, layered cloth-and-leather suited to multi-week field movement.

Rangers carry distinctive **field kit** beyond weapons and armor: signaling devices, ration arrangements suited to multi-week work, and the small specific items that long Ranger experience has identified as essential. **Each Ranger develops their own field-kit preferences over the course of their career.**

**The branch carries no formal insignia.** Rangers are recognized by context, by gear, and — for those who know the [[Feywood]] — by bearing. **Realm visitors who encounter Rangers in the field rarely register them as institutional presence at all;** the work's effectiveness depends on this.

## Cultural character

**The most distinct culture within the [[Greenshields]].** Rangers are quieter on average than general Greenshields, more comfortable with solitude and small-unit work, more given to extended observation, less ceremonial in register. Not less social — Ranger units develop dense internal cultures across long shared service — but their sociability is calibrated to small groups rather than to settlement-scale civic life.

**Branch humor is dryer than the broader Greenshields'.** Rangers' running jokes tend toward observational, often involving specific zones and specific incidents that develop into shared in-jokes across decades. New Rangers picking up the branch's humor over their first decade of service is a recognized rite of acculturation.

Rangers are well-respected within the broader [[Feywood]] but **less commonly known by face**. Settlement residents may know their Greenshields personally; they typically know the Rangers only as a category — the elves who handle the work that Greenshields don't talk about as much because most of it happens elsewhere. **Rangers who retire to civilian life often settle in smaller [[Feywood]] settlements rather than [[Thornveil]].**

## Relationship to other groups

- **[[Hall of Guides]].** Closest operational counterpart in non-military work. Both navigate the [[Feywood]]'s liminal geometry; both escort sanctioned visitors. **The distinction is stance: [[Hall of Guides]] civilian and non-martial, Rangers military and arms-ready.** Working relationships between specific Rangers and specific Guides are common and often warm.
- **[[Heartwardens]].** Parallel sacred-protection order. Coordination occurs on [[Feywood]]-edge scenarios — particularly when threshold work touches sacred-site implications, or when [[Heartwood]] communications via the root network produce signals that Rangers in outer-range postings detect first.
- **[[Elder Roots]].** Working relationships with druids whose practice intersects Ranger work — particularly outer-range druids whose ecological observation overlaps Ranger reconnaissance.
- **Realm-side forces.** Strictly Council-mediated. Rangers maintain readiness for operational contact but do not conduct it absent Council authorization. **No house affiliation pattern is canonical for the branch;** Rangers come from across the houses and from common origins.
