# The Heartwardens

The elite sacred-guardian order of the [[Heartwood]] and the [[Silent Grove]]. Heartwardens are sworn at the [[Heartwood]]'s base for **one century at a time, renewable**, and serve as the [[Feywood]]'s standing answer to the question of who attends the land's living center when it requires attending. The order is small by design: **roughly one hundred sworn at any time**, sized to the work — continuous watch at the [[Heartwood]], sustained presence at the [[Silent Grove]], training and renewal cycles that keep the order whole across centuries, and reserve depth sufficient for genuine crisis without abandoning home posts. The order's name reflects the tree, not the family; **the oath is the deepest standing commitment in elven civic life**.

## Doctrine

The Heartwardens guard what the [[Feywood]] cannot lose. The [[Heartwood]] and the [[Silent Grove]] are not protected because they are valuable. They are protected because **the [[Feywood]] is the [[Heartwood]]**, and the [[Silent Grove]] is where the elves first settled when they gave their immortality to repair the land. To lose either is to lose what makes the elves of the [[Feywood]] themselves.

**Doctrine is exclusively defensive.** The Heartwardens do not project force, do not pursue, and do not campaign. They guard the [[Heartwood]]'s inner sanctums (the surface clearing, the Council Chamber beneath the tree, the Pool, the cardinal stones), the [[Silent Grove]] (its ancient trees, its training and ceremonial sites, its druidic community), and the rituals bound to both. When the order deploys in arms — as during the [[Discordant War]] — it is because the threat has come close enough to the order's charges that defense at the source is the necessary expression of guardianship.

**Continuity is the doctrine. Presence is the discipline.** The latest hundred sworn carry the work and hand it to the next hundred when their century closes.

## Structure

### The Captain

A single Captain leads the order. The Captaincy rotates among the senior sworn at intervals determined by the [[Council of Elders]] in consultation with the Elder Druid of the [[Heartwood]], typically every two to three centuries. The Captain coordinates with the Council, the Elder Druid, and the [[Greenshields|Greenshield]] Commander; commands the order's deployment in crisis; oversees the rotation schedule; presides at oath-takings; and carries decisions that exceed any single Watch's authority.

**[[Lethariel Heartwood]] currently holds Captaincy in emeritus standing**, supporting the active Captain through the preparation phase and resuming the active role when her family's current Proving candidate (her daughter [[Sylvara Heartwood]]) departs.

### Sworn Heartwardens

The body of the order. **One hundred at any given time**, distributed across active service (~90) and the renewal cycle at the [[Silent Grove]] (~10). Sworn Heartwardens hold equal standing among themselves; differentiation is by experience, specialization, and current post. Senior sworn typically hold instructor postings at the Grove during their renewal years.

### Novices

At any given time, **~15–20 novices in training at the [[Silent Grove]]**. Novices are not yet sworn; their decade of initial training precedes the oath. Some will not complete training. Some will complete training and decline to swear. **The novitiate is the period in which both the candidate and the order test whether the relationship will hold.**

### Emeritus standing

Heartwardens who have completed at least one full century may, at the close of their term, accept emeritus standing rather than renewing. Emeritus members retain their bond to the order, their oath of remembrance to the [[Heartwood]], and their counsel to the Captain and the active body. They do not stand Watch and do not deploy in arms. **[[Lethariel Heartwood|Lethariel]]'s current emeritus standing is a particular case** — she has stepped aside to support her family's Proving candidate, with Captaincy waiting for her return.

### Heartwood-sworn, not house-sworn

The order shares its name with House Heartwood because both take their name from the [[Heartwood]]. **The order is not a household martial body;** [[Noble Families#House Heartwood|House Heartwood]] does not control, command, fund, or constitute it. The shared name is historical proximity, not formal claim.

That said, [[Noble Families#House Heartwood|House Heartwood]] is overrepresented in the order at all levels and disproportionately at senior levels through the [common-but-not-exclusive mechanism]([[Noble Families]]) — generational proximity to the work, ceremonial exposure from childhood, and family-culture transmission. **Selection is by Council judgment, not by family affiliation,** and the order's culture explicitly rejects nepotism in selection. Individual exceptions to the broad pattern are real and ordinary.

## The oath

Taken at the [[Heartwood]]'s base in the surface clearing, in the presence of the Elder Druid, the [[Council of Elders]]' representatives, the assembled Heartwarden community, and any family or community members the candidate has invited as witnesses. The candidate places their hand on the trunk and speaks the oath in Elven; **the words are not transcribed**, by the order's own preference. The oath commits the candidate to **one full century of service** — ten years of training and renewal at the [[Silent Grove]], ninety years of active service.

**The oath is renewable.** At the close of each century, the Heartwarden may renew for another century, accept emeritus standing, or release the bond and return to private life. Most elven Heartwardens renew across multiple centuries; some serve five or six; a small number have served longer than ten in living memory. The century structure preserves the dignity of completion — every Heartwarden has at least one moment of formally completing their oath rather than only ending it.

For non-elves, the oath is takable in principle but unprecedented in practice. The path requires [[Heartwood]] trust, the elven community's trust, sufficient lifespan to commit to a century, and the time and capacity to complete training. **No non-elf in recorded [[Feywood]] history has yet aligned all of these.** The order does not consider this a closure; it considers it an alignment that has not yet happened.

## Training

### The ten-year cycle

Every Heartwarden — novice or returning — spends a decade at the [[Silent Grove]]. Novices complete their initial decade before swearing. Sworn Heartwardens return for a decade at the start of each renewed century. **The structural consequence: the [[Silent Grove]] always holds active Heartwardens in renewal alongside novices in initial training.** Renewing Heartwardens carry the order's accumulated experience into the Grove and pass it to the next generation through ordinary daily presence.

### Novice selection

The order accepts novices on a rolling basis. Selection is conducted by the Captain, the senior instructors at the [[Silent Grove]], and the Elder Druid, with consultation from the [[Council of Elders|Council]] when warranted. The order does not advertise; candidates either present themselves or are recommended by Heartwardens, druids, Council members, or community members of standing.

Eligibility is open to elves who have reached at least young adulthood. **In practice, most candidates accepted are mature adults — typically in their second century or later** — who have lived enough of their adult lives to know their own measure. Young-adult Heartwardens exist and have served well, but are uncommon.

The order looks for: composure under pressure, sustained discipline, demonstrated relationship with the land, willingness to serve where needed rather than where preferred, and the specific quality elves call ***steadiness in waiting*** — the capacity to do nothing for long periods without losing edge. **The work is mostly waiting. Candidates who cannot wait well do not become Heartwardens.**

### Curriculum

Novice decade allocates time across:

- **Combat training.** Bow as primary, close-combat secondary. Defense rather than offense, sustained vigilance rather than tactical maneuver.
- **Land-knowledge.** Druidic foundations sufficient for a Heartwarden to read the order's primary signal sources without needing translation.
- **Ceremonial knowledge.** The rites the order witnesses or supports, including the rare communication ritual at the Pool.
- **Order history and lineage.** Generations of Heartwarden service, recorded events, and lessons preserved in oral tradition. The [[Discordant War]] is studied carefully.
- **Stillness practice.** The contemplative discipline that supports steadiness in waiting. Daily, throughout the decade.
- **Watch participation.** From mid-decade onward, novices serve at the [[Heartwood]] under direct senior supervision.

Renewal decades for sworn Heartwardens are differently structured: skills refreshed rather than learned, time spent on instruction of novices and contemplative practice, and the personal work of preparing to renew or step into emeritus standing.

## Service

The active ninety years of each century rotate across several duties.

### Continuous Heartwood-on-Watch

The [[Heartwood]]'s surface clearing is guarded at all times. **At any moment, between two and four sworn Heartwardens are present at the clearing** — typically two on direct watch and two in nearby support. Watch shifts rotate on an eight-hour cycle with overlap at shift change. Most shifts pass without incident; **the discipline is in the attention itself**.

### Silent Grove presence

A small number of sworn Heartwardens — typically three to five at any time — are posted to the [[Silent Grove]] for instruction, ceremonial support, and coordination with the Druid Master and the broader druidic community.

### Council convenings

When the [[Council of Elders|Council]] descends to the Council Chamber beneath the [[Heartwood]] for matters of land-weight, the order provides ceremonial security — guarding access through the buttress roots, supporting the Council's transit, and witnessing the working without intruding on its substance.

### Communication ritual witnessing

When the [[Heartwood]] drops a branch with a flower and the recipient comes to the Pool to perform the ritual, the order witnesses. **The Heartwarden's role is integrity of the working, not its substance.** They observe that the ritual is conducted properly; they do not participate in the recipient's vision; they do not relay what they witness.

### Response to land-stress

When the [[Feywood]] enters its stress signals, the order coordinates with the druids and the Council on appropriate response. **The order does not deploy in arms unless the stress source is hostile and reachable.** More commonly, the response is increased presence, deeper attention, and tighter coordination with the Elder Druid's interpretation.

### Crisis deployment

In genuine crisis — the [[Discordant War]] being the canonical instance — the order deploys in arms beyond the [[Heartwood]] and [[Silent Grove]]. **Such deployment is rare** and is decided by the Council in consultation with the Captain. During the [[Discordant War]], approximately twenty Heartwardens deployed alongside the larger [[Greenshields|Greenshield]] contingent through the [[Hollow Crown]]'s western threshold; the remaining eighty maintained home posts at full strength.

## Equipment

Traditional elven martial gear, refined for the order's specific work.

The primary weapon is the bow. Most Heartwardens carry a Longbough or comparable family bow earned in their pre-novitiate years. Senior sworn sometimes carry distinctive bows — heirlooms from prior service, gifts from the community, or rare pieces that have come into the order's keeping over centuries. **[[Lethariel Heartwood|Lethariel]]'s [[Heartwood]] bow, cut from the branch the [[Heartwood]] dropped to her during the [[Discordant War]], is the order's only canonical heirloom of supernatural origin;** she carries it during active Captaincy and keeps it at [[Heartwood Estate]] during her current emeritus standing.

Close-combat secondary weapons are individually chosen — typically a sword of moderate length, occasionally a paired set of shorter blades, occasionally a polearm. **The order standardizes competence, not equipment.**

Armor is light. Heartwardens wear elven layered cloth-and-leather appropriate to weather and shift, with light bracers and minimal hard pieces. Defense is mobility, awareness, and skill, not encumbrance.

**The order carries no insignia.** Heartwardens are recognized by elves of the [[Feywood]] without need for badge; realm visitors recognize them by context and by the bearing the order's training produces.

## Eligibility

Open to any individual the [[Heartwood]] trusts who can meet the order's other requirements. **The [[Heartwood]]'s trust is not species-bound, and the order's official position is that no species barrier exists in principle.** In practice, the order has been entirely elven across recorded [[Feywood]] history. The order does not consider this a closure; if a non-elf candidate were to present themselves with the [[Heartwood]]'s trust established and the elven community's recognition secured, the order would conduct the same selection process.

## Relationship to other groups

- **[[Greenshields]].** Parallel defensive order — settlement defense, daily security, patrols, the realm-to-realm border. Much larger and carries the bulk of the [[Feywood]]'s standing arms. **The two orders are parallel, not nested;** neither commands the other. Captains consult routinely on matters that touch both orders' work.
- **[[Elder Roots]].** Continuous coordination with the druidic community, particularly the Elder Druid of the [[Heartwood]] and the Druid Master of the [[Silent Grove]]. Druids interpret land signals; Heartwardens act on those interpretations when action is needed. **The relationship is operationally close and culturally distinct.**
- **[[Council of Elders]].** The Council holds political authority; the order operates within that authority. The Captain answers to the Council on policy, deployment, and significant ceremonial work. Day-to-day operations are not Council-directed.

## Notable Heartwardens

### [[Lethariel Heartwood]]

Captain emeritus. [[Discordant War]] commander. Currently in residence at [[Heartwood Estate]] in House Heartwood's Outer Old-Growth Wild seat, supporting [[Sylvara Heartwood|Sylvara]]'s preparation phase. Will resume active Captaincy when [[Sylvara Heartwood|Sylvara]] departs for the Proving.

**Lethariel is the only Heartwarden in the order's recorded history to have received a [[Heartwood]] communication via the branch-and-Pool ritual during her service.** The branch fell at the Council Chamber convening that deadlocked on the [[Oath of the Fallen]]'s mutual-aid clause; Lethariel performed the ritual, received the vision, and the vision overrode the deadlock. The [[Feywood]] deployed. She led the contingent through the [[Hollow Crown]]'s western threshold and fought alongside [[The Platinum Warden|the Platinum Warden]] through the war's final days. She was at the pit's edge when [[The Platinum Warden|Vindrael]] gave his life. The Heartwood-branch bow she carries was cut from the branch the [[Heartwood]] had dropped to her — the only heirloom of its kind in the order's history.
