# The Hall of Trade

The [[Feywood]]'s institutional answer to the practical question of how realm-Feywood trade actually works. **A working organization** with warehouses, inventory ledgers, established protocols, scheduled exchanges, and the people who do the work of moving goods between two sovereign realms across centuries of continuous practice. Most of what the Hall does is unromantic: receiving goods, recording their movement, coordinating their passage, ensuring that what enters and what leaves does so on terms both realms recognize. **The [[Feywood]] and the realm have traded with each other since the founding;** the Hall is the institution that has carried that relationship in its operational details for that entire span.

## Note on naming

The **Hall of Trade** is the organization. The **[[Trade Hall]]** is a building in [[Vaelmere]] that serves as the organization's headquarters. **Building and institution are distinct;** when context risks confusion, use the full names.

## Doctrine

The Hall of Trade exists to operate the [[Feywood]]'s external commerce. The [[Feywood]] produces goods that the realm wants, the realm produces goods the [[Feywood]] wants, and both realms have agreed since the [[Oath of the Fallen]] that this exchange is one of the genuine goods of their alliance.

The Hall's doctrinal stance is **competent, attentive, unhurried trade**. The Hall does not seek growth for its own sake. It does not extract maximum value from every exchange. It does not optimize aggressively. **It conducts trade in the manner the [[Feywood]] considers appropriate to a long-term sovereign-equal relationship:** with care, with patience, with attention to the relationship as much as to the transaction.

The Hall handles:

- **Outbound trade.** Goods bound from the [[Feywood]] for the realm — craftwork from [[Thornveil]]'s [[Crafthall]], herbal preparations and specimens from across the [[Feywood]], specific magical materials, sanctioned timber and craft materials from [[Noble Families#House Ironsap|House Ironsap]] channels, specialty produce, and the rarer goods the [[Feywood]] is known to produce. The Hall aggregates these goods at [[Vaelmere]], prepares them for exchange, and conducts the [[Feywood Border|Border]]-side handoff.
- **Inbound trade.** Goods arriving from the realm at [[Feywood Border|Border]] exchanges. The Hall receives them at [[Vaelmere]], processes them through inventory, and distributes them onward — to [[Thornveil]]'s markets, to specific noble families or institutions with standing orders, to the [[Feywood]]'s broader elven community through [[Vaelmere]]'s own markets and through the distribution caravans that reach the smaller [[Feywood]] settlements.
- **Exchange protocols.** The procedural work of how [[Feywood Border|Border]] exchanges actually function. The protocols have evolved across centuries through accumulated practice; the Hall maintains them, updates them when conditions warrant, and trains its members in their proper execution.
- **Trade Envoy diplomacy.** The Hall's senior members who present at exchange windows are the [[Feywood]]'s most experienced realm-facing diplomats outside the Council level. **The Trade Envoy corps is the Hall's most prestigious internal track.**

### The information question

The Hall does not maintain an intelligence service. **But trade work, conducted continuously across centuries with realm-side counterparts and through [[Feywood Border|Border]] exchange windows that produce sustained observation of realm conditions, naturally accumulates information.** Hall members develop deep awareness of realm trade patterns, political shifts, economic conditions, cultural developments, and the various other realm-side facts that affect how exchanges unfold. This awareness is real and valuable. **It flows through the Hall's natural channels** — to the Trade Master, to the [[Vaelmere]] community at large, to the [[Council of Elders]] through informal advisory channels, to House Vaelaryn family elders, and to specific other interested parties when the awareness touches their work. **It is not gathered as intelligence. It accumulates as a byproduct of attention.**

This distinction is doctrinal. **The Hall does not have spies. The Hall has elves who pay attention, and paying attention is what trade work requires.**

## Leadership

### The Trade Master

The Hall's senior figure. Oversees the entire Hall's operations: setting policy, managing Trade Envoy assignments, maintaining institutional relationships with realm-side counterparts at the Council-recognized level, coordinating with the [[Council of Elders]] on matters touching trade-realm relations, and serving as the Hall's primary public face within the [[Feywood]] and within [[Vaelmere]]'s civic life.

The Trade Master is selected by the [[Council of Elders]] in consultation with the [[Vaelmere]] community and the senior figures of [[Noble Families#House Vaelaryn|House Vaelaryn]]. **The role typically goes to a senior Trade Envoy with substantial career experience and demonstrated capability for institutional leadership.** House Vaelaryn members hold the role disproportionately through generational trade-diplomacy tradition.

Based at [[Vaelmere]]'s [[Trade Hall]] building (the canonical structure in [[Vaelmere]]'s Central Commons). Tenures are typically fifty to a hundred fifty years.

### Senior Trade Envoys

Veterans of long Trade Envoy service — typically a century or more of work at exchange windows. **~5–8 Senior Trade Envoys active at any given time.**

### Trade Envoys

The body of the Hall's diplomatic-operational arm. **~20–25 Trade Envoys** hold active service at any given time, conducting [[Feywood Border|Border]] exchanges on the Hall's regular schedule and managing the day-to-day diplomatic register of trade work. Drawn from senior members of the Hall's other operational tracks who have demonstrated diplomatic aptitude and have completed the additional Envoy-track training.

### Distribution Coordinators and Operational Staff

**The Hall's working middle layer.** Distribution Coordinators manage the inbound-flow logistics. Operational staff — warehouse workers, inventory specialists, caravan personnel, exchange-team support — comprise the bulk of the Hall's headcount and conduct the practical work that keeps trade flowing.

## Working sites

### Vaelmere — The Trade Hall building

**The Hall's primary operational headquarters.** Located in [[Vaelmere]]'s Central Commons. Holds the Trade Master's offices, the principal warehouses for goods in transit, the inventory records, the Trade Envoy corps' working spaces, and the operational coordination center. **~80 members stationed at [[Vaelmere]]'s [[Trade Hall]]** at any given time.

The building's character is workmanlike. Visitors see the practical work of a logistics operation — goods being received and inventoried, traders conferring over manifests, distribution teams preparing for caravan departures, and the steady hum of trade activity at the [[Feywood]]'s characteristic restrained register.

### The Border — Exchange operations

Continuous Hall presence at the realm-to-realm [[Feywood Border|Border]]. **~25 members** stationed at Border exchange operations at any given time, working alongside the [[Greenshields|Greenshield]] Border garrison and the specialist [[Rangers|Ranger]] threshold-watch contingent. Includes Trade Envoys conducting current exchanges or preparing for upcoming ones, exchange-team support staff handling the practical movement of goods at the threshold, and coordination personnel managing the exchange schedule with realm-side counterparts.

**The work develops deep familiarity with realm trade culture and is the principal informal source of the Hall's accumulated realm awareness.**

### Thornveil — Coordination office

A small Hall presence in [[Thornveil]]'s [[Outer Bounds]] district, where high-level coordination work with the [[Council of Elders]], the [[Heartwood Approach]] noble houses, and the broader civic structure is conducted. **~10 members serve at the [[Thornveil]] coordination office** at any given time, including a Senior Trade Envoy serving as the Hall's permanent [[Thornveil]] liaison.

### Distribution — Outer Feywood

Distributed Hall presence across the broader [[Feywood]] for inbound-goods distribution. **~30 members operate as distribution coordinators and caravan personnel**, moving goods from [[Vaelmere]]'s [[Trade Hall]] to smaller [[Feywood]] settlements, specific noble estates, and outlying communities.

### Reserve and operational depth

Total Hall presence including all categories: **~160–180 individuals** at any time.

## Operations

### The outbound flow

Goods bound from the [[Feywood]] for realm trade originate across the [[Feywood]]'s various productive sites. The Hall's distribution coordinators aggregate outbound goods to [[Vaelmere]], where the [[Trade Hall]]'s warehouses prepare them for upcoming [[Feywood Border|Border]] exchanges. Trade Envoys conduct the actual exchange handoffs at scheduled Border windows.

**The outbound flow is calibrated.** The [[Feywood]] does not maximize export volume; it sustains an export level that the [[Council of Elders]] considers appropriate to the broader trade relationship without compromising the [[Feywood]]'s own consumption needs or its specific resource-stewardship principles. **[[Noble Families#House Ironsap|House Ironsap]]'s "restraint in material culture" doctrine applies particularly to outbound timber and craft materials** — the Hall does not export beyond what the [[Feywood]]'s careful sourcing produces.

### The inbound flow

Goods arriving from the realm at [[Feywood Border|Border]] exchanges flow inward through the Hall's distribution network. [[Vaelmere]]'s [[Trade Hall]] receives, inventories, and processes everything that enters; from there, distribution coordinators move goods onward to their various destinations.

Inbound goods include realm-produced craft materials and tools that the [[Feywood]] does not produce, specific metals and minerals not available in the [[Feywood]]'s geography, certain food specialties, realm fabrics, written materials in realm languages (rare but present, primarily for [[Noble Families#House Greybranch|House Greybranch]]'s archival work), and the various other goods that sustained external commerce produces.

### Exchange windows

[[Feywood Border|Border]] exchanges occur on a regular schedule — **typically two to three windows per [[Feywood]] season**, with additional windows scheduled when circumstances warrant. Each window is conducted by Trade Envoys from both sides at the threshold itself, with goods passed through in pre-coordinated lots, recordings made by both sides, and the specific protocols of cross-realm transfer observed.

**Exchange windows are calm by design.** The protocols are well-established; the counterparts on the realm side (typically [[Silver Scale Trading Company|SSTC]] senior personnel) are familiar; the work proceeds with the unhurried character that long practice produces.

### The Trade Envoy corps

Trade Envoys are selected from Hall members who have demonstrated aptitude across multiple operational tracks and who have completed additional Envoy-track training that includes deeper realm-trade-culture instruction, the diplomatic register specific to mixed-sovereign exchange work, sustained Border experience under existing Envoy supervision, and the specific skill of conducting trade conversation that maintains the [[Feywood]]'s posture without straining the relationship.

**Trade Envoy careers are long.** New Envoys typically serve in junior exchange-window roles for decades before moving to lead Envoy work; **Senior Envoys carry the most consequential exchanges and the standing-relationship work with realm-side counterparts who have come to know them by name across centuries.**

## House Vaelaryn association

[[Noble Families#House Vaelaryn|House Vaelaryn]]'s prominence in the Hall is canonical and runs through generational trade-diplomacy tradition, family-culture transmission of realm-awareness skills, and accumulated knowledge passed down through generations of senior Hall service. The Hall's senior ranks — particularly the Trade Master role and the Senior Trade Envoy positions — draw disproportionately from Vaelaryn members.

**Common but not exclusive.** Other houses contribute their own members across all the Hall's tracks, and senior posts have come from many houses across the Hall's history.

**House Vaelaryn's spycraft specialty manifests in the Hall through the natural information-flow culture, not through any formalized intelligence function.** Vaelaryn members in the Hall are particularly attentive to the realm-awareness aspects of trade work; the family's culture trains its members to notice realm patterns, to remember what counterparts say, to read the small signals of realm-side change. **This produces a Vaelaryn-led Hall that is realm-aware to a degree pure trade work would not require** — but the awareness is integrated into ordinary Hall operations rather than separated into a distinct intelligence track.

The [[Council of Elders]] is aware of and accepts this arrangement. **The [[Feywood]] does not maintain a separate intelligence service. The Hall, structured this way, provides what the Council needs.**

## Membership and training

### Membership

Open to elves of the [[Feywood]] who present aptitude and pass the apprenticeship. [[Vaelmere]] residents are common candidates given their proximity to the Hall's primary operations.

**Non-elven membership in the Hall is the most common across all [[Feywood]] orders and communities.** The integration patterns of [[Vaelmere]] — which has the highest non-elven proportion in the [[Feywood]] at five to ten percent of residents — produce non-elven candidates who present for Hall work, and the Hall accepts capable candidates regardless of species. **Non-elven members work primarily in operational tracks** (warehouse, inventory, distribution, Border-team support) **rather than in Trade Envoy roles**, which require the cultural depth that decades of [[Feywood]]-resident experience tend to produce more readily in elves.

### Training

Initial Hall apprenticeship runs **three to five years**. New members are introduced to the operational tracks, complete rotational assignments to develop breadth, and identify the specialty path their work will take. **Those identified for Trade Envoy track receive an additional five to ten years of specialist training.**

Continuing development is ongoing throughout Hall service. Senior Trade Envoys mentor newer Envoys through extended apprentice arrangements.

## Relationship to other groups

- **[[Greenshields]] (Vaelmere outer-rotation, Border garrison).** Operational coordination at [[Vaelmere]] and the [[Feywood Border|Border]]. Hall personnel and Greenshield personnel often work alongside one another at the Border without functional disagreement.
- **[[Rangers]].** Specific coordination at the Border on threshold-watch matters that touch trade operations, and on military-coded escort work for Hall personnel when warranted (rare).
- **[[Hall of Guides]].** Coordination on sanctioned-visitor escort. Realm visitors traveling for trade business are most often escorted by the Hall of Guides through the [[Feywood]]'s geometry; the Hall of Trade hosts them at [[Vaelmere]] or arranges their onward business.
- **[[Council of Elders]].** Reporting and consultation. The Trade Master reports to the Council on matters of trade policy, significant exchange events, and realm-condition awareness when it warrants Council attention.
- **[[Noble Families#House Vaelaryn|House Vaelaryn]].** Closest non-institutional relationship. House Vaelaryn provides the Hall's largest pool of senior members; the family's elders maintain customary communication channels with Hall senior members. **The Hall is not a Vaelaryn possession;** the working relationship is deep and continuous.
- **Other houses.** All seven major houses contribute Hall members across operational tracks. Specific houses have specific recurring intersections: [[Noble Families#House Ironsap|House Ironsap]] members often work in the outbound-timber and craft-materials flow; [[Noble Families#House Silverbough|House Silverbough]] members sometimes work in outbound wild-harvest goods coordination; [[Noble Families#House Dawnveil|House Dawnveil]] members occasionally work in ceremonial-flower exchange.
- **The [[Silver Scale Trading Company]].** The Hall's principal realm-side counterpart for ordinary trade. **The institutional relationship is centuries old and well-developed.** The Hall and the SSTC maintain working trust without naive alignment. The Hall is aware of the SSTC's broader institutional intersections and incorporates that awareness into the Hall's general realm-facing posture without making it a matter of specific operational concern.
