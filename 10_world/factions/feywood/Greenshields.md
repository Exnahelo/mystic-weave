# The Greenshields

The [[Feywood]]'s primary defensive military order, and the order most [[Feywood]] elves know personally. Where the [[Heartwardens]] guard the sacred center, **the Greenshields hold the practical perimeter** — the settlements, the patrol-paths, the border, the daily security work that keeps [[Feywood]] life livable. The order is large by elven standards (though small by realm reckoning), distributed across multiple posts, and structured to sustain continuous defensive presence indefinitely. Greenshield service is the most common path for [[Feywood]] elves who choose to serve in arms; **most elves of the [[Feywood]] have at least one family member who has served, currently serves, or will eventually serve in the order**.

## Doctrine

The Greenshields exist to defend the [[Feywood]] — the land, the people, the settlements, and the relationships that constitute the [[Feywood]] as it is. **The framing is deliberately practical and strictly defensive.** The order does not project elven power, does not deter realm-side forces, does not maintain a martial culture for its own sake, and does not seek combat. When combat comes, the order meets it competently and decisively; when it does not, the order does its work in the absence of combat without seeking to manufacture it.

The doctrine breaks into four pillars:

- **Settlement defense.** Continuous protection of [[Thornveil]], [[Vaelmere]], the smaller [[Feywood]] settlements, and the outlying noble estates. **This is most of what the order does on most days** — presence, patrol, response, the practical maintenance of safety in places where elves live their lives.
- **Border watch.** Protection of the elven side of the [[Feywood Border|realm-to-realm border]]. **The Border is not a wall the order mans; it is a threshold the order attends.** Border-facing Greenshields read what crosses, who crosses, what does not cross that should have, and what crosses that should not have.
- **Interior patrol.** Movement through the [[Feywood]] interior on regular patrol patterns, maintaining presence on the paths between settlements, attending to ecological signals the druids identify, keeping the order familiar with the land and the land familiar with the order.
- **Crisis deployment.** When defense at the settlements or the border is insufficient — when the threat has come deeper than perimeter work can hold, or when external events require the [[Feywood]] to deploy beyond its own borders under the [[Oath of the Fallen]] — the Greenshields are the bulk of the deployable force. **Crisis deployments are rare; the [[Discordant War]] is the canonical instance.**

The order's stance toward realm-side forces is **strictly cooperative and strictly bounded**. The Greenshields do not coordinate operationally with the [[Dragon Guard]], the [[Wardens]], or any other realm body in routine work. Coordination occurs at Council-to-Council level under the [[Oath of the Fallen]]. The two realms defend separately and aid each other in true crisis, exactly as the Oath specifies.

## Structure

### The Commander

A single Commander leads the order, based at the [[Thornveil]] headquarters. Selected by the [[Council of Elders]] from among the order's most senior career officers. The Commander holds overall authority for doctrine, deployment, posting decisions, training oversight, and coordination with the Council, the Heartwarden Captain, the Ranger branch leadership, and (through Council channels) realm-side military authorities. Tenures of one to three centuries are typical.

[[Noble Families#House Thornmere|House Thornmere]] is overrepresented in the Commandership through the [common-but-not-exclusive mechanism]([[Noble Families]]) — generational defensive-vinework tradition, [[Thornveil]] founding-mythic association, and family-culture transmission. **Common but not exclusive;** the role is not house-bound.

### Captains

Senior officers commanding specific posts, units, or branches:

- **Senior Captain of [[Thornveil]]** — [[Thornveil]] settlement defense, headquarters operations, central training cadre.
- **Outer-Rotation Captain** — [[Vaelmere]] outer-rotation post, Border-facing operations conducted from [[Vaelmere]], realm-facing diplomatic-security interface.
- **Captain of the Border Garrison** — continuous border-watch presence at the realm-to-realm threshold itself, distinct from [[Vaelmere]]-based outer-rotation.
- **Patrol Captain** — rotating interior patrol units between the named posts.
- **Captain of the Ranger Branch** — the order's specialist outer-range and threshold-watch arm; see [[Rangers]].
- **Captain of Training** — [[Thornveil]]-based initial training program for new members and continuing-skill maintenance.

Captain tenures are typically half a century to a century and a half before rotation to other posts or to senior advisory roles.

### Senior Sergeants and Lieutenants

The order's working middle layer. **The order draws no sharp line between the two in cultural standing;** both hold real authority and both are understood as the layer where the order's effectiveness actually lives.

### Sworn Greenshields

The body of the order. **Approximately five hundred at any given time**, distributed across the various posts and units. Sworn Greenshields hold equal standing among themselves; differentiation is by post, specialty, experience, and the particular work the individual is suited to.

### Recruits

Approximately fifty to seventy recruits in initial training at [[Thornveil]] at any given time. Either party may end the path before the oath.

### The Ranger Branch

A specialist branch of the order, organizationally distinct but operationally part of the Greenshields. **Rangers are not separate from the Greenshields;** they are sworn Greenshields who have completed additional specialist training. See [[Rangers]] for full detail. **Ranger numbers are included in the order's overall ~600 active count.**

## Service terms

The Greenshield oath is taken at [[Thornveil]] headquarters at the close of initial training. The candidate stands before the Commander or a designated Senior Captain, speaks the oath in Elven, receives their first formal assignment, and takes their place in the order.

The oath commits the candidate to **fifty years of initial service**. After fifty years, service continues at-pleasure with no formal renewal cycle. **Most career members serve a century to several centuries; some serve longer.** The order's oldest currently-active Sergeants and Lieutenants have served seven hundred years or more.

**Veteran's standing** is a recognized status among Greenshields who have completed at least their initial fifty years. Veterans who have transitioned out of active service retain a formal connection to the order, may be called for consultation, retain access to community functions, and in extreme crisis may be recalled.

For non-elven members — uncommon but not unprecedented — the fifty-year initial commitment is a meaningful fraction of most non-elven lifespans, and the order recognizes this in post and assignment decisions.

## Training

### Initial training

**Five years** at [[Thornveil]] headquarters with rotations through [[Vaelmere]] and the Border garrison. Structured but not regimented in the realm military sense; the order's culture preserves elven thoughtfulness even within the practical work of preparing soldiers.

- **Year one** — foundations: physical conditioning, basic combat, weapons handling, doctrine, geography and ecology of the [[Feywood]], command structure, practical literacy.
- **Year two** — competence: deeper combat training, specialty introductions, first practical assignments under direct senior supervision.
- **Year three** — application: operational training in real conditions, including extended [[Vaelmere]] rotations, border-garrison rotations, and patrol unit rotations.
- **Year four** — selection: specialist tracks divide. Most recruits continue toward general sworn standing; those showing aptitude for Ranger work begin the branch's selection process.
- **Year five** — integration: final operational year before swearing, with assignments that closely approximate the recruit's intended post-swearing role.

### Continuing service

Greenshields maintain their skills through continuous practical service rather than periodic refresher training.

## Posts

### Thornveil headquarters and settlement defense

The order's command center and largest single post. **~150 Greenshields stationed at [[Thornveil]]** at any given time, distributed across headquarters command, settlement defense rotations, training cadre, and central reserve. **[[Thornveil]] settlement defense work is mostly low-tempo;** the city has no walls or gates and is defended through visibility, presence, and the [[Feywood]]'s broader liminal protection.

### Vaelmere outer-rotation

The order's realm-facing operational center. **~100 Greenshields stationed at [[Vaelmere]]**, commanded by the Outer-Rotation Captain. Includes settlement defense for [[Vaelmere]] itself, realm-facing diplomatic-security work supporting trade and visitor flow, and operational coordination with the Border garrison. **Tours typically last several years to a decade.**

### Border garrison

Continuous presence at the realm-to-realm threshold. **~50 Greenshields** assigned to the Border garrison at any given time, commanded by the Captain of the Border Garrison. Border-garrison work is **specialized and demanding**; rotations are typically shorter than other postings — two to four years — because the work's specific intensity rewards fresh attention.

### Feywood interior patrols

**~100 Greenshields in patrol units** at any given time, organized into rotating patrol companies under the Patrol Captain. Companies typically operate in groups of four to eight, conducting circuits that range from days to weeks. **Patrol work is the order's most distinctively elven posting;** patrol rotations are typically multi-year.

### The Ranger branch

See [[Rangers]]. **~150 active Rangers within the order's overall ~600 count.**

### Smaller settlement presence

The [[Feywood]] maintains presence at several smaller settlements beyond [[Thornveil]] and [[Vaelmere]]. Typically a small detachment of three to six Greenshields, often integrated with the local community in a way that blurs the line between order presence and resident neighbor.

### Reserve and training depth

Beyond actively-stationed Greenshields, the order maintains structural depth: recruits in initial training (~50–70), specialist trainees in advanced programs, and the reserve of veterans on call for crisis deployment. **In a [[Discordant War]]-scale event, the order can mobilize from ~600 to perhaps ~800 within days through veteran recall**, and to a higher number through extended mobilization.

## Equipment

More variable than the [[Heartwardens]]', reflecting the greater diversity of the order's posts and roles.

The standard primary weapon is the bow. Most Greenshields carry a Longbough or comparable family bow in active service; the order does not standardize bow type, but training maintains consistent capability across the order. Specialist roles may carry alternate primary weapons.

Close-combat secondary weapons are individually chosen. Swords are common; spears and staves are present in specific roles.

**Shields are the order's distinctive equipment and the source of the name.** The order maintains a tradition of small-to-medium round shields used for settlement-defense work, ceremonial standing post, and certain formation-defense applications. Not every Greenshield carries a shield in every role — patrol work, Ranger work, and most border-garrison work do without — but **the shield is closely associated with the order in elven cultural recognition**.

Armor varies by role. Settlement-defense Greenshields wear medium-weight elven armor; patrol Greenshields wear lighter kit; Border-garrison Greenshields wear specialized threshold-appropriate armor. **The order does not wear distinctive uniform in daily work;** Greenshields are recognized by context, weapon, shield (when present), and bearing.

## Eligibility and recruitment

Open to elves of the [[Feywood]] who have reached at least young adulthood. **Unlike the [[Heartwardens]], the Greenshields routinely accept young-adult recruits** — the order is the most common path of first service for elves coming of age, and the recruit cohort each year is typically dominated by young adults rather than older candidates.

The order looks for: physical capability, mental composure under sustained pressure, willingness to follow the order's doctrine, demonstrated relationship with the [[Feywood]] land, and ***steadiness in the unremarkable*** — the capacity to do useful work in conditions that will mostly never become exciting.

**Non-elven eligibility** is the same in principle as for the [[Heartwardens]]: open to any individual the order judges suitable. **Non-elven Greenshields are uncommon but not unprecedented.** A small number of integrated [[Vaelmere]] residents have served across the centuries, primarily in [[Vaelmere]] outer-rotation roles. **The order's culture treats non-elven members as full Greenshields once sworn.**

The order does not inherit posts in family lines. **The order's culture is explicitly anti-nepotistic on this point.**

## The Discordant War deployment

The Greenshields contributed approximately **180 members** to the [[Feywood]]'s 200-elf contingent during the [[Discordant War]]: ~150 general Greenshield personnel and ~30 Ranger-branch members, alongside the ~20 Heartwardens. The combined contingent under [[Lethariel Heartwood]]'s command operated as one force in the field, with the Heartwarden contingent embedded in the larger Greenshield deployment but retaining separate identity and chain. **Greenshield casualties during the deployment were approximately fifteen percent** of the deployed force, and the order's recovery from those losses occupied its institutional attention for decades after the war's end.

**The [[Discordant War]] deployment is the only time the Greenshields have deployed under the [[Oath of the Fallen]]'s mutual-aid clause.** The order maintains the readiness for similar deployment as a continuous structural commitment.

## Cultural character

**Broadly accessible, broadly respected, broadly familiar.** Most [[Feywood]] elves have at least one family member who has served, currently serves, or will eventually serve in the order. Greenshield service is woven into ordinary civic life in a way that the more set-apart [[Heartwardens|Heartwarden]] order is not.

Internal culture is **practical, plainspoken, and humorously self-aware**. Settlement-defense rotations develop their own micro-cultures around specific posts. Patrol companies develop running jokes, traditions, and shared observational stories from years on the same routes. Border-garrison Greenshields develop a particular stylistic dryness around the threshold work's strangeness. [[Vaelmere]] outer-rotation Greenshields develop a specific cosmopolitan ease that other postings sometimes find amusing.

Relationship with the [[Heartwardens]] is **friendly but distinct — parallel and culturally separate**. A career Greenshield is not a failed Heartwarden candidate; the orders are different vocations with different selection patterns and different cultural homes.

Relationship with the broader civilian [[Feywood]] is warm and functional. Greenshield Sergeants and Lieutenants are often community figures known by name in the neighborhoods they serve.
