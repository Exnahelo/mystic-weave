# Draconic Council

## Overview

The Draconic Council is the supreme governing authority of [[Drakenvale]]. Three ancient dragons rule by consensus. Major decisions of lasting political, legal, military, or theological consequence require unanimous approval. When the Council cannot reach consensus, disputes may be referred to the [[Heartstone]], the radiant artifact housed at the center of the [[Draconic Hall]].

## Purpose

To preserve the sanctuary, uphold the [[Codex of Remembrance]], and maintain the harmony among dragons, drakari, kobolds, and mortals that the [[Oath of the Fallen]] established. The Council exists because dragons who once destroyed civilizations chose to build something else instead.

## Structure

Three ancient dragons hold the seats:

| Dragon | Alignment | Domain | Commands |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Eryndor the Radiant]] | Lawful Good | Justice, diplomacy, moral guidance | [[Wardens]] (peacetime), [[Platinum Acolytes]] |
| [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] | Lawful Evil | Strength, defense, resources | [[Dragon Guard]], Wardens (crisis) |
| [[Varethyn of the Amethyst Gaze]] | Neutral | Wisdom, arcane knowledge, balance | [[Arcane Conservatory]], [[Amethyst Veil]] |

All three hold equal weight in deliberation. Unanimous approval is required for major decisions. In cases of true deadlock, the [[Heartstone]] may be invoked for guidance.

## The Draconic Conclave

A larger gathering of all resident dragons, convened approximately every century or when major structural questions or external threats demand it. The Conclave debates amendments to the [[Codex of Remembrance]] and addresses sanctuary-wide concerns too large for ordinary Council process. Its timing is not fixed — it is called by Council decree.

In ordinary governance, only the Council votes. In a Conclave, all dragons in good standing may participate and vote.

## Holdings

- [[Draconic Hall]] — primary seat of governance, houses the [[Heartstone]]
- Authority over the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]]'s civic functions
- Ultimate jurisdiction over the [[Codex of Remembrance]]

## Relationships

- [[Drakari Stewards]] — direct civic enforcement arm
- [[Platinum Acolytes]] — spiritual order most closely aligned with [[Eryndor the Radiant]]
- [[Dragon Guard]] — military force under [[Zarkeros the Inferno]]
- [[Wardens]] — internal security; commanded by Eryndor in peacetime, Zarkeros in crisis
- [[Feywood]] Council of Elders — sovereign-equal partner under the [[Oath of the Fallen]]

## Doctrine

The Council operates by consensus and restraint. The [[Codex of Remembrance]] guides every formal ruling. Power is to be wielded for the greater good; sanctuary secrecy is foundational; redemption is preferred to execution.

## Internal tensions

The legacy of the [[Discordant War]] leaves an unspoken layer of caution and distrust around questions of loyalty, especially in matters touching the [[Temple of Mordrax]], secrecy, or old fault lines within the realm. This distrust is cultural rather than legal.

The three sitting dragons represent three different alignments and three different governing instincts. Their unanimous-approval requirement is both the source of the Council's stability and the source of its slowness.
