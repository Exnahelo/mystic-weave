# The Dragon Guard

## Overview

[[Drakenvale]]'s primary external defense force. The Dragon Guard is responsible for external defense, border security, aerial response, major threat mobilization, and military deterrence.

## Purpose

To defend the [[Hollow Crown]] from external threat and to project force when the [[Draconic Council]] determines force is necessary. The Guard's authority is strongest at the edges of the basin, along dangerous corridors, and in periods of overt crisis.

## Structure

**Type:** Military.

**Leadership:** [[Zarkeros the Inferno]].

**Composition:** drakari warriors, kobold engineers, mortal mages, dragons of varying alignments, mounted and aerial assets, and reserve support forces.

The Dragon Guard is the only major force directly commanded by a single Council member in both peace and crisis. This concentration of authority is a deliberate feature — external defense requires unity of command — and a recognized risk: a single dragon's military discretion is a load-bearing assumption.

## Holdings

- The outer perimeter of the [[Hollow Crown]] and its safe-routing infrastructure
- Aerial deployment capacity
- Strategic logistics tied to the [[Volcanic Highlands]] and the [[Infernal Forge]]

## Relationships

- [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] — direct commander
- [[Draconic Council]] — operates under Council mandate; mobilization for sustained external operations requires Council unanimity
- [[Wardens]] — distinct internal force; jurisdictions overlap during emergencies
- [[Infernal Forge Guild]] — supplies enchanted weaponry, armor, and strategic materiel

## Doctrine

Deterrence first, decisive force when necessary. The Guard does not exist to win wars after they begin — it exists to make the [[Hollow Crown]] too costly to attack in the first place. Visible discipline, coordinated drilling, and ceremonial presence at festivals and major rites are part of that deterrent, not separate from it.

## Internal tensions

The Guard's command-of-one structure under [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] is a settled feature of the realm but a recurring concern in [[Draconic Conclave]] discussions. Critics from within the moral-philosophical wing of the sanctuary worry about the realm's reliance on a single Council member for external force. Defenders point to the alternative — divided command — and note that the [[Discordant War]]'s lessons about unity-in-defense remain unresolved.

The overlap with the [[Wardens]] in emergencies produces operational friction, particularly when the line between "external threat" and "internal corruption with external dimension" is blurred.

## Open questions

- The Guard's standing strength in both routine peacetime and full mobilization
- How the Guard coordinates with the [[Feywood]]'s forces under the [[Oath of the Fallen]]'s mutual-aid clause
- Succession protocol if [[Zarkeros the Inferno]] is incapacitated
