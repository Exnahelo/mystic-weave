# The Platinum Acolytes

## Overview

The spiritual backbone of [[Drakenvale]]'s public sacred life. The Platinum Acolytes maintain sacred rites, public observances, atonement practice, moral instruction, and the routine sacred life of the sanctuary. They tend the [[Platinum Heart]], oversee much of the sanctuary's visible [[Solveris]]-aligned religious life, and help preserve the moral authority of the [[Codex of Remembrance]].

## Purpose

To carry [[Solveris]]-aligned devotion as a lived practice across the sanctuary — public sacred life, ritual atonement, moral instruction, and the institutional memory of the founding ideals expressed in liturgy.

## Structure

**Type:** Religious order.

**Leadership:** Broadly aligned with [[Eryndor the Radiant]]. The Acolytes are not formally commanded by Eryndor — they are an order with their own internal hierarchy — but their doctrinal alignment and Council patronage flow through him.

The order maintains a graduated structure: novices, acolytes proper, high acolytes (such as [[Seraphine of the Still Pool]]), and senior leadership whose interface with the Council runs through Eryndor's sphere.

## Holdings

- The [[Platinum Heart]] — primary sanctum and highest sacred site under their care
- The Platinum Flame — the sacred fire watched continuously
- Public ritual spaces across the [[Stronghold of Drakenvale]] and the city of [[Drakenvale]]
- Atonement and redemption ritual infrastructure throughout the sanctuary

## Relationships

- [[Eryndor the Radiant]] — Council patron and doctrinal anchor
- [[Solveris]] — the deity whose rites they maintain
- [[Wardens]] — coordinated protection of the [[Platinum Heart]] and other sacred sites
- [[Vigil]] — distinct order, but the public-versus-restricted relationship has historical depth; the Acolytes are the visible Solveris-aligned order, the Vigil is the restricted one
- [[Codex of Remembrance]] — the moral framework they preserve through ritual

## Doctrine

Public sacred life is not separate from civic life. The Acolytes hold that [[Solveris]]'s presence in [[Drakenvale]] is sustained by ongoing ritual practice — that ceremonial atonement, formal observance, and moral instruction are not optional supplements to governance but its sacred foundation. The [[Codex of Remembrance]] is, in their reading, the practical liturgy of a sanctuary held together by deliberate sacred work.

## Internal tensions

Their cultural influence is wide and not coercive — they do not hold formal political authority — but their alignment with [[Eryndor the Radiant]] gives them real leverage on the moral-political axis of the realm. Critics from the strength-pragmatist wing of the sanctuary (associated with [[Zarkeros the Inferno]]'s sphere) have historically described the Acolytes as overreaching their non-political mandate. The Acolytes themselves describe their cultural reach as the natural product of doctrinal seriousness, not political ambition.

There is a question, never quite settled in public discourse, about how much the Acolytes know of the [[Vigil]] and the deeper Platinum-aligned tradition. Many Acolytes know nothing; some senior figures know more.

## Open questions

- Whether the order has internal philosophical factions
- How the Acolytes handle [[Mordrax]] in liturgical practice — silence? Ritual refusal? Counter-invocation?
