# Halflings

## Overview

Halflings arose in the fertile restoration zones — the lands where post-[[Cataclysm]] healing took most fully. They are the people of hearth, field, kinship, and ordinary joy. Their cultural identity is built less around grand cosmic narratives than around the miracle that life, home, and small joy survived at all.

## Origin

Halflings emerged in the post-[[Cataclysm]] lands where the celestial restoration spread most fully and life returned most generously. They are children of the fertile aftermath, shaped by abundance rather than by hardship or by sacrifice.

## Cataclysm memory

Halflings generally believe the world's great truth is not catastrophe but continuation. Their traditions often downplay grand cosmic explanations in favor of the miracle that life, hearth, field, kinship, and ordinary joy survived at all. In their telling, [[The Cataclysm]] matters because the world did not end completely.

> Halflings: life endured, hearth endured, and that is the miracle.

Halfling belief emphasizes continuity, luck, home, fertility, and the stubborn persistence of small life. They are likely to frame the post-Cataclysm world not as tragedy alone, but as proof that gentleness and ordinary living are worth preserving.

## Culture

Defined by hearth and home rather than by grand narrative. Halfling culture emphasizes hospitality, fertility, family network, the small festivals that mark seasons and lives, and the conviction that ordinary joy is itself a sacred thing. Halfling diplomacy tends toward kindness as practice rather than principle — they are not easily provoked into formal stances.

This is not naïveté. Halfling history includes its own losses, its own political conflicts, and its own difficult choices. The cultural emphasis on continuity is the consequence of those losses, not the absence of them.

## Distribution

In the fertile lands across the world, particularly the rolling agricultural regions where post-[[Cataclysm]] healing took most fully. Halfling presence within [[Drakenvale]] is steady but unobtrusive — halflings settle in the [[Hollow Crown]]'s agricultural communities, market towns, and lakefront districts.

## Magical character

Halflings work within the [[Nine Magical Fields]] but tend to favor Druidry, Sacred (in the gentler aspects), and small-scale practical workings. Halfling magical traditions emphasize blessing, ward of home, hearth-fire, and the magic of the threshold-protected. Halflings tend not to seek mastery in dangerous fields.

The [[Unchanged]] — the god of thresholds and small offerings — has more living folk-practice presence among halflings than among most other peoples.

## Relationships with other peoples

- [[Humans]] — often in shared communities; deep cooperative tradition
- [[Elves]] — generally cooperative; mutual respect across different framings of restoration
- [[Drakari]] and [[Drakenvale]] — present in the sanctuary's civic life, integrated without difficulty
- [[Orcs]] — limited contact; differing fundamental orientations
- [[Gnomes]] — frequently allied or neighbored; complementary temperaments
