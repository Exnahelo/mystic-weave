# Drakari

## Overview

Drakari emerged from the old mingling of [[Dragons]] and mortals, carrying draconic inheritance into the new age. They are a bloodline people — their identity bound to descent from dragons and to the magical affinity that descent produces. Drakari are central to [[Drakenvale]]'s civic order; they are the closest civic inheritors of draconic culture, governance, and military service, and they hold naturally high standing within the sanctuary's social structure.

## Origin

Drakari trace their origin to ancient mingling between dragons and mortals. They predate [[The Cataclysm]] in some lineages and emerged in others through post-Cataclysm circumstance. What distinguishes the Drakari is the carrying-forward of draconic inheritance through bloodline — not the wholesale transformation of a single mortal generation, but the multi-generational presence of dragon-blood in their ancestry.

## Cataclysm memory

Drakari generally believe history cannot be understood without dragons. Their traditions often frame the world not only through [[The Cataclysm]], but through the role of [[Dragons]] as enduring powers before, during, and after it.

> Drakari: history is bloodline, inheritance, and draconic legacy carried forward.

Because they are a bloodline people, their origin stories are usually personal: lineage, inheritance, and the carrying forward of ancient draconic force into mortal life. Drakari belief emphasizes bloodline, continuity with [[Dragons]], duty, inherited strength, and the idea that history moves through lineages as much as through disasters.

## Culture

Defined by inheritance and duty. Drakari culture takes seriously the obligations that draconic bloodline carries — to honor the lineage, to serve where service is owed, to maintain the discipline that distinguishes inherited strength from inherited entitlement. Drakari military, civic, and ritual traditions reflect this seriousness.

In [[Drakenvale]], Drakari Stewards serve as civic officers, mediators, military leaders, record-keepers, and direct enforcers of [[Draconic Council]] rulings. The role is not purely administrative — it is a sacred-civic responsibility, the link between the [[Draconic Council]]'s draconic authority and the realm's daily governance.

## Distribution

Drakari are concentrated where dragons are present. [[Drakenvale]] is the most consequential Drakari realm — the [[Hollow Crown]] is, in significant ways, a Drakari homeland as well as a draconic one. Other Drakari communities exist elsewhere, typically in proximity to dragons or to ancient draconic sites.

## Magical character

Drakari inherit magical affinity from draconic bloodline. What is passed down is not a fixed universal expression but a natural inclination toward the [[Nine Magical Fields]] most strongly embodied by the dragon in that bloodline. A Drakari descended from a Sacred-aligned lineage will tend toward Sacred-field affinity; a Drakari from an Elemental-aligned lineage will tend toward Elemental.

This inherited inclination is real but not deterministic. A Drakari may study and develop fields outside their bloodline affinity. The inheritance is a starting point, not a ceiling.

## Relationships with other peoples

- [[Dragons]] — ancestral; the relationship is foundational rather than diplomatic
- [[Drakenvale]] — central civic role; Drakari stewardship is structural to the sanctuary
- [[Humans]], [[Halflings]], [[Kobolds]] — coexistence within the sanctuary's civic order
- [[Elves]] of the [[Feywood]] — sovereign-equal allies under the [[Oath of the Fallen]]
- Other peoples — varies by region; Drakari outside [[Drakenvale]] have their own diplomatic histories

## Open questions

- Whether Drakari descended from dragons aligned with the [[Mordraxian Rebels]] still carry social distrust within the sanctuary
- The mechanism by which draconic affinity expresses across multiple generations of distance from the founding dragon
