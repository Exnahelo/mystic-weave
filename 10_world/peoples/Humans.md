# Humans

## Overview

Humans are the old root people of the world — present before [[The Cataclysm]], enduring through it, and rebuilding afterward. They are the most likely of the ancestries to treat [[The Cataclysm]] historically rather than sacredly, though older cults, local traditions, and regional faiths vary widely.

## Origin

Humans existed across the world in many early cultures before [[The Cataclysm]]. They were the dominant mortal presence in the old, mostly-unenchanted age. When the meteor fell, they endured — not transformed by it, not made by it, not given a special role in its aftermath. They were already there when the world changed, and they remained.

## Cataclysm memory

Humans generally believe [[The Cataclysm]] was the great break between the old world and the new. They do not tend to see themselves as chosen, cursed, or specially transformed by it; instead, they view themselves as the people who endured it and rebuilt afterward.

In human telling, the meteor is usually remembered as a divine punishment, a cosmic accident, or the great disaster that forced history to begin again.

> Humans: the world broke, and we rebuilt.

Most human traditions emphasize survival, adaptation, migration, and the founding of new civilizations from the ruins of old ones.

## Culture

Defined by survival and rebuilding rather than by any single grand cosmic narrative. Human culture varies tremendously by region, era, and circumstance — there is no single "human" tradition, only the family resemblance of peoples who endured an event larger than they were and chose to keep going.

## Distribution

Across the world, in many cultures. In [[Drakenvale]], humans are integrated into civic life as one of the founding mortal lineages who entered the [[Oath of the Fallen]]. They may rise to positions of serious responsibility, expertise, and honor, but do not possess a [[Draconic Council]] vote.

## Magical character

Humans do not embody magical fields innately the way [[Dragons]] do. They learn the [[Nine Magical Fields]] through study, practice, and institutional authorization. The [[Hall of Scales]] and [[Arcane Conservatory]] both train human practitioners alongside other ancestries.

## Relationships with other peoples

Generally the most diplomatically flexible ancestry — present in nearly every realm, allied or in conflict with each of the other peoples in different times and places. In [[Drakenvale]] specifically, humans coexist alongside [[Drakari]], [[Kobolds]], and the broader sanctuary order.
