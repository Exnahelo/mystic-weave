# Dwarves

## Overview

Dwarves are the people who endured [[The Cataclysm]] by going below. When the surface burned and the sky failed, the wise withdrew into the deep places, and what survived there became the dwarves. Their culture is shaped by stone, shelter, endurance, and the long memory of what it took to survive when the world above did not.

## Origin

Dwarves emerged from those who survived [[The Cataclysm]] beneath the earth. They are not made-by-the-impact in the way some peoples are; they are the ones who chose underground when the surface was dying.

## Cataclysm memory

Dwarves generally believe [[The Cataclysm]] was the age when the surface died and the wise went below. Their cultural memory centers less on celestial sacrifice and more on endurance, shelter, stone, and preservation through discipline.

> Dwarves: the world died above, and we endured below.

Even where they acknowledge the world above was later healed, their deepest identity comes from the long survival beneath it. Dwarven belief emphasizes the earth as protector, endurance over wonder, craft as survival, and memory guarded through generations of darkness.

To dwarves, the defining truth is not that the world was saved, but that they survived when it was not.

## Culture

Endurance over wonder. Craft as survival. Memory as institution. Dwarven tradition holds that the earth itself is protector and witness — that what is preserved underground is preserved properly, and that anything entrusted to the surface is provisional. Dwarven craft, mining, smithing, and stoneworking are not merely practical skills but sacred continuities.

Dwarven politics tends toward conservatism in the proper sense: long-form preservation, slow decision-making, distrust of rapid change.

## Distribution

Across the world, in deep places — mountain holds, cavern systems, subterranean cities. The [[Crystal Caverns]] beneath the [[Hollow Crown]] are a major dwarven-relevant region, though dwarven presence there is independent of [[Drakenvale]]'s sanctuary politics.

## Magical character

Dwarven magical tradition tends toward Runecraft, Warding, and Binding — the inscribed, the durable, the structural. Dwarves work within the [[Nine Magical Fields]] but bring distinctive cultural emphasis to the fields concerned with permanence.

## Relationships with other peoples

- [[Humans]] — often cooperative, particularly in trade and craft
- [[Gnomes]] — mixed; both peoples have deep-place affinities but very different temperaments
- [[Orcs]] — often tense; competing for harsh terrain
- [[Elves]] — generally distant, occasionally cooperative
- [[Drakenvale]] — present but not central; dwarves participate in the sanctuary's economy through the [[Crystal Caverns]] and trade networks
