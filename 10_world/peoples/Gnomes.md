# Gnomes

## Overview

Gnomes arose in the subtle magical and crystalline lands left behind by [[The Cataclysm]] — the regions where the impact did not destroy or merely restore, but transformed. The strange residues, crystal harmonics, altered matter, and hidden magical structures that emerged from the fragments shaped the gnomes into a people defined by curiosity, pattern-recognition, and the conviction that the world's hidden laws are worth understanding.

## Origin

Gnomes emerged from the lands transformed by [[The Cataclysm]] — places where the impact left subtle magical residue, crystalline structure, and altered matter rather than ordinary destruction or restoration. Their origin is bound to those transformed regions, and their cultural orientation reflects it.

## Cataclysm memory

Gnomes generally believe the [[Cataclysm]] did not just break the world; it changed the rules of reality. Their traditions are more likely than others to focus on the strange residues left behind: crystal harmonics, altered matter, subtle magical structures, and the hidden logic that emerged from the falling star. They tend to interpret the world as something transformed, not merely damaged.

> Gnomes: the world changed, and its hidden laws changed with it.

Gnomish belief emphasizes curiosity, hidden patterns, magical residue, the complexity of change, and the idea that catastrophe revealed as much as it destroyed.

## Culture

Defined by inquiry and pattern. Gnomish culture values intellectual rigor, technical craft, magical theory, and the willingness to investigate phenomena other peoples consider settled or dismiss. Gnome politics tends toward deliberation and consultation; gnome social life tends toward small communities of shared interest.

Gnomish workshops, observatories, and small-scale academies are characteristic — gnomes pursue understanding at human-or-smaller scale, with patience.

## Distribution

In the transformed regions across the world. The [[Crystal Caverns]] beneath the [[Hollow Crown]] have gnomish-relevant features (mineral resonance, psionic amplification, luminescent pools), though gnome presence there is independent of [[Drakenvale]]'s sanctuary politics. Gnomes are present in [[Drakenvale]] proper as scholars, artisans, and engineers — particularly within institutions like the [[Hall of Scales]] and the [[Arcane Conservatory]].

## Magical character

Gnomes work across the [[Nine Magical Fields]] but with characteristic emphasis on Alchemy, Illusion, and Runecraft — the fields concerned with transformation, perception, and inscribed pattern. Gnomish magical tradition is rigorous, theoretical, and willing to pursue dangerous knowledge for the sake of understanding. The [[Arcane Conservatory]] has historically had strong gnomish presence.

## Relationships with other peoples

- [[Humans]] — generally cooperative; strong tradition of joint scholarship
- [[Halflings]] — frequently allied or neighbored; complementary temperaments
- [[Dwarves]] — mixed; both deep-place peoples but very different temperaments
- [[Elves]] — generally cooperative; mutual respect for ancient knowledge traditions
- [[Drakari]] and [[Drakenvale]] — well-integrated into the sanctuary's intellectual institutions
- [[Vulkar]] — uneasy; the question of "transformation versus corruption" is theologically charged between them
