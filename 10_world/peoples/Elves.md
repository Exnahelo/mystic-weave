# Elves

## Overview

The elves are the once-celestial people who became mortal in [[The Cataclysm]]. Carried in the wake of the celestial body that broke against the world, they were not yet wholly of the world when it struck. They looked upon the dying land and gathered their power together, spending their immortal nature to steady life, heal the wound where they could, and keep the world from falling fully into ruin. That act cost them their immortality. They became mortal — long-lived, but no longer pure beings of starlight — and from then on they were the elves: a people of memory, grief, beauty, and stewardship.

## Origin

Star-borne. The elves came in the wake of the meteor that caused [[The Cataclysm]], originally not of the world. Their decision to spend their celestial nature to heal what they could is the founding act of their mortal existence. Where their power pooled most deeply, life returned first — and those places became the cradle-lands.

The [[Feywood]] is one of the cradle-lands.

## Cataclysm memory

Elves generally believe [[The Cataclysm]] was not merely a disaster, but the moment of their fall into mortality. In their deepest sacred traditions, they remember themselves as beings carried in the celestial wake who chose to spend their immortal nature to keep the dying world alive.

For them, the world is not simply a homeland; it is a responsibility purchased at terrible cost.

> Elves: the world was dying, and we gave ourselves to preserve it.

Different elven factions may disagree about details, but most would agree that their people are bound to the land by an ancient act of relinquishment.

Some elves believe sacrifice made stewardship sacred. Others may one day believe sacrifice was exploited, and that relics like the [[Heartstone]] should belong to elven inheritance.

## Culture

Defined by sacrifice, stewardship, memory, sacred obligation, and the idea that life in the world persists because someone gave something irretrievable to preserve it. Elven art, ritual, and politics carry the weight of that founding act — beauty as memorial, restraint as inheritance, long timescales as proper measure.

## Distribution

The [[Feywood]] is the elven realm most directly relevant to [[Drakenvale]]. Other cradle-lands and elven realms exist elsewhere in the world.

## Magical character

The [[Heartwood]] in the [[Feywood]] is held in elven tradition as the living remainder of the elves' original celestial nature. Other cradle-lands hold similar concentrations. Elven magical practice tends toward Druidry and Sacred fields, though elves work across the [[Nine Magical Fields]].

## Relationships with other peoples

- [[Drakari]] and [[Drakenvale]] — sovereign-equal allies under the [[Oath of the Fallen]]; mutual respect with quiet undercurrents about the [[Heartstone]]'s contested origin
- [[Humans]] — long-running shared history, generally cooperative, sometimes uneasy
- Other peoples — varies by region

The elves did not fight in the [[War of the Fallen]]. They watched from outside it and chose not to engage. The decision is preserved in elven tradition as an ethical choice, not a failure to act — the elves had given enough already.

## Open questions
