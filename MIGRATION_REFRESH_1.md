## Decisions locked this session

1. **The Two *are* Solveris and Mordrax.** Not a separate, deeper pair
   behind the deities — the same polarity in two registers: "the Two" for
   the cosmological/creation face, "Solveris and Mordrax" for the worshipped
   face. The prior `The Two.md` claim that they "are not deities in the
   sense that Solveris and Mordrax are" and "precede the categories within
   which deity-figures function" was **false under this decision and has
   been cut.**
2. **The world arose *from* the collision.** The creation-collision is the
   act of creation, not an event that preceded a separately-existing world.
   This resolves the prior discrepancy between `The Two.md` ("collision
   complete before the world existed") and `mw_mech_cosmic_foundation.md`
   ("from their collision the world was") in favor of the latter.
3. **Creation-collision and the Cataclysm remain distinct.** The collision
   made the world; the Cataclysm (far later, extra-solar) brought/woke
   magic. Not conflated.
4. **Magic origin — timing settled, source contested by design.** Magic
   arrived with the Cataclysm (settled). *Where it came from* is a permanent
   multi-tradition dispute, now owned by a dedicated `Origin of Magic` note
   rather than embedded ad hoc. Positions seeded: carried-in, awakened,
   and a draconic-theistic strand (the deities sent it). The "magic comes
   from the Two as a cosmological source" framing is **dropped** — the Two
   appear only as the deities the theistic strand credits.
5. **`Origin of Magic` as its own note.** Chosen over repointing to
   `[[The Cataclysm]]` because origin-of-magic is expected to grow into a
   catalog of competing traditions as more peoples/civilizations enter
   canon. The event note owns the *event*; the new note owns the *dispute*.
