## Issues surfaced during this batch

1. **Ardrynn placement.** I put her in `dragons/` rather than `npcs/` because her primary type is dragon (she is one) and the dragon template fits her better. The NPC vs dragon split for active dragon characters is something to be deliberate about. My current rule: dragons go in `dragons/` regardless of whether they're Council or not. If you want dragon-NPCs in `npcs/` instead, tell me.

2. **The Unchanged in `deities/` rather than `cosmology/`.** Despite its cosmological character, folk practice and the source material both treat it functionally as a god-figure. I followed that. Tell me if you'd rather keep cosmology and deity types separate by content rather than by function.

3. **Kobolds.** Mentioned in `Drakenvale.md` as one of the integrated peoples but I didn't make a note for them in this batch. Should be in batch 4 — they belong with the eight ancestries even though source material treats them more as a sub-grouping.

4. **The "Heartwood" tree.** Distinct from "Heartstone" (artifact in Drakenvale) and from "Heartwood Estate" (location in Feywood). Referenced in `Feywood.md` and `Lethariel Heartwood.md` but does not have its own note yet. Should be batch 4.

5. **Eight ancestries — formal wikilink consistency.** I've used `[[Drakari]]`, `[[Elves]]`, etc. Some references in earlier batches use lowercase or non-linked variants. The notes I'm creating in this batch and forward use the linked title-case form. If you want a consistency pass on earlier notes, that's a separate cleanup task.
